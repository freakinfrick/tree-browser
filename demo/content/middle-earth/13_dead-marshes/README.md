**Kind:** a fen that is also a grave.
**Position:** between the Emyn Muil and the Dagorlad, north-west of the Black
Gate.
**Who lives here:** the dead of the Last Alliance, under the water.

## What it is

The Dead Marshes are the waterlogged ground where the great battle of the
**Dagorlad** (Second Age 3434) was fought, when the Last Alliance of Elves
and Men marched on Mordor. The fallen sank where they lay, and the marsh
preserved them: faces under the surface, and lights — the "candles of
corpses" — burning above them at night.

## The crossing

In **TA 3019-03**, Frodo, Sam, and Gollum cross the marsh on the way to
Cirith Ungol. The safety notice is filed, in Sam's hand, in the margin of his
copy (`do-not-follow-the-lights.txt`):

1. Keep to the path the guide is using.
2. Do not look into the pools.
3. Do not follow the lights. They are candles of corpses.

Gollum — who has passed this way before, and stayed down here for five
hundred years — walks them straight across. The guide rating is filed:
5/5, "creepy but correct."

## Status

Undisturbed. The pools still burn, the faces still wait, and the only safe
way across remains the one the guide knows.
