## The battle

**Date:** Second Age 3434. **Site:** the Dagorlad, the Battle Plain, before
the Black Gate. **Sides:** the Last Alliance of Elves and Men (Gil-galad,
Elendil, Isildur, Anárion) against the host of Sauron.

The Alliance won the field. Sauron retreated into Barad-dûr, which was then
besieged for seven years. The field did not stay a field: over the long
Third Age the marshes crept east across it and took the graves with them.

## What the survey found (TA 3019)

- The faces in the pools are **Elves, Men and Orcs** alike; the marsh does
  not sort by side.
- The lights appear at dusk. There is no record of anyone following one
  and filing a follow-up.
- Nothing can be recovered. The guide confirms: "You cannot reach them. Only
  lights to see, yes. Only lights."

## Retention

Indefinite. There is no process for closing a grave that is also a marsh.
The roll stays open; the rows in the water stay unread.
