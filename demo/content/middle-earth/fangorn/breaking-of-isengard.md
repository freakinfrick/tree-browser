# The breaking of Isengard

**Filed:** 3019-03-03, after the waters settled · **Severity:** resolved (the problem no longer exists)
**Status:** closed · **Reviewer:** the Entmoot, en banc

**Date:** 3019-03-03 · **Perpetrator:** the Ents, at the end of a long debate
**Target:** the Ring of Isengard · **Result:** flooded, felled, done.

> **Addendum (3019-03-04).** The Huorns have been counted. The count took

> still for it. See `huorn-migration.csv`.

## Why the Ents moved

Saruman's furnaces had been eating Fangorn for weeks (`../isengard/orthanc/uruk-hai-production.csv`).
Merry and Pippin walked into the forest, met Treebeard, and the Entmoot
(`entmoot/`) that had been deliberating for three days suddenly found its
answer: the trees Saruman cut were their own.

## The march

- The Ents walked. Slowly. The whole time they moved, they were not hasty,
  and they were not stopping.
- The Huorns followed, filling the valley behind them (`huorn-migration.csv`).

> "A forest walked." — Treebeard, on the road to Isengard, 3019-03-03
> (see also `entmoot/minutes-day-2.txt`, where this was not yet a plan).

## The assault

- The Ents tore down the gates and the walls; no axe of Isengard could hurt
  them for long, because they were the thing the axes were made from.
- They broke the dam of the river Isen and let the water into the Ring.
  The river did not need persuading; it had been dammed for years and had
  opinions. Water into every vent, culvert, and shaft, and the Ring became
  a lake that still holds a grudge.
- The furnaces went out. The pits flooded. The orcs drowned or fled into a
  forest that had no intention of letting them out.

## Aftermath

- Isengard: `decommissioned` (see the k8s deployment, replicas set to 0).
- Saruman: trapped in Orthanc, in a tower that cannot be broken, with
  nothing left worth guarding.
- Treebeard, on the whole matter: "A thing is about to happen which has not
  happened since the Elder Days." — Treebeard, Fangorn edge, 3019-03-03.

> **Later (3019-03-04).** The Ring of Isengard is now the main wading pool
> of a very large forest. Treebeard has assigned two Huorns to stand guard
> on Orthanc and neither has yet found a reason to leave. See
> `../isengard/orthanc/prisoner-log.md`.

## Lesson

Do not industrialise a forest. The forest has a trade union, and its strike
action is the entire forest walking to your front door.
