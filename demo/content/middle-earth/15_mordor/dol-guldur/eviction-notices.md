# Dol Guldur: eviction notices served

**Filed by:** the Galadhrim, jointly with the Wood-elves of the north ·
**Ticket:** DOLGULDUR-EVICT-003 · **Status:** CLOSED (premises demolished) ·
**Cross-refs:** `necromancer-log.md`, `08_lothlorien/`, `15_mordor/barad-dur/`

## Notice 1: TA 2063

Served by: Gandalf the Grey, entering alone.
Result: occupant withdrew to the East without a hearing. **The Watchful
Peace** follows, about four hundred years of it. Occupant returns in 2460
with more staff.

## Notice 2: TA 2941

Served by: the White Council (Saruman, Gandalf, Elrond, Galadriel and
others), in force. Gandalf had been inside the premises in 2850 and found
Thráin, the last of the Seven Rings already taken from him.
Result: occupant vacated and moved to Mordor, as planned. His plan, it
emerged. Saruman had delayed the notice for years; the Council later
learned why.

## Notice 3: TA 3019

Served by: the forces of Lórien, under Celeborn, after three assaults on the
Golden Wood from Dol Guldur (3019-03-11, 03-15, 03-22) were repelled.

| date | action |
|---|---|
| 3019-03-25 | the Ring is unmade; the host of Dol Guldur loses heart |
| 3019-03-28 | Celeborn leads the Galadhrim across the Anduin |
| 3019-03-28 onward | Dol Guldur taken. Galadriel throws down its walls and lays bare its pits |
| 3019-04-06 | Celeborn and Thranduil meet in the middle of the forest |

## Disposition

The wood is renamed **Eryn Lasgalen**, the Wood of Greenleaves. North to the
Mountains goes to Thranduil; the south, below the Narrows, to Celeborn as
**East Lórien**. The hill is left bare, and the wood is allowed to come back
at its own pace.

No forwarding address was left by any occupant.
