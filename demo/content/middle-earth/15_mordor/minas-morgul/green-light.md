Once Minas Ithil, the Tower of the Moon. Captured, renamed, and generally
let go — it now faces the Black Land and answers to no-one pleasant.

- **the green light:** a pale glow that rises from the top when the city
  empties for war. It is not a beacon. It is the opposite of a beacon
  (`14_gondor/minas-tirith/beacon-schedule.toml` is the friendly version).
- **the road:** from here to Cirith Ungol the path climbs; the Stair is not
  a shortcut, it is a test (`../cirith-ungol/the-stair.md`).
- **the watchers:** two stone guardians at the gate. They do not blink, and
  they do not need to.

> Frodo and Sam passed beneath the city, not through it. The green light
> watched them go and said nothing, which was worse than saying something.

## The green light (3019-03-10)

The light rose for the first time in an age on the night the host mustered
for the march to Minas Tirith. It is not a signal *for* Mordor; it is a
sign *of* Mordor — the city telling the dark that the dark is now riding.
See `15_mordor/minas-morgul/witch-king-departure.log`.

- "THE CITY OF THE MOON IS OURS, AND THE MOON WILL NOT RISE AGAIN." — the
  Witch-king (reported), Minas Morgul, 3019-03-10 (the keeper did not check).

## The march (3019-03-13)

The host passed beneath the green light and down the Morgul-road toward the
Pelennor, where it would lay siege to Minas Tirith and where its lord would
not return (see `14_gondor/pelennor-fields/eowyn-vs-witchking.md`). The light
went out behind them and has not been lit since. The tower is dark again;
the host does not return.
