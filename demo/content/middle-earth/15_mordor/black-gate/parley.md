## The offer

The Mouth of Sauron — a Man, long corrupted, who had forgotten his own name
— rode out with a small guard and offered terms:

- the host of the West to disband,
- the lands west of the Anduin to be ceded to Mordor,
- Isengard to be restored to Sauron's ally Saruman.

## The proof of the Ring's capture

He produced Frodo's mithril coat, the elven cloak, and Sam's sword, taken at
Cirith Ungol (see `15_mordor/cirith-ungol/package-incident.md`). "A spy," he
said, "will be tormented until he speaks." — the Mouth of Sauron, before the
Morannon, 3019-03-25.

Then he recited the terms Management had prepared for him:

- "THE RING-BEARER IS OURS." — the Mouth of Sauron, before the Morannon, 3019-03-25.
- "HE HAS BEEN DELIVERED TO BARAD-DÛR, AND THE EYE HAS SEEN HIM." — the same.
- "THE HOST OF THE WEST MAY SURRENDER, OR IT MAY DIE HERE. THE EYE DOES NOT CARE WHICH." — the same.

All three recited exactly as given; the Mouth has forgotten his own name and
face, but he has never once forgotten a message.

## The rejection

Aragorn did not parley. He met the Mouth's eyes until the Mouth — who had
forgotten how to be looked at — flinched and looked away. There was no
counter-offer, because there was nothing to offer; the terms were refused
before they were finished. The record shows only that the West held its
ground, and that a King does not bargain with a door.

## What the parley actually was

A stalling tactic by the West — to hold the Eye's attention on the Gate so
that two hobbits could cross the plain unobserved. It worked. The Mouth of
Sauron demanded surrender; the West refused; the Black Gate opened; and
then, ten minutes later, the ground answered, and there was no more parley.
The Eye, which had been watching the door, looked elsewhere for the first
time in an age, and found it was already too late.

## Note

The Mouth of Sauron rode back through the gate and is not mentioned again.
Some endings are not recorded because the record-keeper also ended.

## Addendum (3019-03-25, post)

The proof the Mouth produced — the mithril coat — was real, but the
prisoner it came from was not. The coat was taken at Cirith Ungol from a
hobbit who was not wearing it at the time, and the "spy" was already gone.
The parley proved only that the Eye cannot tell a coat from its owner. See
`15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`.
