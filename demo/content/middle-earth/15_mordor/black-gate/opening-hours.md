- No hobbits. (Try the stair at Cirith Ungol. *Please don't.*)
- No admission except by parley. The parley is the only door that opens,
  and it opens only for the host of the West, one time (see
  `15_mordor/black-gate/parley.md`).
- Parley available with the Mouth of Sauron; results not guaranteed.

## The Mouth of Sauron (press office)

- Always available. He has no other commitments, having forgotten his name
  and his face and everything but the message.
- Standard terms: disband, cede the west, restore Isengard. See `parley.md`
  for the last parley, which went poorly for everyone present.
- Quote of record: "THE RING-BEARER IS OURS." — the Mouth of Sauron, before
  the Morannon, 3019-03-25 (the parley). Later the record-keeper could not
  confirm it, because the record-keeper also ended.

## House rules

- No hobbits. No ring-bearers. No armies of the West (except the one time).
- The gate is guarded by more than orcs. The orc count is in
  `../barad-dur/orc-hr/shift-roster.csv`.
- If the ground begins to shake, the gate is no longer your problem. The
  gate is no longer anyone's problem.

## Addendum (3019-03-25)

The gate opened for the host of the West as a diversion. The Eye watched
the door while two hobbits crossed the plain unobserved. Ten minutes after
the parley, the door stopped being a door. See
`15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`.
