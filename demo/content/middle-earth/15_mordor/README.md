**Kind:** Sauron's realm, walled by mountains and guarded at every gate.
**Position:** east of Gondor, ringed by the Ash Mountains and the Mountains of
Shadow; entered by the Black Gate and the pass of Cirith Ungol.
**Who lives here:** orcs, the Nazgûl, the Eye — and, at the end, two hobbits.

## What it is

Mordor is the one place the Ring must go, and the one place it must be
unmade. Its heart is **Barad-dûr**, the Dark Tower, from which the Eye
watches without sleep (`barad-dur/eye-uptime.prom`, `barad-dur/eye-dashboard.json`);
its furnace is **Mount Doom**, Orodruin, the volcano where the Ring was
forged in **Second Age 1600** and where alone it can be destroyed
(`mount-doom/`, `the-ring/forging.md`).

## The gates and the road in

- **The Black Gate (Morannon).** The Teeth of Mordor, and the parley that was
  a diversion (`black-gate/parley.md`, `black-gate/opening-hours.md`).
- **Minas Morgul.** The Tower of the Moon, once Minas Ithil of Gondor, now the
  Witch-king's seat; the green light over the Morgul Vale
  (`minas-morgul/green-light.md`, `minas-morgul/witch-king-departure.log`).
- **Cirith Ungol.** The Spider's Pass: the Stairs, **Shelob**'s lair, and the
  tower where Frodo was taken (`cirith-ungol/the-stair.md`,
  `cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`, `cirith-ungol/package-incident.md`).
- **Dol Guldur.** The Hill of Sorcery in southern Mirkwood — Sauron's fortress
  as the Necromancer, thrown down at the end by Galadriel and Celeborn
  (`dol-guldur/necromancer-log.md`).

## The end

- **TA 3019-03-18.** The host of the West challenges the Black Gate — a
  diversion with everything on it (`black-gate/`).
- **TA 3019-03-25.** In the Cracks of Doom, Gollum seizes the Ring and falls;
  the Ring is unmade, Barad-dûr fails (`barad-dur/structural-failure.log`),
  the Nazgûl perish (`barad-dur/nazgul-roster.csv`), and the Eye closes —
  uptime reset to zero, permanently (`mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`).

## Status

Unmade. The Dark Lord is gone, the Tower has fallen, and the volcano is the
only thing left running — and it was always the plan.
