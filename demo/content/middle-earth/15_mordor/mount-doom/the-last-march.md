# The last march: Samwise carries Frodo

**Route:** the ash of Gorgoroth → the foot of Mount Doom
**Date:** 3019-03-24/25
**Status:** both arrived. This is the line the whole story leans on.
**Filed by:** SAMWISE GAMGEE (the carried party did not sign) · **Ticket:** `march-0001`
**Cross-refs:** `15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md` · `15_mordor/mount-doom/eruption-forecast.csv` ·
`15_mordor/mount-doom/.eye-is-watching.log` (which logged the two small figures and called them "too small to matter")

## Context

- Frodo, spent, could not walk. The Ring had grown too heavy; the land was
  furnace; the water was gone.
- Sam, who had not stopped carrying things since the Shire, said the line
  that is, functionally, the thesis of the entire repo:

> "I can't carry it for you, but I can carry you." — Samwise Gamgee, on the
  slopes of Mount Doom, 3019-03-25.

## The final steps

- 3019-03-24: the guide (Gollum) defects at the Cracks. See gollum/.
- 3019-03-25 morning: Sam lifts Frodo and climbs. The Ring offers Frodo
  nothing by this point; the Ring has never offered Sam anything, which is
  why Sam is the one still walking.
- The Cracks of Doom, the chamber where the Ring was forged in SA 1600
  (`the-ring/forging.md`), is reached at 10:30. The rest is in
  `15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`.

## What Sam carried, total

- two backpacks, the cooking gear, the rope, the phial, the sword, the
  water (until it ran out), and, at the last, Frodo.

## What he would not carry

- the Ring. Not out of fear, but because the Ring only lets one bearer at a
  time, and Sam knew the bearer was Frodo.

## Arrival

They reached the mountain, climbed it, and the rest is in
`ring-destruction.md`. The last march is why any of it happened: the quest
was never carried by a hero. It was carried by a gardener.
