WALKING INTO MORDOR: FAQ

Filed: 3018-10-25 · Ticket: `gate-entry-0001` · Severity: INFRASTRUCTURE · Status: OPEN, FOREVER
See also: `15_mordor/black-gate/opening-hours.md` (the gate does not open for you).

Q: Can one simply walk into Mordor?
A: No. Its black gates are guarded by more than orcs.

> "One does not simply walk into Mordor." — Boromir, Captain of Gondor,
  Council of Elrond, Rivendell, 3018-10-25 (source: 05_rivendell/council-minutes/).
  He also called it "the very air you breathe is a poisonous fume" — a
  ventilation complaint filed to no one (disputed: the Eye denies all fumes).

Q: But could one... not simply walk into Mordor?
A: Two hobbits and a guide are trying it now. Updates to follow.

Q: Is there a side entrance?
A: There is a stair. You do not want the stair.

> "There is a passage, a dark way... through the mountains, but it is a
  frightful way." — Boromir, Council of Elrond, Rivendell, 3018-10-25.
  Nobody at the Council could remember the password for the west gate; see
  07_moria/west-gate/ ("mellon").

Q: What if I am simply walked into Mordor by a guide?
A: Still not simply. The guide is Gollum, and Gollum is a whole itinerary.

Q: Is there a toll?
A: Yes. The toll is the Ring, and you must not pay it.

Q: What time does the gate open?
A: See `15_mordor/black-gate/opening-hours.md`. It does not.

Later: 3019-03-25 — the gate opened exactly once, and that was the ground
opening it. The two hobbits and the guide never used the gate; they used the
stair, which is why you do not want the stair. See
`15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`.
