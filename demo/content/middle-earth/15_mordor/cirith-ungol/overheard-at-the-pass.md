- **Shagrat**, captain of the Tower (east-facing, understaffed)

- one prisoner (unconscious, wrapped in cords) carried between them

## Summary of the discussion

1. **The prisoner is not dead.** Shelob's venom paralyses; it does not
   kill. She prefers her meat fresh. (Witness was *not* told this before
   the incident, and would like it noted.)
2. **Orders from Lugbúrz** (Barad-dûr): any trespasser to be stripped,
   searched and held; every item sent up. Nothing to be kept.
3. **The missing party.** Both captains agree there was a second one: a
   "large warrior" who cut Shelob, "maybe an Elf". The large warrior is
   three feet six and listening from a crack in the rock.
4. **Morale.** Both captains would like a quiet post somewhere with no big
   bosses. Gorbag proposes going into business for themselves "with a few
   trusty lads". Shagrat does not trust him, correctly.
5. **Shelob.** Referred to as "Her Ladyship"; kept on because she is good
   at the job and the Tower does not have to feed her.
