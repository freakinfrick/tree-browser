# Cirith Ungol: the package incident

**Filed by:** Shagrat (survivor, temporarily) · **TA 3019-03-13**
**Ticket:** CU-PACKAGE-3019-001 · **Severity:** CRITICAL (a package went
missing; then everyone else did)
**Cross-refs:** `15_mordor/cirith-ungol/tower-guard-roster.csv`,
`15_mordor/cirith-ungol/the-watchers.md`, `15_mordor/cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`,
`15_mordor/black-gate/parley.md`

**Date:** 3019-03-13, after the Stair
**Parties:** Shagrat (Tower of Cirith Ungol) vs Gorbag (Minas Morgul)
**Subject:** one prisoner, one mithril shirt, one small sword, one argument.

## What happened

- Shelob stung Frodo; the orcs carried him, wrapped, to the tower (see
  `15_mordor/cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`).
- Sam, following, overheard two orc-captains quarrel over the loot and the
  prisoner. The quarrel became a fight; the fight became a massacre; the
  garrison of the tower killed each other down to almost no one.
- The fight was over who got to deliver the package to Barad-dûr, and who
  got to keep the mithril shirt. Neither side accepted the obvious answer:
  the shirt did not belong to either of them, and neither did the prisoner.

## The loot in dispute

- a mithril shirt (see `07_moria/mazarbul-chamber/`, and later
  `15_mordor/black-gate/parley.md` where it is produced as "proof").
- a small elf-blade (Sam's).
- one prisoner, "the great elf-warrior", who turned out to be neither great
  nor an elf nor a warrior.

## The two accounts (each party disputed the other)

- "THE MORGUL LOT STARTED IT." — Shagrat, Tower of Cirith Ungol, 3019-03-13.
- "SHAGRAT WAS GOING TO CHEAT US AND KEEP THE SHIRT." — Gorbag (reported),
  Minas Morgul, 3019-03-13 (disputed; Gorbag did not survive to confirm).

## Result

- Sam walked into a tower whose guards had solved themselves, and walked out
  with Frodo. See `15_mordor/cirith-ungol/tower-guard-roster.csv` for the final
  shift record, which lists forty guards and no survivors.

## Lesson

The enemy's greatest organisational weakness is the enemy's organisation.
