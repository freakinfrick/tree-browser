# Tom Bombadil: personnel record

**Record ID:** TOM-0 (he declined the number)
**Name:** Tom Bombadil (also "Eldest", "the Master"; he does not check a box)
**Address:** the Old Forest, near the Withywindle
**Spouse:** Goldberry, the River-daughter
**Role:** none. This is the important part.

## Observations

- He is older than the trees, older than the hills, older than the first
  raindrop and the first acorn. He remembers the first dark, before the
  Dark Lord came from Outside.
- He sings everything. Even the part where he saves your life.
- The Ring has no power over him. He picks it up, peers through it, makes it
  vanish for a joke, hands it back, and is not changed in the least.

> "Eldest, that's what I am. ... Tom remembers the first raindrop and the
> first acorn."
> — Tom Bombadil, to the hobbits, his house on the Withywindle, 3018-09-30

## The Ring test

At the Council, the obvious question was raised: give the Ring to Tom
Bombadil. He would be the safest place in the world.

The answer: he would *forget* it, the way you forget a button. And then
Sauron would get it eventually, because Tom does not think of the Ring as
important, and he would not guard it, and he would not even notice when he
lost it.

## Status

Still there. Still singing. Not involved. The only entity in this repo with
perfect uptime and zero incidents, because he files nothing and fears
nothing.
