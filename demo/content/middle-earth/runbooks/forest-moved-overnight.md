# Runbook: a forest has moved overnight

**Alert:** trees where there were no trees; a wood in the valley at dawn
**Severity:** depends entirely on which side you fought on last night
**Owner:** none of yours · **Escalation:** the Ents of Fangorn, slowly, and
only if you ask politely (`10_fangorn/`)
**Cross-refs:** `10_fangorn/huorn-migration.csv`,
`10_fangorn/breaking-of-isengard.md`, `11_rohan/helms-deep/siege-postmortem.md`

## Triage

1. Check which side you are on. If you are an orc from the siege of the
   Hornburg: this runbook will not help you. Nothing will.
2. If you are a Rider of Rohan: do not enter the wood. Ride around it.
   Gandalf says it is safe to ride *past*.
3. Do not cut, burn, or chop anything. The last people who did are the
   reason the forest is here.

## Response

- Stay out of the trees. The Huorns are not hostile to you; they are also
  not interested in you.
- Do not file a ticket about the forest moving without one. The forest does
  not use tickets. It will be gone again when it has finished.
- If you need something from the Ents, allow days. Hasty requests are
  answered last, and that is fair.

## Postmortem

**Last run:** TA 3019-03-03/04, Helm's Deep. The Huorns came down from
Fangorn in the night and filled the Deeping-coomb. At dawn the orcs fled
into the wood. None came out. The trees went home the following night and
left a mound, the Death Down, which nobody has since been asked to explain.

Records of the forest's own processes are kept in the forest
(`10_fangorn/treebeard/`), in its own time, which is the correct time.
