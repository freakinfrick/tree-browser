# Runbook: Balrog encountered

**Alert:** drums in the deep; a shadow with fire in it; the orcs go quiet
**Severity:** beyond SEV-1 (a Maia of the ancient world; not a load-test)
**Owner:** the most senior wizard present · **Escalation:** there is none.
The owner *is* the escalation.
**Cross-refs:** `07_moria/balrog-incident-report.md`,
`07_moria/durins-bridge-inspection.md`

## Triage

1. Stop digging. (This alert has one known root cause: digging too deep for
   mithril. See Durin's Bane, TA 1980.)
2. Run. Toward the nearest narrow bridge.
3. Do not fight it in the open. Do not fight it at all, if you are not the
   owner.

## Response

- Owner holds the bridge alone. Everyone else crosses and keeps going.
- Owner declares, in a loud voice, the name of the fire he serves and the
  one the Balrog serves. Recommended wording: short, and final.
- Owner breaks the bridge. **Check for whips before leaving the edge.**

## Recovery

- The party must not wait at the far side. Grief comes later; the orcs come
  now. Leader of record passes to the next most senior (Aragorn).
- Owner is not recoverable by the party. Owner may be returned by other
  means, after a fight on a mountain peak and some weeks' absence
  (`10_fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log`). This is not a plan. It is a precedent.

## Postmortem

**Last run:** TA 3019-01-15, the Bridge of Khazad-dûm. Outcome: bridge
broken, Balrog fell, owner fell with it (whip). Pursuit lasted ten days, from
the Endless Stair to the peak of Zirakzigil. Balrog destroyed. Owner
returned, upgraded to white.

Action item carried forward: "check for whips" moved to step one of the next
revision.
