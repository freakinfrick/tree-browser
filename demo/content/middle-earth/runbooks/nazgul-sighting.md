# Runbook: Nazgûl sighting

**Alert:** `NazgulActive` drop (see `mordor/barad-dur/alerts.yml`)
**Severity:** high, then suddenly over.
**Owner:** the Bree watch (on behalf of the Rangers) · **Escalation:**
Aragorn (Strider) → Gandalf → Elrond (`rivendell/`). If Aragorn is also
missing, you are having a very bad week; see the last run below.

**Last run:** TA 3018-10-06, Weathertop (`bree/weathertop/incident-report.md`).
Outcome: five Nazgûl driven off by fire and a name (Elbereth); one Morgul
wound logged, routed to Rivendell. Runbook held up under load, with
reservations.

## Triage

1. They do not see you in daylight; they smell the ring.
2. Do not be between them and the Ring.
3. Fire and running water slow them. Neither stops them; both buy minutes.

## Response

- If on foot: get to a ford, a ferry, or a very stubborn farmer with dogs
  (`the-shire/black-riders.md`).
- If mounted: ride for the nearest Elven watercourse
  (`rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`).
- If you are a hobbit on a hill at night, and five of them arrive: put out
  the fire. Then run. (`bree/weathertop/incident-report.md`.)

## Escalation

If one dismounts and draws a Morgul-blade, the wound is not a wound; it is a
countdown. Route the victim to Elrond (`rivendell/`) at speed.

**Contacts:** on foot, flag a Ranger; the Prancing Pony holds a message-drop
(`bree/prancing-pony/`) and Butterbur will forget it, so tell him twice. If
the ford is close, Glorfindel is already on his way — do not wait for the
flood to be "scheduled" (`rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`).
