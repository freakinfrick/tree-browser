# Runbook: company member missing

**Alert:** headcount below the number that left Rivendell
**Severity:** SEV-2 (SEV-1 if the missing member is carrying anything)
**Owner:** the acting leader of the Company · **Escalation:** whoever is
still standing
**Cross-refs:** `09_anduin/last-known-locations.json`,
`09_anduin/amon-hen/`, `09_anduin/rauros-falls.md`

## Triage

1. Count again. Hobbits are small and sit behind things.
2. Find the Ring-bearer first. Everything else is second.
3. Check the boats (`09_anduin/boats.csv`). A boat gone is a direction.

## Response

- Split the search by what you know. One tracker follows the carriers of
  the missing; one party follows the boat.
- Leave marks: a brooch dropped in the grass is worth a search party.
- Travel light. Three hunters on foot can outrun an orc-band over four days
  if they do not stop to argue.
