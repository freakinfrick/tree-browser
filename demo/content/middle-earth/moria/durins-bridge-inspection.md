# Durin's Bridge: inspection report

**Ref:** MOR-BRD-3019-0115 · **Inspected by:** Gimli, son of Glóin (last
dwarf to look it over and the first to wish he hadn't) · **Date:** 3019-01-15
**Status:** FAILED — condemned in the course of the inspection
**Cross-ref:** `moria/balrog-incident-report.md`

## Span

- **Structure:** one slender arch, no rail, fifty feet, over a very long drop
- **Load rating:** one company at a time, single file
- **History:** the dwarves' own bridge, "to be defended by few" — narrow by
  design so a single defender could hold it against many.

## Findings

- no handrail (by design; "to be defended by few")
- hairline cracking noted on approach (pre-existing; the mine's condition)
- full structural failure at mid-span after the **Balrog incident**
  (`balrog-incident-report.md`): the bridge is now *two bridges*
- both halves remain attached to their abutments; the middle is missing.

## Result

The bridge held exactly long enough for the Fellowship to cross, and failed
exactly when asked to hold one wizard and one Balrog at once. As a
load-shedding device it performed admirably. As a bridge it is now a
staircase that ends in a very long way down.

## Recommendation

do not cross. do not shout "you shall not pass" near it (see the incident
report; the phrase has since been observed to produce a measurable echo
from the pit, which the company has declined to instrument further).

## Later

3019-01-16 — the eastern span was observed to shed a chunk of parapet on
its own. No one was on it. The abyss was unimpressed.
