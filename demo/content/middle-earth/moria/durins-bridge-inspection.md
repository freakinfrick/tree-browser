# Durin's Bridge: inspection report

- **Span:** one slender arch, no rail, fifty feet, over a very long drop
- **Load rating:** one company at a time, single file
- **Findings:**
  - no handrail (by design; "to be defended by few")
  - structural cracks after the **Balrog incident**: the bridge is now *two bridges*
- **Recommendation:** do not cross. Do not shout "you shall not pass" near it.
