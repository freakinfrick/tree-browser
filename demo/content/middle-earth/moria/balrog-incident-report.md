# Incident: Durin's Bane (the Balrog)

**Ticket:** MOR-3019-0115 · **Filed by:** Legolas Greenleaf, watcher on
point · **Date:** 3019-01-15 · **Site:** the Bridge of Khazad-dûm, Moria
**Severity:** SEV-0 · **Status:** closed (both parties off the bridge)

## Summary

While evacuating the Fellowship through Moria, a resident of the deep —
first logged in the dwarves' records as *Durin's Bane* — came up to see
what all the drums were about. It was met by the wizard Gandalf, who made
a stand on the bridge and issued the standard denial-of-service.

## The asset in question

Durin's Bane, a Balrog of Morgoth — a survivor of the First Age, of the
same order the dwarves roused when they "delved too greedily and too deep"
in TA 1980. No other specimen has been sighted in the mine since. The
dwarves did not so much record it as leave.

## Sequence

1. drums in the deep (`moria/mazarbul-chamber/drums-in-the-deep.wav`).
2. the company reaches the bridge. It is narrow, rail-less, and single-file.
3. the Balrog appears: "a thing of shadow and flame." — Legolas,
   Bridge of Khazad-dûm, 3019-01-15. Gimli, whose cousin Balin died in
   this mine (`moria/mazarbul-chamber/book-of-records.txt`), drops his axe.
4. Gandalf: **"You shall not pass."** — Gandalf, Bridge of Khazad-dûm,
   3019-01-15 (book form: "you cannot pass"). Bridge rejects the connection.
5. the Balrog steps on. The bridge, already weakened (`durins-bridge-inspection.md`),
   drops both endpoints into the abyss.

## Impact

- bridge: now two bridges (structural, see inspection report).
- Gandalf: fell with the adversary. Status: DOWN, then unexpectedly
  back (see `fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log`).
- company morale: also fell; recovered at the gate.

## Last transmission

**"Fly, you fools!"** — Gandalf, Bridge of Khazad-dûm, 3019-01-15,
immediately preceding the fall. Regarded by the company as his final
instruction. It was not final; see the reboot log.

## Note

He fought the thing from the deepest foundations to the highest peak, and
then he came back different. The full fight has no eyewitnesses and no logs;
the mountain was not instrumented.

### Addendum (recovered)

The "flame of Udûn" referenced in Gandalf's stand ("I am a servant of the
Secret Fire, wielder of the flame of Anor. The dark fire will not avail you,
flame of Udûn." — Gandalf to the Balrog, 3019-01-15) confirms the asset's
lineage: Udûn is the old name of the pit at the root of Angband, Morgoth's
fortress. The Balrog is not a cave-troll that learned fire tricks. (disputed
in the minstrels' retellings; the company's own account is unambiguous.)
