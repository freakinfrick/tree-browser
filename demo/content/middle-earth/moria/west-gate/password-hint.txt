DOORS OF DURIN — access log
filed: 3019-01-15, west gate of Moria, by the Fellowship (scribe: unwitnessed)
cross-ref: moria/west-gate/watcher-incident.md · rivendell/istari/dossier.md

inscription (drawn by Narvi the dwarf, written by Celebrimbor of Eregion,
Second Age; the ithildin lettering shows only under starlight and moonlight):
  Ennyn Durin Aran Moria. Pedo mellon a minno.
  (the Doors of Durin, Lord of Moria. Speak, friend, and enter.)

password, as discovered: "mellon" — Sindarin, "friend". not a riddle of
knowledge; a riddle of manners. the door asks you to say the word, and the
answer is the word itself.

attempted passwords (in order, with guesser):
  - "open"                     (Gandalf, reading the runes aloud — no)
  - "open sesame"              (Merry — "wrong story," said Gandalf)
  - "Durin"                    (Aragorn, the obvious lord — no)
  - elvish for "open", 14 dialects (Gandalf, working the spelling — no)
  - sitting on a rock and thinking hard (the whole fellowship — no)
  - "what's the elvish word for friend?" (Frodo, asking Gandalf — this was
    the answer, not the attempt)
  - "mellon"                   (Gandalf, to the door — YES)

later: Gandalf, after the door opened — "speak, friend, and enter" was never
a password at all. It was the welcome mat. The door trusted that a friend
would say the word, and an enemy would never think to.

note: the watcher in the water does not care about the password.
(see moria/west-gate/watcher-incident.md — it took Óin, and it waited for us.)
