# The Black Arrow

**Owner:** the line of Girion, Lord of Dale → Bard the Bowman
**Status:** spent (not recovered; the family say it went where it was meant).

## History

- forged for the Lords of Dale, in the days when Dale was rich and the
  Mountain was theirs.
- every one of Bard's ancestors who used a Black Arrow got it back, because
  they aimed true. Bard inherited the last one, and the skill.

## The shot

Smaug's armour was a coat of gold and gems — save for one bare patch over
the heart, where a thrush had learned the secret and told Bard. Bard fired
his last arrow into the patch from the tower of Laketown.

## Result

Smaug fell, and the age of dragons with him (`smaug-death.md`). The arrow
was not recovered from the wreck; Bard's family say it went where it was
meant, and that is where it stayed.

## Lesson

One arrow, one thrush, one bare patch. The dragon was not killed by an army.
It was killed by a sentence spoken in a bird's voice.
