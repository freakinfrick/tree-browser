# Incident: the three trolls

**Site:** a clearing east of the Shire, on the road to Rivendell
**Date:** Third Age 2941
**Severity:** SEV-1 (thirteen dwarves and one burglar, all in sacks)

## What happened

- The company found three trolls (Tom, Bert, William) arguing over how to
  cook thirteen dwarves and a hobbit.
- Bilbo, sent to pick a pocket, picked badly. The trolls argued all night
  about the recipe; dawn arrived; and trolls, as is well known, turn to
  stone in the sun.

## Aftermath

- the trolls: stone, permanently. The clearing is a tourist stop now.
- the cave: plundered. Elven swords recovered (Orcrist, Glamdring) and a
  small knife for Bilbo, which he later named Sting.

## Lesson

If you are a burglar, burgle. If you are a hobbit, stall for sunrise. Bilbo
did the second thing, and it worked better than the first.
