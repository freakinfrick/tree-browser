# The Battle of Five Armies: after-action report

**Site:** beneath the Lonely Mountain, before the gate
**Date:** Third Age 2941
**Result:** a very expensive peace.

## The five armies

1. the dwarves of the Iron Hills (Dáin's host)
2. the Elves of Mirkwood (Thranduil)
3. the Men of Laketown (Bard)
4. the goblins of the Misty Mountains (Bolgo's host)
5. the wargs (the goblins' ride, counted as their own army by everyone who
   had to fight them)

## The twist

The first three armies were about to fight each other over the treasure
when the last two arrived, and everyone discovered they were all on the
same side after all.

## Casualties

- Thorin Oakenshield: died of his wounds; reconciled with Bilbo first.
- Fíli and Kíli: died defending Thorin.
- Bolgo: died. The goblins did not.

## What Bilbo did

Slept through part of it (a stone found his head). Woke up to find the
battle won and his friends dead. Took two small chests of treasure and went
home — which, given the size of the hoard, was the most restrained decision
in the entire record.

## Lesson

The treasure was never the point. The journey was. Bilbo is the only member
of the company who noticed.
