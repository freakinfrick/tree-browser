# Incident: unauthorised guests in the Front Porch

**Ticket:** GBT-2941-0001 · **Filed by:** the Great Goblin's office (filed
posthumously by his successor) · **Date:** TA 2941, summer · **Site:**
Goblin-town, beneath the High Pass · **Severity:** SEV-1 · **Status:** closed
(complainant deceased)

## Summary

A party of thirteen Dwarves and one unclassified small person sheltered from
a thunder-battle in a cave that was, by design, our Front Porch. The crack at
the back opened as intended. Intake proceeded normally until a wizard who was
not on the intake list arrived by other means.

## Sequence

1. guests fall through the Front Porch; ponies confiscated (processed as
   inventory, see `headcount.csv`).
2. guests brought before the Great Goblin. Thorin states they were "on a
   journey to visit relatives". Noted as evasive.
3. the sword **Orcrist** ("Biter") is recognised. Hall erupts.
4. all lights go out. A sword that glows is not the same as a light.
5. the Great Goblin is removed from office by **Glamdring** ("Beater").
6. guests exit by the back door. One guest is counted out as missing and
   later turns up on the far side of the mountain with a new ring and no
   explanation (`01_lonely-mountain/the-quest/riddles-in-the-dark.md`).

## Root cause

The Front Porch has no check for wizards. It has never had one.
