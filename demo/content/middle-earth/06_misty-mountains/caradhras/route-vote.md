## The vote

- **Boromir** — the pass. ("We have wood; we can burn our way up.")
- **Gimli** — under. ("Give me the deep roads and I will not complain of the dark.")
- **Aragorn** — the pass, last. ("I will take the pass first; I will not go under unless no other way remains.")
- **Gandalf** — cast with Aragorn. ("The pass, this once. But if the mountain speaks, we turn for the gate.")

**Result:** *over* carried 3–1 (Gimli dissenting). The mountain then cast its own vote, at 3019-01-12, in snow and stone. See `07_moria/`.
