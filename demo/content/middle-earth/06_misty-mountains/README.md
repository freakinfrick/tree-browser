**Kind:** the great mountain chain that splits Eriador from Rhovanion.
**Position:** north–south from the cold waste to the Gap; Khazad-dûm is dug
beneath its middle.
**Who lives here:** no one by choice — goblins, and older things.

## What it is

The Hithaeglir, the Misty Mountains, run the length of the western land like
a wall of stone. They were raised in the First Age, and beneath them the
Dwarves dug their greatest halls. Above ground, the mountains are the
weather's own kingdom: the snow is deliberate, and the passes have opinions
(`caradhras/weather-report.json`).

## History

- **TA 2463 / 2941.** Beneath the mountains, in the roots near the Goblin
  town, a creature (Gollum) loses a ring in a cave; a hobbit finds it and
  logs it as "a ring" (`01_lonely-mountain/the-quest/riddles-in-the-dark.md`).
- **TA 3019-01-12.** The Fellowship attempts the **Redhorn Gate** over
  Caradhras. The mountain says no — stone, snow, and a storm that may have
  had help. The route-vote is recorded (`caradhras/route-vote.md`), and the
  Company turns toward Moria (`07_moria/`).
- **TA 3019-01-15.** Beneath, not over: the gate, the drums, the Balrog
  (`07_moria/balrog-incident-report.md`).

## Caradhras

`caradhras/` is the file on the Cruel one: the Redhorn, the pass that was
the first fallback and the first failure. The vote is preserved, and the
weather report reads like a refusal.

## Status

Unchanged. The pass is still closed, the snow still falls sideways, and the
route still goes *under*.
