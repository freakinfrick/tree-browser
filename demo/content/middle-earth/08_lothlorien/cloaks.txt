CLOAKS OF LÓRIEN: care instructions

filed: TA 3019-02-15 · issuer: the wardrobe of Caras Galadhon · lot: 9 of 9
issued to the Fellowship on their departure (see 08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md)

- colour: whatever you are near; grey among stones, green among leaves
- fabric: "not magic, just good weaving" — the wardrobe-mistress, TA 3019-02-14
- cloth: elven-cloth, woven to hide the wearer from unfriendly eyes; grey as
  twilight, thin enough to fold, warm in the cold, cool in the heat
- wash: cold, by hand, in a clear stream
- do NOT tumble dry
- brooch: leaf of green and silver; keep it, it may be useful later
- fastening: no seam, no tie; "they wear as if they grew there" — the
  wardrobe-mistress, TA 3019-02-15

recipients (one each): Frodo, Sam, Merry, Pippin, Gandalf, Aragorn, Boromir,
Legolas, Gimli.

later: Pippin and Merry were found at the Black Gate still in theirs, and the
cloak was said to have hidden them from more than one searching orc.
