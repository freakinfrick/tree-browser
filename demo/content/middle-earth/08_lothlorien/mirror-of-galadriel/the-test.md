# The test: Galadriel and the Ring

**Site:** Caras Galadhon, by the Mirror
**Date:** 3019, the night before the Fellowship departed
**Outcome:** she passed. It cost her everything, and she passed.

## What happened

Frodo offered her the Ring. The Lady of Lórien, who had desired the world to
be otherwise for seven thousand years, looked at the thing that would give
her the power to make it so — and was tempted as she had never been.

## The temptation (transcript fragment)

> "In place of a Dark Lord you would have a Queen! Not dark but beautiful
> and terrible as the Morning and the Night! Fair as the Sea and the Sun and
> the Snow upon the Mountain! Dreadful as the Storm and the Lightning!
> Stronger than the foundations of the earth. All shall love me and
> despair!" — Galadriel, at the Mirror, TA 3019-02.

She lifted her hand, and in the light of Nenya she stood revealed — tall
beyond her seeming, and terrible and beautiful. And then the light passed.

## The refusal

She laughed, and it was like the shadow passed. She said:

> "I pass the test. I will diminish, and go into the West, and remain
> Galadriel." — Galadriel, at the Mirror, TA 3019-02.

## Why it matters

This is the moment the Ring's strongest offer was made, to its strongest
would-be owner, and refused. If anyone could have used the Ring — and been
lost to it — it was her. She chose to be less, and stayed herself.

> "I will not go down in darkness, nor will the darkness take me." — her own
> note, added to the record after the fact, TA 3019-02.
