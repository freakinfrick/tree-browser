# Not yet come to pass

> **Record:** mirror-operations-0009 · **Filed:** the Lady of the wood,
> TA 3019-01 · **Site:** the glade in the hollow, Caras Galadhon ·
> **Status:** open — the Mirror keeps its own counsel · **Cross-refs:**
> `visions.log`, `the-test.md`, `../../02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/`

The Mirror shows three kinds of things:

1. things that **were**
2. things that **are**
3. things that **yet may be**

It does not label which is which. Use at your own risk.

> "This is what your folk would call magic, I believe; though I do not
> understand clearly what they mean; and they seem also to use the same word
> of the deceits of the Enemy." — Galadriel, the Mirror, TA 3019-01.

## What it showed the hobbits

- To Sam, it showed the Shire spoiled — trees felled, a new mill, smoke on
  the water (see `../../02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/`). Sam cried that he
  must go home, and did not.
- To Frodo, it showed a lidless Eye, rimmed with fire, searching — and at
  the last it was his own burden it found, swinging on its chain toward the
  water.

> "Remember that the Mirror shows many things, and not all have yet come to
> pass. Some never come to be, unless those that behold the visions turn
> aside from their path to prevent them." — Galadriel, the Mirror, TA 3019-01.

> Do not touch the water.
