# Celeborn, Lord of Lórien

> **Record:** lothlorien-roll-0014 · **Filed:** Haldir, warden of the north
> marches, TA 3019-01-18 · **Status:** current · **Classification:** open to
> the Galadhrim · **Cross-refs:** `mirror-of-galadriel/the-test.md`,
> `08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`

**Consort:** husband of Galadriel
**Role:** co-ruler of Lothlórien, warden of the east, the one who stays.

## Record

- While Galadriel holds Nenya and the Mirror and the weight of the ages,
  Celeborn holds the borders. He is the Lord of the Galadhrim: the army, the
  walls, the watch.
- He is slow to trust outsiders (frosty with Gimli over the Dwarves' old
  wake-the-Balrog habit) and quick to forgive once the danger is over.
- "The Lord's welcome was cold at first, but the thaw, when it came, was
  warmer than any fire in the wood." — Haldir, the west gate of Caras
  Galadhon, TA 3019-01-18.

## In the war

- After the Fellowship leaves, Lórien is attacked three times from Dol
  Guldur (`15_mordor/dol-guldur/`). Celeborn holds it each time.
- After the Ring is destroyed, he leads the Galadhrim across the river and
  throws down Dol Guldur for good; Galadriel, with the light of Nenya,
  unmakes its walls from within.

## Counsel (addendum, TA 3019-02-16)

At the parting of the Fellowship, Celeborn gave the Company counsel, not
gifts — he left the gifts to the Lady (see `08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`).
He spoke first of the river, and last of the way ahead:

- On the choice of the road: "If any of you have not the heart to go on,
  you may remain here, and no evil shall come to you from the wood." — Celeborn,
  the lawn of Caras Galadhon, TA 3019-02-16. None remained.
- On mercy and judgement, to the whole Company and to no one in particular
  (the hobbits took it to heart): "do not be too eager to deal out death in
  judgement; for even the very wise cannot see all ends." (disputed — Gandalf
  spoke near-identical words in Bag End, `02_the-shire/bag-end/`, TA 3018; the
  two sayings have since been cross-filed and neither will own the other.)

## The ending

- He and Galadriel linger into the Fourth Age, then sail West — he last of
  all, because someone had to turn out the lights.
- **Later:** his parting from the wood is not recorded in any log this tree
  holds; the Galadhrim note only that the light went last, and the mallorns
  were left standing (see `mallorn-census.csv` for the final count).
