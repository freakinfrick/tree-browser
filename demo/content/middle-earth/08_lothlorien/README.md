**Kind:** the Golden Wood, realm of the Galadhrim.
**Position:** between the Celebrant and the Anduin, east of Moria; the city
Caras Galadhon at its heart.
**Who lives here:** the Silvan Elves under Celeborn and Galadriel.

## What it is

Lórien is a wood preserved out of time: the **mallorn** trees, golden all
year, grow nowhere else in Middle-earth (`mallorn-census.csv`). Galadriel
keeps **Nenya**, the Ring of Water, and with it the land stays in an ageless
spring — which is why the Fellowship, stepping out, found a month had passed
in what felt like a few days.

## History

- **Since the Elder Days.** The Galadhrim, Silvan Elves of the great woods,
  dwelt here before the return of Sauron; Celeborn and Galadriel became their
  lords after the wars of the Second Age.
- **TA 3019-01-17 to 02-16.** The Fellowship rests here after Moria: blindfolded
  entry for Gimli, the lawn of Caras Galadhon, and the **Mirror of Galadriel**,
  which shows "things that were, things that are, and some things that have
  not yet come to pass" (`mirror-of-galadriel/the-test.md`,
  `mirror-of-galadriel/not-yet-come-to-pass.md`).
- **TA 3019-02-16.** At the parting, the Company receives gifts: cloaks, ropes,
  **lembas** (`lembas-recipe.yaml`), and for Frodo the Phial of Galadriel;
  for Sam, a box of earth with a mallorn seed that will outlive the wood
  (`farewell-gifts.md`).
- **TA 3019-03.** After the Fellowship leaves, Lórien is attacked three times
  from Dol Guldur; Celeborn holds it (`celeborn.md`).
- **TA 3019, the end.** Galadriel throws down Dol Guldur itself
  (`15_mordor/dol-guldur/necromancer-log.md`).
- **Fourth Age.** Celeborn and Galadriel sail West; without Nenya, Lórien
  fades.
