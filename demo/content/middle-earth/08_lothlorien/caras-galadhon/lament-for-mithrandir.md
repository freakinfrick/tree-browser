# Minutes: the Lament for Mithrandir

**Venue:** Caras Galadhon, the hall beneath the great mallorn · **Period:**
TA 3019-01-17 onward, nightly · **Chair:** none (the Elves sing without one)
· **Recorded by:** Frodo Baggins, as guest

## Attendance

The whole city, in the trees. The fellowship, on the ground, understanding
about one word in ten.

## Agenda

1. The fall of Mithrandir at the Bridge (`07_moria/balrog-incident-report.md`).
2. Whether it can be put into the Common Speech.

## Proceedings

- The Elves sing the lament in their own tongue. Legolas declines to
  translate it: "it is too near, and grief is still grief."
- Frodo attempts a verse in Westron about the fireworks. The verse is
  accepted into the record, with thanks, as "a start".
- Sam adds a verse about the rockets that "burst in stars of blue and green".
  Sam states the verse "doesn't come up to the Elves". The Elves disagree
  quietly and do not say so.
