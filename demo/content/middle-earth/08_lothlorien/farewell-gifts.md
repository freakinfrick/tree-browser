## The three hairs (verbatim account)

Gimli was asked what he would have, and for a long while he could not speak.
Then he said, in a low voice, while the Lady stood above him in the light of
the evening:

> "Nothing, unless it might be — unless it is permitted to ask, nay, to name
> a single strand of your hair, which surpasses the gold of the earth as the
> stars surpass the gems of the mine." — Gimli, the lawn of Caras Galadhon,
> TA 3019-02-16.

Galadriel laughed, and the sound was like falling water. "What gift would a
Dwarf ask of the Elves?" she said; and she told him that long ago Fëanor,
greatest of the Noldor, had begged the same of her three times, and three
times been refused. "But you, Gimli son of Glóin, shall have three." —
Galadriel, the lawn of Caras Galadhon, TA 3019-02-16.

Gimli afterwards told Legolas he would set them in imperishable crystal, as
a token of friendship between the Mountain and the Wood.

## Note

The phial is the load-bearing gift of the whole quest. Everything in the
dark of Cirith Ungol would have ended differently without it.
