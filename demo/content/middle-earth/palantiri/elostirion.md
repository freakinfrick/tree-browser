# Elostirion: the stone that only looks West

**Site:** the tallest of the White Towers, on the Tower Hills (Emyn Beraid),
west of the Shire · **Unit:** stone 1 in `registry.md` · **Status:** ONLINE
(one direction only) · **Cross-refs:** `16_grey-havens/README.md`,
`device-spec.yaml`

## What it does

Every other palantír in Middle-earth can be turned to look where its user
wills. The Elostirion stone cannot. It was set by Elendil to look **West**,
across the sea, and it looks nowhere else. On a clear night a user of the
right sort might glimpse Tol Eressëa, and the Tower of Avallónë, where the
master stone stands.

## Access

- Not networked with the other six. Never compromised. The only stone in
  the registry with a clean audit history.
- Tended by Círdan's people. Elves climb the tower in the long years to look
  West and remember.
- The Kings of Arnor came here to look, once, and kept doing so after the
  kingdom fell.

## Last known use

When the Ring-bearers sailed in 3021-09 the stone was, by tradition, taken
aboard with them, and the tower was left to watch the sea on its own.

## Notes

The design choice looks like a limitation. It is the reason the stone was
never misused: there was nothing to its east it would show you.
