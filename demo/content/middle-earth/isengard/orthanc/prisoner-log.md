# Orthanc: detention log — prisoner "Gandalf"

**Prisoner:** Gandalf the Grey
**Charge:** refusing to join Saruman (see `isengard/saruman-memo.pdf`)
**Status:** escaped (assisted). No further detentions.

## Timeline

- 3018: Gandalf rides to Orthanc to consult Saruman. Saruman reveals himself:
  the White has joined Sauron, and would like Gandalf to join too, or
  "remain as a guest".
- Gandalf declines. He is confined to the pinnacle of the tower — the one
  cell with no door, because it is a roof.
- Escape: a moth carries a message; Gwaihir the Windlord arrives and carries
  him away before Saruman's men can loose a shot.

## Lessons

- A wizard's cell is a rookery if the wizard knows the birds.
- Saruman kept the Great Eagles in mind as potential servants, and never
  realised the Eagles have their own chain of command. See `eagles/`.

## Note

The moth is not in the record. The moth is the single most important
un-logged asset of the entire war.

## Prisoners: Meriadoc Brandybuck, Peregrin Took

**Filed by:** Uglúk, captain of the uruk-hai, Orthanc relay, 3019-02-26
**Charge:** "halflings" — to be brought to Isengard intact
**Status:** misdelivered. The escort never reached Orthanc.
**Cross-ref:** `fangorn/`, `fangorn/breaking-of-isengard.md`, `uruk-hai-deployment.yaml` (the Fangorn batch)

### Timeline

- 3019-02-26: captured at Amon Hen. Saruman's instruction, relayed by Uglúk:
  "bring me the halflings alive and unspoiled" — Uglúk, Rauros shore, 3019-02-26.
- 3019-02-27: Gríma Wormtongue sent to inspect the cages (see below). He
  files "unremarkable little things" and returns to his ledger.
- 3019-02-28: column overtaken by the Riders of Rohan on the Fangorn edge.
  The halflings slip away into the trees; the escort does not.
- The uruk-hai burn the forest's edge. The trees do not appreciate the
  gesture. See `fangorn/breaking-of-isengard.md`.

### Gríma's visits

- 3019-02-27: Gríma visits the cages twice, both times carrying the key he
  keeps on a cord under his robe. He asks no questions. He counts them. He
  leaves.
- 3019-02-28: Gríma does not visit. He is heard arguing with Saruman about
  "the wizard's tricks" — Gríma, Orthanc, 3019-02-28. Later he is found by
  the window with the palantír. See `palantir-access.log`.

### Misdelivery

The escort never reached Isengard. The halflings were last logged at the
western edge of Fangorn, moving with something that walks like a tree and
talks like a shepherd. See `fangorn/entmoot/`.

## Addendum (3019-03-05)

The Ents have been here. The tower still stands — the stone would not yield —
but the ring of Isengard is a lake. Prisoner intake is suspended until the
water level falls and Saruman stops shouting from the pinnacle.
