# The Eldest (of Fangorn's trees)

**Kind:** a tree, only a tree. Not an Ent, not a Huorn.
**Rooted:** before the Sun. **Herded by:** nobody; it is older than herding.
**Portrait:** `portrait.jpg`

Treebeard is the oldest living thing that still walks under the Sun.
This is not a thing that walks. It was here when he first woke, and it
had already been here some while, and it has never said what it saw.

The Ents stand near it on the first morning of each Age. Nobody speaks.
Afterwards Fangorn says that it is well, and they go home.

The rings were cored once, in the Third Age, by Fangorn himself, with a
hollow reed and the tree's leave. See `rings/`.
