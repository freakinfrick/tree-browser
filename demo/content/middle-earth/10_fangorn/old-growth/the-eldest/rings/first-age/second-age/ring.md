# Ring: the Second Age

- **Width:** wide for most of the Age, then very narrow at the end.
- **Marks:** smoke at the outer edge; no fire reached here.

The long wide part is the good years, when the great forests still ran
from here to the Shire, and the Entwives kept their gardens across the
river. The narrow part is the Last Alliance, and the burning of those
gardens (`../../../../../entwives/`).

The Eldest did not know what was lost. The Ents did. The ring is narrow
anyway.
