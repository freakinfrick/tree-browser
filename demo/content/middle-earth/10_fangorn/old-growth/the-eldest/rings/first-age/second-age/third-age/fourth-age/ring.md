# Ring: the Fourth Age

- **Width:** still being decided. The Age is young.
- **Marks:** none yet.

This is the ring of the Age of Men. The Elves have gone over the Sea;
the Ents remain, and are fewer, and are not in a hurry about it.

The forest's own tool was renamed in this Age
(`../../../../../../../treebeard/rfcs/0001-rename-from-tree-browser.md`).
The Eldest was not consulted. Nobody thought it would mind.

Further in: `cambium/`, which is not a ring yet. It is the part that is
alive.
