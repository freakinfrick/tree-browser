# Ring: the First Age

The first band of the Eldest that the Sun can take credit for.

- **Width:** wide. The world was new-lit and everything grew.
- **Colour:** gold at the inner edge, where the first sunrise is.
- **Marks:** one dark line near the end (`fire-scar.txt`).

In this Age the Elves woke the trees to speech, and the Ents learned to
talk, and began, very slowly, to talk about everything. The Eldest did
not learn to talk. It was already listening.
