# Ring: the Third Age

- **Width:** narrow, and narrower; a slow dimming, three thousand years.
- **Marks:** axe-marks near the outer edge (`axe-scar.txt`).

The forest shrank in this Age. Men cut the edges and the great woods
became islands. The dark came into the east of Fangorn and the Huorns
grew wakeful and unkind. Some Ents grew treeish and stopped walking.

The last band is thicker, oddly. It is the spring after Isengard.
