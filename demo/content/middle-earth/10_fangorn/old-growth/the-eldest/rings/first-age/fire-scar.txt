FIRE SCAR -- outer edge of the First Age ring

a thin black line, all the way round.

cause:     the War of Wrath, far to the west; smoke came east on
           the wind for a long time. the Eldest was not burned.
           it breathed it.
after:     the land of Beleriand went under the sea.
           the Eldest's roots felt the ground move, once, and settle.
action:    none. there was nothing a tree could do, and the tree
           did it: it kept growing.
