ENTMOOT, DAY 2, MINUTES — 3019-03-02

filed by: the clerk of the Entmoot (the same birch; the numbering is slow, not the hand)

morning:   still on "good morning"
afternoon: news of the trees Saruman has cut
evening:   a long silence, then: "we must march on Isengard."
           nobody says hoom.

vote:      motion to march carried. all Ents in favour; none against.
           "we have decided." — Treebeard, chair, 3019-03-02.
           (this is the closest the Entmoot has come to haste in
            an age, and it was still a full day in the deciding.)

attribute: "do not be hasty" — Treebeard, chair, repeated once more
           as the vote concluded, in case anyone mistook marching
           for hurrying.
