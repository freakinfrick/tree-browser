**Kind:** the oldest surviving forest in Middle-earth, home of the Ents.
**Position:** at the south-eastern feet of the Misty Mountains, bordering
Isengard to the south-west.
**Who lives here:** Treebeard, the Ents, and the Huorns.

## What it is

Fangorn is a remnant of the great woods of the First Age — a forest of
ancient trees, and of the **Ents**, the tree-herders who are older than any
story that names them. Treebeard (Fangorn himself) is the eldest. Nothing
here is hasty; the Entmoot is a parliament that can take three days to say
"yes" (`entmoot/agenda.md`, `entmoot/minutes-day-1.txt`).

## In the war

- **TA 3019-02-26.** Merry and Pippin, escaping the uruk-hai, vanish into the
  eaves of the wood and meet Treebeard.
- **TA 3019-03-01.** Gandalf returns here as the White, after his fall in
  Moria and his battle with the Balrog (`gandalf-reboot.log`).
- **TA 3019-03-03.** The Entmoot ends in anger: the Ents march on Isengard,
  break the dam, and flood the wizard's works (`breaking-of-isengard.md`,
  `12_isengard/orthanc/uruk-hai-production.csv`). The **Huorns** march
  separately to Helm's Deep and eat the orcs (`huorn-migration.csv`,
  `11_rohan/helms-deep/siege-postmortem.md`).
