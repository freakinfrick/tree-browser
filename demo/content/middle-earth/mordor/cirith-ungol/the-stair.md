## Notes for travellers

- The stairs are carved, not built. Whoever carved them did not expect you.
- The guide (Gollum, see `../../gollum/`) knows a side path. It is worse.
- The tunnel at the top has no light, no turning, and a resident who has
  never once left a bad review because it eats the reviewers.

## Do not

- do not stop at the top of the stairs to admire the view of the world you
  are leaving. You will be late for the dark.
- do not follow the second set of footsteps (that is the guide, circling back).

## The descent of 3019-03-13

Two hobbits climbed it this age, on their way down the wrong side of
Mordor. The smaller one, Sam, later told no one; the other one was wrapped
in web before he reached the top. The stair does not keep a guest book; it
keeps a toll. See `mordor/cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`.

> "It is a long way down, and a long way up, and no way out." — Gollum, the
> stair, 3019-03-13 (overheard; see `../../gollum/service-record.md`).
