# The Watchers of Cirith Ungol

**Filed by:** the gate (it watches; it does not file, but it watches) · **TA 3019-03-13**
**Ticket:** WATCHERS-0001 · **Status:** on duty (until the arch comes down)
**Cross-refs:** `mordor/cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`, `mordor/cirith-ungol/the-stair.md`,
`mordor/cirith-ungol/tower-guard-roster.csv`, `lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`

**Site:** the gate between the two towers, on the pass
**Type:** two stone guardians, silent, aware
**Status:** still there. Do not walk between them.

## Behaviour

- They are not alive and not dead; they are the gate's own vigilance.
- Between them there is a barrier no mortal will can pass. Frodo, spent and
  despairing, could not.
- The only thing that broke the barrier was the light of the Phial of
  Galadriel (`lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`), held up by Sam, who then ran
  the gate and watched the watchers shriek as the arch came down.

## The passage of 3019-03-13

Sam carried Frodo down to the gate and found it shut by a will he could not
name. He lifted the Phial, and the will — which was not mortal — looked at
the light, which was older than it, and let go.

> "Aiya Eärendil Elenion Ancalima!" — Sam, the gate of the Two Watchers,
> 3019-03-13 (echo of `mordor/cirith-ungol/shelob-warning.md`).

The Watchers shrieked; the arch fell; the tower behind them — with its
forty dead guards — took no notice, because it was empty (see
`mordor/cirith-ungol/tower-guard-roster.csv`).

## Note

The Watchers are the last thing between Cirith Ungol and Mordor. If you are
reading this and the Watchers are behind you, you have gone the wrong way.
