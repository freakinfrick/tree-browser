**You are entering the lair of Shelob.**
**She is not a spider. She is "the last child of Ungoliant". The distinction
matters to exactly one person, and you are looking at her.**

## Rules of the tunnel

1. There are no rules. There is a web.
2. The web is sticky. The dark is not a blanket; it is *watching*.
3. If you see many little eyes, the many little eyes have already seen you.
4. The only light here is the one the Elves gave you — and it is the only
   thing she will not walk into. Keep it out. Keep it *on*.

## What happened (for the record)

- Gollum led the hobbits here on purpose. His plan: let Shelob take the
  ringbearer, then retrieve the ring from the leftovers. The plan failed in
  the only way that matters: the ring was not in the leftovers.
- Frodo was stung, wrapped, and taken to the tower. Sam, against the
  recommendation of every guide and every instinct, followed (see
  `mordor/cirith-ungol/package-incident.md`).
- The light of Eärendil (`lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`) was the only reason
  the tunnel did not become a tomb.

> If the web is across the path and you cannot see the other side, do not
> duck under it. There is no other side.

## The guide's testimony

- "WE COULD NOT GO ON WITHOUT A LITTLE HELP. SHE ALWAYS HELPS." — Gollum,
  the stair, 3019-03-13 (recorded by the web; the web records everything it
  does not eat).
- "MY PRECIOUSSS." — Gollum, the tunnel, 3019-03-13 (overheard, repeated,
  and not understood by any listener who lived to file it).

## The light

> "Aiya Eärendil Elenion Ancalima!" — Frodo, the tunnel, 3019-03-13, holding
> the Phial of Galadriel (`lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`).

Hail Eärendil, brightest of stars. The phial held the light of the Silmaril,
the only light Shelob had ever fled from in her long hunger. It did not
defeat her; it only reminded her, for one moment, of a light that burned
before the world was bent.

## Addendum (3019-03-13, later)

The web reported one sting, one wrapped package, and one small angry follower
carrying a light it could not touch. Management logged it as "handled" and
the tower logged it as "captured". Both logs were wrong in different ways.
See `mordor/cirith-ungol/the-watchers.md`.
