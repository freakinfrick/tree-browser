# Dol Guldur: the Necromancer's tenure

**Filed by:** the Warden of the Wood (name withheld) · **TA 2941**
**Ticket:** DOLGULDUR-2941-001 · **Status:** VACATED (see addendum;
re-occupied, then thrown down for good in 3019)
**Cross-refs:** `rivendell/istari/dossier.md`, `lonely-mountain/the-quest/`,
`mordor/barad-dur/`

**Site:** the Hill of Sorcery, southern Mirkwood
**Occupant:** Sauron, in disguise as "the Necromancer"
**Status:** vacated 2941 (the White Council served notice); re-taken by
servants later; thrown down for good after the Ring's destruction.

## The disguise

- Sauron rebuilt a body here after the Last Alliance and gathered power in
  the dark of the wood. The Wise knew *something* was there; they called it
  the Necromancer because the alternative was too bad to say out loud.
- Gandalf walked in, alone, once, and came back out. He found Thráin, last
  of the dwarf-kings of Durin's line, dying in the pits — and took from him
  a key and a map, and a warning.

## The eviction (2941)

The White Council attacked while Thorin's company was busy with Smaug.
Sauron retreated east to Mordor, which had been prepared for him. He let the
Council think it was a victory. It was a relocation.

The attack was pressed by Gandalf and delayed — for long enough to matter —
by Saruman, who was already looking into the palantír for his own account
(see `rivendell/istari/dossier.md`). By the time the Council moved, the
Necromancer had already begun moving east.

## The departure (2941)

The log's last entry, in the hand of the Warden:

> "THE HOUSE WAS EMPTY WHEN WE BROKE THE DOOR. IT WAS EMPTY BEFORE WE
> ARRIVED." — the Warden of the Wood, Dol Guldur, 2941.

A victory in the wood, a tenant already gone, and a dark lord who moved
house of his own accord. See `mordor/barad-dur/` for the forwarding address.

## Lesson

When the dark lord you are hunting moves house of his own accord, you have
not won. You have been rescheduled.
