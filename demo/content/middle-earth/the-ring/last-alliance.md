# The Last Alliance of Elves and Men

**Date:** end of the Second Age, 3434–3441
**Belligerents:** the armies of Gil-galad and Elendil vs the host of Sauron
**Result:** Sauron overthrown — but the Ring survived.

**Filed by:** the chronicles of Rivendell (Elrond's archive) · **Ticket:**
WAR-SA-3434 · **Status:** CLOSED (battle), OPEN (the Ring survived it).

## The war

- Sauron had made the One and burned the smiths of Eregion. The Elves and
  the Men of Númenor-in-exile, the only powers that could, joined against
  him.
- Elendil the Tall, High King of Arnor and Gondor, and Gil-galad, the last
  High King of the Noldor, led it — the two thrones, elf and man, for the
  only time in the record.
- The alliance fought across the continent for seven years, then laid siege
  to Barad-dûr for seven more. This is the first recorded attempt on that
  address; see `15_mordor/black-gate/` for the last.

## The duel before the tower

- Elendil the Tall and Gil-galad, the last High King of the Noldor, cast
  Sauron down — and died doing it.
- Isildur, Elendil's son, took the hilt-shard of his father's broken sword
  and cut the Ring from Sauron's hand.

## The refusal

- Elrond and Círdan counselled Isildur to cast the Ring into the fire of
  Mount Doom, which was right there. Isildur refused; he took it as weregild
  for his father.

> "This I will have as weregild for my father, and my brother." — Isildur,
> at the Sammath Naur, SA 3441 (as recorded by Elrond, the only witness who
> would say it plainly).

- Two years later he was dead in the Gladden Fields (`gladden-fields.md`),
  and the Ring was in the river, waiting.

## Why it matters

The Last Alliance won the war and lost the peace. Every line of the Third
Age — every log in this repo — follows from one man standing beside a
volcano and saying "no, I'll keep it."

**Addendum:** the weregild decision is the root entry of the chain of
custody — see `the-ring/bearers.csv` (row "Isildur", SA 3441). Every later
bearer is an incident raised against this one ticket.
