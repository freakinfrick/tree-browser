ARTIFACT LABEL: the One Ring
catalogue no.   FORGE-1600-001 (see forging.md)
material        gold (apparently plain)
markings        none visible at room temperature

RECOVERED MARKINGS (heat test, Bag End, TA 3018-04-13; see heat-test.md)
  script        Fëanorian letters (Tengwar), elvish in form
  language      the Black Speech of Mordor
  position      inside and outside the band, in fine lines of fire
  duration      visible only while heated; fade as the metal cools

  line 1 (transliterated)
      Ash nazg durbatulûk, ash nazg gimbatul,
  line 2 (transliterated)
      ash nazg thrakatulûk agh burzum-ishi krimpatul.

  gloss (short form, per Gandalf; the full verse is filed in rings-of-power.txt)
      one ring to rule them all, one ring to find them,
      one ring to bring them all, and in the darkness bind them.

HANDLING
  - do not read the inscription aloud in Imladris (Gandalf did, at the
    Council, in the Black Speech; the valley went dark; Elrond objected)
  - do not heat for longer than needed. it will not melt. that is the point
  - the only fire hot enough is the one that made it: see
    15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md

PROVENANCE NOTE
  The same words were spoken aloud by the maker at the forging. The Elves
  of Eregion heard them in their own rings and took the Three off at once.
  That was the first incident report. This label is the last.
