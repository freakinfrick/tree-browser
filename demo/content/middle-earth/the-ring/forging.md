# The forging of the Ring

**Site:** Mount Doom (Orodruin), the Sammath Naur ·
**Date:** Second Age c. 1600 ·
**Forger:** Sauron, posing as Annatar, "Lord of Gifts".

**Filed by:** the loremasters of Eregion (surviving notes), recovered by Elrond's
archivists, TA 3018 ·
**Ticket:** FORGE-1600-001 ·
**Status:** CLOSED — artifact destroyed 3019-03-25 (see
`15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`).

## The recipe (as far as it is known)

1. Win the trust of the Elven-smiths of Eregion.
2. Teach them ring-lore. Let them make the Three (Celebrimbor's own work).
3. Make the Seven and the Nine with their help.
4. Secretly return to Mount Doom and make the One — to rule them all.

## What went into it

- a great part of Sauron's own strength and will (this is why its
  destruction unmade him).
- the heat of the only volcano hot enough to hold it.
- a binding verse, burned into the band in the Black Speech.

## Why Mount Doom only

The Ring can be unmade only where it was made. Every other fire is
insufficient. This single fact is the entire reason a hobbit had to walk to
a volcano.

> See `15_mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md` for the other end of the story.

## The Ring's will

The Ring is not a device you wear; it is a device that wears you. It is a
listening instrument — every bearer is gradually owned by the original
author. This is not metaphor; it is the entire failure mode documented in
`SECURITY.md` (CVE-3018-0001): "a single artifact grants its bearer
invisibility, long life, and the undivided attention of Sauron."

- It slips off fingers it does not intend to stay on (see
  `the-ring/gladden-fields.md`).
- It wants only to return to its maker. Every bearer is a courier with an
  overwritten destination field.

**Addendum (TA 3018, Rivendell):** "One Ring to rule them all, One Ring to
find them" — the verse, read aloud by Gandalf at the Council
(`05_rivendell/council-minutes/session-01.md`). The writing burned into the band
is not a poem; it is the device's own handshake, and reciting it in the
Black Speech is how you answer the ping.

**Later (3019-03-25):** artifact failed in the only heat capable of
unmaking it. The forge that made it logged its own decommission.
