# Heat test: Bag End, the parlour fire

**Tester:** Gandalf the Grey · **Assistant:** Frodo Baggins (owner, reluctant) ·
**Date:** TA 3018-04-13 · **Record:** `RING-TEST-3018-001` ·
**Status:** POSITIVE (identity confirmed) · **Cross-refs:** `inscription.txt`,
`provenance.md`, `bearers.csv`, `02_the-shire/bag-end/`

## Background

Gandalf had suspected the ring since Bilbo's party in 3001: the lie about
how it was won, the fact that Bilbo did not seem to age, the trouble he had
leaving it on the mantelpiece. Seventeen years of research followed,
including the scroll of Isildur in Minas Tirith and a long conversation with
Gollum, who was not a willing witness.

## Procedure

1. Ask the owner for the ring. Note that the owner hesitates. (Recorded.)
2. Throw it into the fire without warning. Note that the owner cries out
   and reaches for the tongs. (Recorded; restrained.)
3. Wait a short time.
4. Retrieve the ring with the tongs. Hand it back to the owner.

## Observations

| check | expected for an ordinary ring | observed |
|---|---|---|
| temperature on retrieval | hot | cool, as if never heated |
| weight in hand | light | heavy, heavier than the owner remembered |
| markings | none | fine letters of fire, inside and out |
| metal after fire | discoloured | unmarked; no ordinary fire can harm it |

## Conclusion

This is the One Ring, the Ruling Ring, lost by Isildur at the Gladden
Fields (`gladden-fields.md`) and sought by its maker ever since. It has been
sitting in a hobbit's pocket for seventy-seven years.

## Recommendations

- Owner to leave the Shire, under an assumed name, as soon as practicable.
- Destination: Rivendell, first. Destination after that: the Council's call.
- Owner asked whether the tester would take it instead. Tester declined, at
  length, and with some feeling. (See `05_rivendell/`.)
