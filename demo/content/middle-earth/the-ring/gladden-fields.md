# The Disaster of the Gladden Fields

**Date:** Third Age 2 · **Site:** the Gladden Fields, near the Anduin ·
**Loss:** Isildur, and the Ring with him.

**Filed by:** the survivors' account, set down for the heirs of Arnor ·
**Ticket:** AMBUSH-TA-0002 · **Status:** unresolved for 2,461 years (see
`the-ring/bearers.csv`, row "the River Anduin").

## What happened

Isildur, marching north to claim Arnor, was ambushed by orcs. He put on the
Ring to escape unseen. The Ring, which had no intention of staying with
Isildur, slipped from his finger as he swam the river.

> "The Ring betrayed him." — the Arnor household record (disputed: the
> Ring maintains it "merely changed owners"), the Gladden Fields, TA 2.

## Aftermath

- Isildur: shot by orcs, the Ring gone from him.
- the Ring: sank to the riverbed, where it lay for two and a half thousand
  years — the longest quiet in its history.
- the inheritance: Arnor's line broken; the Ring forgotten, waiting.

## Why it matters

The Gladden Fields are where the Ring learned it can leave a bearer anytime
it likes. Every owner after Isildur is, in some sense, just a longer version
of the same swim.

**Addendum:** the next bearer came out of the same river — Déagol, TA 2463,
fished it up and was dead within the hour. See `the-ring/bearers.csv` for
the full ledger, and `the-ring/last-alliance.md` for the decision that put
Isildur on this road.
