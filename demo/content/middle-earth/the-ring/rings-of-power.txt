RINGS OF POWER: DISTRIBUTION LEDGER
(as recited in the rhyme of lore)

filed by: the loremasters of Rivendell · TA 3018-10-25 ·
ticket: LEDGER-RINGS-0001 · status: 19 of 20 rings accounted for (the One:
destroyed 3019-03-25; the Nine: wraith-ridden, see roster below)

recited aloud at the Council by Gandalf, 3018-10-25, and not without
incident — the sky went dark at "burzum" (see
rivendell/council-minutes/session-01.md). do not recite in the Black Speech
indoors.

Three Rings for the Elven-kings under the sky
Seven for the Dwarf-lords in their halls of stone
Nine for Mortal Men doomed to die
One for the Dark Lord on his dark throne
In the Land of Mordor where the Shadows lie.

One Ring to rule them all, One Ring to find them,
One Ring to bring them all and in the darkness bind them
In the Land of Mordor where the Shadows lie.

THE THREE (elven, made by Celebrimbor, never touched by Sauron):
  Vilya  (sapphire, ring of Air)  -> Gil-galad -> Elrond
  Nenya  (adamant, ring of Water) -> Galadriel
  Narya  (ruby, ring of Fire)     -> Cirdan -> Gandalf

THE SEVEN (dwarven; the dwarves proved hard to own):
  the Longbeards' ring was recovered last -> Thrain -> taken in Dol Guldur
  the other six: consumed by dragons, or recovered by Sauron. the dwarves
    grew greedy, not obedient — a known defect in the owning protocol
    (see SECURITY.md, "the Seven did not take")

THE NINE (for men; all nine became wraiths):
  see mordor/barad-dur/nazgul-roster.csv
  three of the nine were Númenórean lords; all nine are now the Nazgûl,
    led by the Witch-king of Angmar (see runbooks/nazgul-sighting.md)

THE ONE (the subject of this repo):
  destroyed 3019-03-25. no reissue.
