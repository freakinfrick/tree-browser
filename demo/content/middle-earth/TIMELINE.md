# Timeline of the Third Age (event stream)

*The story, in the order the machines logged it. Timestamps are Shire
Reckoning; some are estimates because a hobbit ate the logbook's last page.*

```text
SA 1600     Sauron forges the Ring in Mount Doom. see the-ring/forging.md.
TA 2        Isildur loses it in the Gladden Fields. see the-ring/gladden-fields.md.
TA 2463     Déagol finds it; Gollum takes it. see the-ring/bearers.csv.
2941        a hobbit finds a ring in a cave. logs it as "a ring".
2941        Smaug is retired; the Battle of Five Armies. see 01_lonely-mountain/.
3001-09-22  the Long-expected Party. host vanishes mid-speech. see bag-end logs.
3018-09-23  Frodo leaves Bag End; four hobbits + one ring cross the Hedge 09-26. Bree 09-29, then Weathertop.
3018-09-26  en route, before Bree: Old Man Willow, Tom Bombadil, the Barrow-wights (09-28). see 03_old-forest/.
3018-10-18  Nazgûl attack on Weathertop. see 04_bree/weathertop/incident-report.md.
3018-10-20  the ford. Elrond commands the river. see 05_rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/.
3018-10-25  Council of Elrond. item 4 deferred to lunch, then to session 2.
3018-12-25  the Fellowship (9) leaves Rivendell.
3019-01-12  Caradhras says no. route-vote.md records the argument.
3019-01-15  Moria. "speak, friend." the watcher. drums.
3019-01-15  Gandalf vs Balrog. the bridge breaks. see 07_moria/balrog-incident-report.md.
3019-01-17  Lothlórien. the Mirror shows things that may be.

3019-02-26  Merry & Pippin captured by uruk-hai; misdelivered to Fangorn.
3019-02-29  Éomer meets three hunters; lends horses. see 11_rohan/edoras/eomer-attendance.csv.
3019-03-01  Gandalf returns as the White. see 10_fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log.
3019-03-02  Théoden restored. Gríma's parking revoked. see 11_rohan/edoras/horse-parking.md.
3019-03-03  Ents march on Isengard. see 10_fangorn/huorn-migration.csv.
```

**Footnotes**

- Gollum is present at many of these lines; he is simply never asked to sign in.
- The Eagles appear in only two lines, which is why nobody ever counts on them.
- Samwise Gamgee attended everything except the parts he had to carry Frodo through.
- The Watcher in the Water appears once and is never explained; do not ask it for a password.
