# Riddle-game: dispute record

**Venue:** an island in an underground lake, under the Misty Mountains ·
**Date:** TA 2941 (summer; the venue has no seasons) · **Record:**
`RIDDLE-2941-001` · **Status:** DISPUTED (ruled for the visitor on appeal) ·
**Cross-refs:** `service-record.md`, `precious-ledger.csv`,
`the-ring/bearers.csv`, `02_the-shire/bag-end/`

## Terms (as agreed in the dark)

- **Players:** Gollum (home) and Bilbo Baggins (visitor, lost, armed with a
  small sword that glows only for orcs).
- **Stakes:** if the visitor loses, Gollum eats him. If Gollum loses, he
  shows the visitor the way out.
- **Rules:** ancient and sacred. Even the wicked fear to cheat at them.

## The disputed round

Gollum's guesses: *hands* (one was, then was not), *knife*, *string or
nothing*. All wrong. The appeal board notes:

1. "What have I got in my pocket?" is **not a riddle**. It is a question.
2. Gollum accepted it and guessed anyway, which the board treats as consent.
3. Gollum then went to fetch his "birthday present" to settle the matter
   another way. It was not on the island. It was in the pocket.

## Outcome

- Visitor shown the way out (involuntarily, by following the host, invisible).
- Host: "Thief! Baggins! We hates it for ever!" (filed as a closing statement).
- Bilbo's first written account said the prize was *given*. The account was
  later corrected. Gandalf noticed the lie first, and did not like it.
