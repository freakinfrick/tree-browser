THINGS TO REMEMBER (Barliman, write it DOWN this time)

kept by Barliman Butterbur, innkeeper, the Prancing Pony, Bree.
first page 3018-06 (the letter). this page: 3018-09-29, late.

- letter for Mr. Frodo Baggins, from Gandalf, handed in last summer
  -> send it on to Hobbiton  [ ] not yet  [ ] next week  [x] forgot
  (this is why the Ranger was waiting; see `the-shire/black-riders.md`)
- the black men came round asking for "Baggins". i told them no one of that
  name stops here. he signs the book "Underhill", so it was true, and it
  was also the wrong answer.
- do not tell the next black rider about Mr. Underhill
- order more beer before the dwarves come through
- tell Nob to tell Bob to see to the ponies
  (the ponies are why the room was empty when the riders cut the beds;
   the four slept with Nob and me — see `lost-and-found.md`)
- remember what Nob told Bob
- Strider: owes nothing, knows everything; be polite. he sleeps in the
  common room, hood up, watches the road, and asked after the letter
  before he asked for anything else.

later (3018-09-30, after the raid): room 4 was found slashed, bolsters in
the beds, nobody in them. the stable was broken open and the ponies run off.
i forgot the letter for a whole summer, and that is the only reason any of
this happened on my doorstep. write it down next time, Barliman.
