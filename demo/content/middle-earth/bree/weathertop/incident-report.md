# Incident: Nazgûl raid at Weathertop

**Filed by:** Aragorn son of Arathorn, the Ranger known at Bree as "Strider" ·
**Date filed:** 3018-10-19, on the road · **Incident ID:** AMON-SUL-3018-10-18
**Site:** Amon Sûl (Weathertop) · **Date:** 3018-10-18, night ·
**Severity:** SEV-1 · **Status:** resolved (the victim walked to the ford)
**Refs:** `the-shire/black-riders.md`, `runbooks/nazgul-sighting.md`,
`rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`, `rivendell/elrond-notes.md`

## What happened

- The party camped on the hill. The ranger left to scout; the hobbits lit a
  fire, because hobbits.
- Five of the Nine arrived. Darkness, then blades.
- Frodo (carrying the ring, against all advice) put the ring on. This is the
  one act guaranteed to summon the entire network to your coordinates.

## Account (as filed by the ranger)

Aragorn reports he was off the hill when the Five came up out of the dark,
and that the fire — lit against his explicit instruction — marked the
hobbits from a mile off. By his account the Witch-king himself stood forward
with a long blade, and the point of it took Frodo in the shoulder before
the ranger came back over the rim with fire in both hands. The Nine do not
love fire; they drew back, and did not press. He offers no explanation for
why they left, and none is on file.

## Damage report

- Frodo: shoulder wound from a Morgul-blade. Prognosis at the time: "not great."
- the campfire: put out (finally).
- the hobbits: a lot of shouting, one of them (Merry) permanently changed.

## The wound is a countdown

A Morgul-blade does not wound; it schedules. The point broke off inside the
shoulder and was still travelling inward when they rode — a shard working
toward the heart, a little closer each day. Per `runbooks/nazgul-sighting.md`,
route the victim to Elrond at speed; fire and running water buy minutes,
nothing more.

- shard removed: 3018-10-20, at Rivendell, by Elrond (`rivendell/elrond-notes.md`).
- shard removed in time: yes, by a margin nobody here cares to file.

## What saved them

- the ranger returned, and turned out to be far more than a ranger
  (`bree/prancing-pony/guest-register.csv`, the entry marked "Strider").
- the Nine did not press the advantage, for reasons they never file anywhere.

## Follow-up

- Ride to the ford (see `rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`).
- The shard of the blade is still travelling inward. Estimated arrival: the
  heart. Escalated to Elrond, who does not like being escalated to.

## Addendum (3018-10-20)

Frodo walked the last stretch, then rode, and the wound that should have
taken him in days did not. The ring he carried was the reason they came; it
is also filed, without a ticket number, as the reason he held on.
