# Change request: carry the boats around Sarn Gebir

**Ticket:** AND-3019-0224 · **Raised by:** Aragorn · **Approved by:** the river,
by refusing the alternative · **Date:** TA 3019-02-24 · **Site:** west bank,
above the rapids of Sarn Gebir

## Problem

Downstream of the Brown Lands, the Anduin passes through Sarn Gebir: a mile
of rapids, stakes and rocks. On the night of 3019-02-23 the boats were swept
toward it in the dark and turned back under orc arrows from the east bank.

## Proposed change

Take the boats out on the west bank and carry them along the old portage way
to the quiet water below the rapids.

## Options considered

| option | cost | risk |
|---|---|---|
| shoot the rapids | none | "we will not live to regret it" |
| cross to the east bank | none | the orcs are on the east bank |
| carry the boats | one day, eight people | Sam carries more than his share |

## Rollout

- scouts (Aragorn, Legolas) find the old path in the fog, 3019-02-24 morn
- boats carried one at a time; baggage in two trips
- Gimli carries a boat with Boromir and does not complain about the dark,
  there being none
- all boats relaunched below the rapids by dusk

## Result

Change applied. No rollback required. Next hazard on the route is the Argonath,
which is less a hazard than a statement (`argonath/clearance-report.md`).
