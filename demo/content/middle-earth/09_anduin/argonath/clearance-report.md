# Clearance report: the Gates of Argonath

**Structure:** the Pillars of the Kings, twin statues of Isildur and Anárion
· **Built:** TA ~1340 by Rómendacil II, marking Gondor's northern border ·
**Inspected:** TA 3019-02-25, from a boat, looking up · **Inspector:** Aragorn
son of Arathorn (with a personal interest)

## Findings

| item | spec | observed |
|---|---|---|
| height | "great" | greater than that |
| left hand of each king | raised, palm outward, "in gesture of warning" | still warning |
| right hand | holding an axe | holding an axe |
| helms and crowns | intact | cracked, weathered, birds nesting |
| channel between | navigable | navigable, fast, loud |
| clearance for three grey boats | yes | yes, with the hobbits' eyes shut |

## Notes

- Aragorn stood in the stern and was, briefly, "a king returning from exile
  to his own land". The inspector recuses himself from this line.
- Frodo reports feeling small. Recorded as expected behaviour.
- Beyond the gates: the long lake of Nen Hithoel, Tol Brandir, and the roar
  of Rauros (`../rauros-falls.md`).

## Recommendation

No maintenance. The kings are doing their job: they are still telling
people to stop. Nobody on the river listens either.
