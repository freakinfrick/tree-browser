## What it is

The Anduin is the long road the Fellowship took south from Lórien in three
boats of the Galadhrim (`boats.csv`). It carries them past the **Argonath** —
the Pillars of the Kings, Isildur and Anárion — and down to **Rauros**, the
great falls, where the road runs out and the Company is torn apart
(`rauros-falls.md`).

## The breaking of the Fellowship

At **Amon Hen**, the Hill of Sight, above Rauros (TA 3019-02-26):

- Boromir, under the Ring's pull, tries to take it from Frodo; Frodo puts it
  on and is revealed to himself.
- The uruk-hai of Saruman attack; Boromir falls defending Merry and Pippin
  (`amon-hen/boromir-last-stand.md`).
- Frodo and Sam cross the river alone; Merry and Pippin are taken; Aragorn,
  Legolas, and Gimli send Boromir over the falls and hunt the captives.
- The Fellowship v1.0 is dissolved (`amon-hen/fellowship-dissolution.md`).

`last-known-locations.json` is the machine-readable split: two pipelines, one
ring, and the Eye watching the wrong one.
