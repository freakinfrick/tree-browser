# Rauros: the falls, the hill, the break-up

**Filed by:** the Anduin survey, revised by Aragorn, 3019-02-26 (the day the boats went over)
**Ref:** RAUROS-AT-3019 · **Status:** unchanged (the falls keep falling)

The Anduin flattens here and then forgets to keep being a river; below the
Falls of Rauros the water stops politely carrying boats and starts falling.

## Geography

- **The falls themselves:** a single, impassable drop at the southern reach
  of the river, loud enough to be heard from both banks and from the seat of
  Amon Hen. No boat may be portaged here; the portage road lies on the west
  bank, and the company did not use it.
- **Amon Hen** (hill of the Eye): the seeing-seat on the west bank. Has a
  better view than the palantír and none of the subscription fees.
- **Amon Lhaw** (hill of the Ear): the east bank. All listening, no answers.
- **the boat question**: three boats, one destination each, and nobody
  agreeing on which. The meeting that settled it is not in any minutes,
  because there were no minutes. There was shouting.

## The funeral boat

- On 3019-02-26, one boat was launched with no crew, bearing the sword, horn,
  and shield of Boromir of Gondor, and sent over the falls. See
  `09_anduin/amon-hen/boromir-last-stand.md` and the `funeral` row of
  `09_anduin/boats.csv`.
- It was the only boat that ever went over Rauros by intent. The falls did
  not object; the falls do not object to anything.
- The boat was recovered downriver by Faramir; the horn was cloven in two.

> The company was meant to go south together. The river had other ideas.
