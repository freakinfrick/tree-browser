# Boromir, son of Denethor — final incident report

**Filed by:** Aragorn son of Arathorn, Amon Hen, 3019-02-26, near dusk
**Incident ID:** GOND-AT-3019-0226-003 · **Severity:** fatal · **Status:** closed (body committed to the river)

**Status:** DECEASED (3019-02-26, Parth Galen) · **Cause:** hostile arrows ·
**Contributing factor:** protecting the halflings with the entire horn section.

**Cross-refs:** `09_anduin/amon-hen/fellowship-dissolution.md`, `14_gondor/minas-tirith/stewards-succession.md`, `09_anduin/boats.csv`, `09_anduin/rauros-falls.md`.

## Sequence of events

1. Horn of Gondor sounded twice — warning, not panic.
2. Third blast: heard by Aragorn across the river, too late.
3. Boromir intercepted the uruk-hai carrying Merry and Pippin.
4. Account from survivors: "he was still defending them when he was found." — Aragorn, Parth Galen, 3019-02-26 (reporting the account of the halflings, taken before their capture).

## The horn of Gondor

- Instrument: the Great Horn of Gondor, an heirloom of the Stewards' line; see `14_gondor/minas-tirith/stewards-succession.md` for the chain of custody above him.
- Blown twice at need, then a third time in defeat. The third blast crossed the river; by the time it was answered, the horn was answering alone.
- Post-incident condition: cloven in two, recovered downriver by Faramir (`14_gondor/`, a later incident).

## Tally at the time of death

- arrows in the body: three (reported; the orcs' count is higher)
- Merry & Pippin: taken (see `fangorn`, `isengard`).
- last words to Aragorn: *"I tried to take the Ring from Frodo. I am sorry."* — Boromir, Amon Hen, 3019-02-26.
  Also, unrelatedly, "go to Minas Tirith." — Boromir, same place, same hour (final instruction, not complied with on schedule).

## Addendum (filed later)

- "It is a strange fate that we should suffer so much fear and doubt over so small a thing." — Boromir, the Council of Elrond, 3018-10-25 (entered here as the first recorded sign of the temptation that ended this report).
- Confession on record before death: the bearer and the Ring parted at Amon Hen; the Captain did not part with his regret. No ticket was filed.

## Disposition

- Sword, horn, shield: placed in a boat with no crew, sent over Rauros.
  Logged in `09_anduin/boats.csv` as the funeral boat — an earlier, sadder
  sailing than anything in `16_grey-havens/last-boat-timetable.csv`.
- The boat was found later by Faramir, downriver of the falls. The horn was cloven in two.
