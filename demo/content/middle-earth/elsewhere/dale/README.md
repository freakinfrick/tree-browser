**Kind:** a city of Men, built for trade, in the valley at the Mountain's foot.
**Position:** below the southern spur of Erebor, where the River Running bends;
Lake-town (Esgaroth) downstream on the Long Lake.
**Who lives here:** the Bardings, and a steady traffic of Dwarves buying toys.

## What it is

Dale is the merchant town of the North-east: bells, markets, and the best
toymakers in Middle-earth. Its wealth follows Erebor's. When the Mountain is
full, Dale is rich; when a dragon is on the gold, Dale is a ruin.

## History

- **Before TA 2770.** A prosperous town of the Northmen under Girion, lord of
  Dale, trading with the Dwarves above.
- **TA 2770.** Smaug burns Dale in the same night he takes Erebor. The town
  stays a ruin for 171 years.
- **TA 2941.** Bard of Esgaroth, heir of Girion, shoots Smaug with the black
  arrow (`01_lonely-mountain/the-black-arrow.md`). His share of the hoard
  pays for the rebuilding.
- **TA 2944.** Bard rebuilds Dale and becomes its king. Toys resume
  (`toy-market-ledger.csv`).
- **TA 3019-03-17.** The Battle of Dale: Easterlings cross the Carnen and
  attack. King Brand, grandson of Bard, falls before the Gate of Erebor;
  Dáin Ironfoot falls standing over him. The survivors hold the Mountain
  (`siege-of-3019.log`).
- **TA 3019-03-27.** Bard II and Thorin III Stonehelm break the siege.
- **Fourth Age.** Bard II rules; Dale is a friend of the new King in Gondor
  (`succession.yaml`).
