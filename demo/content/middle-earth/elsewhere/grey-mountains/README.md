**Kind:** a long range of cold, bare peaks, rich in gold and full of drakes.
**Position:** north of Mirkwood, running east from Mount Gundabad; the
Withered Heath, where dragons breed, lies in a gap at its eastern end. The
Anduin rises on its southern slopes.
**Who lives here:** nobody sensible. Formerly Durin's Folk; before them, and
after them, dragons.

## What it is

The richest mountains in the North, and the most dangerous place to be rich.
The Dwarves dug here for gold for nearly four hundred years. The dragons
came for it, and in the end the dragons stayed and the Dwarves left.

## History

- **About TA 2000.** Fram son of Frumgar, lord of the Éothéod (the people
  who later became the Rohirrim), kills **Scatha the Worm** and takes its
  hoard. The Dwarves say the hoard was theirs. Fram sends them a necklace
  made of Scatha's teeth by way of reply. The feud outlives him
  (`halls.toml`).
- **TA 2210.** Thorin I leads the heirs of Durin out of Erebor into the
  Grey Mountains, where most of the kin now gather.
- **TA 2570.** Dragons multiply in the northern waste and begin to raid.
- **TA 2589.** King Dáin I and his second son Frór are killed by a great
  cold-drake at the door of their own hall (`drake-sightings.log`).
- **TA 2590.** Durin's Folk leave. Thrór goes back to Erebor with the
  Arkenstone; his brother Grór goes to the Iron Hills.
