**Kind:** a castle-city on a headland over the sea; seat of the Princes.
**Position:** on the western shore of the Bay of Belfalas, southwest of
Minas Tirith; the old Elf-haven of Edhellond a little to the north.
**Who lives here:** the Men of Belfalas, the Swan Knights, a great many gulls.

## What it is

Dol Amroth is the brightest of Gondor's fiefs and the one nearest the sea.
Its banner is a silver ship shaped like a swan on blue. Its princes are said
to have Elvish blood, and nobody who has met them argues.

## History

- **TA 1981.** Amroth, king of Lórien, waits at Edhellond for Nimrodel, who
  never arrives. A storm drives his ship to sea; he leaps overboard to swim
  back and is lost in the bay that now carries his name on its headland.
- **TA 2004.** Galador, son of the Númenórean Imrazôr and the Elf-maiden
  Mithrellas (a companion of Nimrodel), becomes the first Lord of Dol Amroth.
- **TA 3019-03-09.** Prince Imrahil rides into Minas Tirith with his knights
  on grey horses and seven hundred men-at-arms (`swan-knights-roster.csv`).
- **TA 3019-03-15.** Imrahil fights on the Pelennor, finds the Lady Éowyn
  still breathing, and sends her to the Houses of Healing.
- **TA 3019-03-15 to 05-01.** Imrahil holds the city while the King waits
  outside the walls; rides with the host to the Black Gate
  (`imrahil-dispatches.log`).
- **Fourth Age.** His daughter Lothíriel marries Éomer, King of Rohan.
