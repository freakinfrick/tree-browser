**Kind:** a great natural harbour and fortress-city, home of the Corsairs.
**Position:** far south of Gondor, on the coast below the Bay of Belfalas;
the Haradwaith inland behind it.
**Who lives here:** the Corsairs of Umbar: descendants of Gondor's rebels,
mixed with the Men of Harad, and loyal mostly to the tide.

## What it is

Umbar is the best harbour south of the Anduin, and whoever holds it holds
the southern sea. Númenor fortified it; Gondor took it and lost it several
times; for most of the Third Age it has belonged to people who raid Gondor's
coast for a living.

## History

- **SA 2280.** Númenor makes Umbar a great fortress.
- **SA 3261.** Ar-Pharazôn lands here to humble Sauron. Sauron allows himself
  to be humbled, and goes back to Númenor as a prisoner and then an adviser.
  Umbar is where that bad idea came ashore.
- **TA 933.** Gondor's ship-kings take Umbar.
- **TA 1448.** After the Kin-strife, the sons of the usurper Castamir flee to
  Umbar and found the Corsairs. The grudge is permanent.
- **TA 2980.** A captain of Gondor called Thorongil (Aragorn, under another
  name) leads a small fleet into the haven by night, burns much of the Corsair
  fleet, and kills the Captain of the Haven (`raid-2980.md`).
- **TA 3019-03.** Fifty great ships and many smaller sail north for the
  Anduin. At Pelargir, on 3019-03-13, they are taken by Aragorn and the Dead
  (`fleet-manifest.csv`).
- **Fourth Age.** The King makes peace with the South; the haven is quieter.
