# After-action: the night raid on the haven (TA 2980)

**Ref:** GON-2980-UMB · **Filed by:** the Steward's office, Minas Tirith ·
**Commander:** Thorongil, captain of Gondor (name on file: see below) ·
**Severity:** favourable · **Status:** closed; commander resigned the same week

## Summary

A small fleet under Captain Thorongil entered the haven of Umbar at night,
set fire to a large part of the Corsair fleet at anchor, and withdrew. The
Captain of the Haven was slain on the quays. Gondor's losses were light.

## Sequence

1. Approach by night, lanterns covered, from the sea side.
2. Fireships among the moorings; the Corsair ships were close-packed.
3. Fighting on the quays. Thorongil kills the Captain of the Haven.
4. Withdrawal before dawn.

## Aftermath

- On return to Pelargir, when the city wanted a triumph, Thorongil did not
  come home. He sent word, turned east, and crossed the river alone. He was
  not seen in Gondor again for thirty-nine years.
- Steward Ecthelion's heir, Denethor, noted in the file that the captain
  had been "too well liked." This remark has been kept for context.

## Identity

The commander used a name meaning "Eagle of the Star". The office is now
satisfied that he was Aragorn son of Arathorn, who came back in 3019 with a
different fleet and the same habit of arriving from the sea.
