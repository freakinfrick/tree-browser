# Field note: a fallen Southron, Ithilien

**Ref:** ITH-3019-0307-A · **Filed by:** Mablung, Ranger of Ithilien, from
an account given by Samwise Gamgee · **Date:** 3019-03-07 · **Site:** the
Harad Road, north of the Crossroads · **Status:** kept, not closed

## Summary

During the ambush of a Southron column, one of the enemy fell from the
bank above into the fern where the halflings were hiding. He was dead when
he landed. The column's mûmak ran past soon after and was not stopped.

## Description

Brown-skinned, black hair braided with gold, scarlet coat, a collar of gold.
A green arrow-feather in his neck. He had a long spear and a round shield.

## Witness statement (summarised)

The hobbit did not look at the mûmak first. He looked at the man. Asked
about it later, he said he had caught himself wondering who the man was, how
far away home was, and whether he had marched all that way because he
believed what he was told or only because he was told to.

## Disposition

Buried by the rangers where he fell. The name remains unknown. This note is
filed with the Harad record because it is the only line in the file that
asks a question the war did not answer.
