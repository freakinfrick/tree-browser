**Kind:** the whole South: deserts, grasslands, and hot forests, in two halves.
**Position:** south of Gondor and Mordor, below the river Harnen. Near Harad
borders Gondor; Far Harad is further than any Gondor map is confident about.
**Who lives here:** the Haradrim (Southrons), many peoples under many lords,
and the **mûmakil**.

## What it is

Harad is the country the maps of Gondor fill with guesses. Its kings have
been Gondor's enemies more often than its friends, mostly because Sauron's
messengers reached them first and made the better offer.

## History

- **Second Age.** Sauron's influence spreads south; the Black Númenóreans
  of Umbar rule some Haradrim as overlords.
- **TA 1015.** Umbar besieged by the Men of Harad; King Ciryandil of Gondor
  is slain. **TA 1050.** Hyarmendacil I defeats them; Harad acknowledges
  Gondor for a long, grudging while.
- **TA 3019-03-07.** In Ithilien, Faramir's rangers ambush a Southron column
  marching to Mordor. A mûmak runs loose; two hobbits in a thicket see it
  (`mumak-registry.csv`, `a-fallen-southron.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-15.** At the Pelennor, the host of Harad fights under a black
  serpent on scarlet. Théoden kills its chieftain and is himself killed soon
  after; the mûmakil stand like towers in the field, and the riders go round them
  (`muster.yaml`).
- **Fourth Age.** King Elessar makes peace with Harad.
