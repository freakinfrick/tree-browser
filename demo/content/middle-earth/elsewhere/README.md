# elsewhere — out-of-route locations

> **Record:** `elsewhere-index-0001` · **Filed:** the archive, cold storage ·
> **Status:** never visited by the Company · **Classification:** out of scope,
> still in the war · **Cross-refs:** `TIMELINE.md`, `CAST.md`,
> `14_gondor/pelennor-fields/battle-report.md`, `01_lonely-mountain/README.md`

The numbered folders `01`–`16` are the route: the road the Ring actually
took, in the order it took it. This folder is everything else.

None of the Nine walked into any of these places. The road went nowhere near
them. The war went everywhere, though, and each of these regions sent
ships, beasts, knights, or losses into the story the numbered folders tell.
They are filed here, unnumbered, so the journey keeps its order and these
keep their own.

## Regions

| folder | what it is | how the war reached it |
|---|---|---|
| `dale/` | the city of Men under Erebor | besieged in 3019; two kings fall at the gate |
| `umbar/` | the haven of the Corsairs | its fleet sails for Gondor and is taken at Pelargir |
| `harad/` | the South, and its mûmakil | its host marches to the Pelennor under the black serpent |
| `dol-amroth/` | the Prince's city on the sea | the Swan Knights ride to Minas Tirith |
| `grey-mountains/` | Ered Mithrin, dragon country | older: a king killed at his own door |

## Retention policy

Records in this folder are rarely opened and never updated, which is why
they read cold on the heat map. That is correct. Nobody here is waiting on
a courier from Rivendell.

## Filing notes

- Dates are Third Age reckoning (`3019-03-15`) unless marked SA or FA.
- Each region carries a location record (`README.md`) and a few working
  documents. The working documents are the point; the records just say where.
- To walk every region at once: open this folder in `tb` and press `e`.
