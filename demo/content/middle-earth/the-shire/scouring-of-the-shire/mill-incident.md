# The Mill: a change request, rejected

**Filed by:** Bywater Restoration Committee ·
**Date:** 3019-11-08 (decision) · **Ticket:** SCOUR-MILL-004 ·
**Status:** closed — change request denied; rollback applied ·
**Cross-refs:** `sharkey.md`, `ruffians-ledger.csv`, `report.md`,
`the-shire/green-dragon-inn/tab.csv`.

**Old Mill:** small, honest, water-powered, hobbit-scale ·
**New Mill:** large, brick, "modern", foul-smelling, output-first ·
**Decision:** remove the new mill. Restore the old. Remember the tree.

## What was wrong with the new mill

- it ground grain day and night with a great rumbling that the hobbits
  mistook, at first, for Mordor following them home.
- it fouled the Water; the fish went away.
- it was built on the principle that more output is better than a good river.
- Ted Sandyman ran it and never once paid his tab at the Green Dragon
  (`the-shire/green-dragon-inn/tab.csv`, the unpaid row).

## Site chronology

| 3019-11-08 | committee orders the new mill pulled down; old stone recovered |

"Sharkey wanted the mill 'running day and night', and said a hobbit mill
that slept at dusk was 'a toy'. We wrote it down and threw it out."
— the Bywater Restoration Committee minutes, 3019-11-08.

## Resolution

- the new mill was pulled down.
- the old millstone was found and put back where it belonged.
- the water ran clear again, and the swans returned.

## Lesson

Industrialisation is fine, in its place. Its place is not the Shire, and
especially not over a river that everyone loves.

## Later (filed 3019-11-20)

Ted Sandyman's tab at the Green Dragon remains unpaid; the committee has
declined to charge it to the restoration fund. "The new mill was Sharkey's
idea. The tab was Ted's." — Butterbur, in a letter to the Green Dragon,
3019-11-20.
