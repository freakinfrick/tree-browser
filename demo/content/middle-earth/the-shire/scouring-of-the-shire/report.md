# The Scouring of the Shire: incident report

**Filed by:** Shirriff Logging Office, Bywater ·
**Date:** 3019-11-05 (compiled) · **Incident ID:** SCOUR-001 ·
**Severity:** high, resolved · **Cross-refs:** `sharkey.md`,
`mill-incident.md`, `ruffians-ledger.csv`,
`isengard/orthanc/prisoner-log.md`.

**Date:** 3019, after the ring · **Perpetrator:** one wizard, badly down-ranked ·
**Result:** the Shire is restored, slightly less innocent than before.

## What had happened while everyone was away

- 3019-09-22: a "Chief" (alias: Sharkey) had taken over the Shire. He
  arrived with the autumn; by the day of the last anniversary of Bilbo's
  party, the gate was up and the "rules" were posted.
- the mill at Hobbiton had been torn down and replaced with a bigger, uglier
  one; the water was fouled (see `mill-incident.md`).
- trees — including the Party Tree — had been cut down.
- "gatherers" and "sharers" took whatever they liked. The ledgers called it
  collection; the hobbits called it theft (see `ruffians-ledger.csv`).

## The counter-incident

- 3019-11-01: Four travellers walked back into the Shire and found a gate
  across the road.
- 3019-11-02: Merry and Pippin raised the Shire. It turned out the Shire
  did not need much raising.
- 3019-11-03: The battle, such as it was, was won at Bywater. The ruffians
  were expelled.
- Sharkey's identity: Saruman, reduced to spite and a very small staff
  (`see isengard/orthanc/prisoner-log.md` for the happier version of him;
  `sharkey.md` for the end of the record).

## Resolution

- the Party Tree was mourned; a mallorn was planted in its place.
- the mill: removed (`mill-incident.md`).
- Bag End: repainted. The spoons: returned. The Shire: saved, and therefore
  no longer quite the place the hobbits left.

> "I am glad you are here," said Sam. "I am glad the Shire is still worth
> saving." It was, but it had to be re-proven.

## Witness statement (filed 3019-11-05)

"Four of them came back, and two of them were taller than any hobbit has a
right to be, and the small one did not draw a sword at all. The Shire
fought itself free with a mob, a millstone, and a very loud horn."
— Hob Hayward, Bywater, 3019-11-04 (statement given to the Shirriff, later
corrected: "the horn was a rumour; it was mostly the mob").

## Follow-up (filed 3019-11-20)

Sharkey's body was removed from Bag End without ceremony; Gríma
Wormtongue's knife is still in the evidence room, wrapped, unclaimed. No
claimant is expected (`sharkey.md`).
