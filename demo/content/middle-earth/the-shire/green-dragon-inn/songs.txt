SONGS PERMITTED IN THE COMMON ROOM
(approved by the landlord of the Green Dragon, Bywater, after the incident
of 2968-09-22; revised 3018-09-27 on the complaint of a table)

- the one about the bath (hot water only, no splashing)
- the one about the road that goes on (one verse; not all of them)
- the drinking song (only before 10 pm)
- the Man in the Moon one (allowed; not from a standing position; see below)

SONGS NOT PERMITTED
- anything by dwarves (13 voices, 3 hours, the table)
- the Man in the Moon one when Pippin is standing on a table
- any song containing the word "eleventy"

Attribution: all songs above are the Green Dragon's own, that is, they
belong to whoever is still upright when the last verse lands. "The Man in
the Moon" is an old Bywater standard and is led, when permitted, by Rosie
Cotton (`rosie.md`), who is the only singer the landlord has never fined.
