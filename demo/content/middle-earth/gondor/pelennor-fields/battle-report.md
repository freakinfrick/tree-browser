# Battle of the Pelennor Fields: after-action report

**Date:** 3019-03-15 · **Theatre:** the fields before Minas Tirith ·
**Result:** the siege is broken · **Cost:** heavy, and specific.
**Filed by:** the Steward's clerks, from the surviving captains' accounts ·
**Report ID:** GND-PEL-3019-03-15

## Order of battle

- **the defenders:** Minas Tirith, then Rohan, then (at the last) the dead.
- **the attackers:** the host of Minas Morgul, plus everything the East sent.

## Timeline

- **dawn** — the Riders of Rohan arrive. The horn of Théoden is heard.
  (see `the-charge.md` for the speech that preceded the horn.)
- **morning** — the charge. It carries far, then meets the Haradrim and their
  oliphaunts.
- **mid-morning** — the Witch-king descends. Théoden falls, Snowmane with him.
- **mid-morning** — the Witch-king is destroyed. See `eowyn-vs-witchking.md`.
- **late morning** — the black fleet arrives up the river. It is not the
  black fleet. It is Aragorn, and a company of ghosts, and the wind has
  turned. (For the ghosts' side of it, see
  `../../rohan/paths-of-the-dead/oath-keepers.md`.)
- **noon** — the field is ours. The dead are released.

## Reinforcements (in order of arrival)

1. **Rohan** — six thousand riders, at dawn, with the King at their head.
2. **The Dead of Dunharrow** — a debt called in
   (`../../rohan/paths-of-the-dead/oath-keepers.md`), arriving in the black
   ships of the Corsairs at the moment the field had no hour left in it.
3. **The King** — Aragorn, son of Arathorn, flying the standard Elrond's
   daughters had made for him: the White Tree, the Seven Stars, and the
   crown. The clerk notes the field did not so much receive him as
   recognize him.

## Notable casualties

- Théoden, King of Rohan (see `theoden-requiem.txt`).
- the Witch-king of Angmar (a long-overdue outage).
- the charge of the Rohirrim, as a single unit of pure momentum.

## Addendum: what the machines logged

Nothing. The battle was fought in the open, in daylight, and the Eye was
looking the other way — at a hobbit near Cirith Ungol. The clerk appends:
*the Eye's log for 3019-03-15 records, in full: "NOTHING TO REPORT." It is
the most accurate entry the Eye ever filed, and it means the opposite of
what the Eye meant by it.*
