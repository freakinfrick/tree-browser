THE FALL (3019-03-13)
  the east bank was overrun before dawn; the host of Mordor crossed the
  river on boats and on the ruined arches of the lower bridge.
  Faramir held the Causeway Forts until the retreat could not be delayed,
  then withdrew under a rear guard. He was carried back wounded — a dart
  in the shoulder, and the black breath on him. He was taken to the
  Houses of Healing (see gondor/minas-tirith/houses-of-healing/hands-of-the-king.md).
  The great bridge was cast down by our own hands at dusk, to deny the
  enemy the crossing. It will not be rebuilt this season.
  the river is now the wall. See runbooks/siege.md for standing orders.
