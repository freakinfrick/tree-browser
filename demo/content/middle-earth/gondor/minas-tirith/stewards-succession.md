**Status:** RESOLVED 3019-05-01 (see `coronation.md`). The chair is no longer empty.

## The complete roll (Mardil Voronwë to Denethor II)

The line begins with Mardil, called the Steadfast, who took the rod after
King Eärnur rode to Minas Morgul and did not return (TA 2050). Each Steward
ruled in the King's name, swore to yield the chair when the King came again,
and kept the White Tree's hall.

## The chair

The throne of the King in the Court of the Fountain stood empty through all
twenty-six reigns. The Steward sat in a low chair at its foot, with the rod
of office, never on the steps of the dais. "The chair is not mine to fill,"
the Stewards were sworn to say; "I hold it for the King, and until the King
returns." — (the Steward's oath, recited at each taking of office).

> "How long before a steward becomes a king?" *(Boromir, aged 10, court of
> the Steward, 2988)*
> "In other places, a few years. In Gondor, ten thousand years would not
> suffice." — Denethor II, to his son, Minas Tirith, 2988 (recorded by the
> house-keeper; the boy later asked again, at the Council, and was not
> answered).

## Resolution

3019-05-01 — Aragorn son of Arathorn was crowned King Elessar; the chair was
filled; the rod was laid down. Faramir served as Steward under the King and
as Prince of Ithilien, but is not numbered among the Ruling Stewards, for
the King had returned. See `coronation.md`.
