# The coronation of Aragorn

**Date:** 3019-05-01 · **Site:** the Court of the Fountain, Minas Tirith
**Result:** the King returns. The chair is no longer empty
(`stewards-succession.md` can be retired).
**Filed by:** the Warden of the Keys, on order of Faramir, Steward of Gondor ·
**Record:** CORONATION-1 · **Witnesses:** the host of Gondor, the Rohirrim,
the Dúnedain, and "the whole city that had not slept" — reported in
`houses-of-healing/hands-of-the-king.md`.

## The crowning

- Frodo, of all people, carried the crown to Gandalf; Gandalf set it on
  Aragorn's head.
- The people sang; the city, which had not seen a king in an age, looked up.

The crown of Elendil was brought out of the tomb where it had lain since
the death of Eärnur. The oath was spoken before all, and recorded verbatim:

> "This day the lordship of the Kings of old has returned, and with it the
> oath of the King: to rule in justice and mercy, to defend the realm, and
> to sit in the chair of the King until death or the world's ending."
> — Aragorn son of Arathorn, Court of the Fountain, 3019-05-01 (transcribed
> by the scribe of the Steward).

## The sign that preceded it

The coronation was anticipated, not by banner or sword, but in the Houses of
Healing on the day of the Pelennor: the King's hands healed the black breath,
and the herb kingsfoil answered him. See
`houses-of-healing/hands-of-the-king.md` — the healing was the first proof of
the return, and the crowning only confirmed it.

## The White Tree

The dead tree in the Court of the Fountain was the sign of the line of
Elendil. Aragorn did not replace it; a new sapling was found on the
mountain, grown from the fruit of Nimloth, and planted. The old tree was
laid in Rath Dínen with the kings.

## The oaths and the mercy

- Aragorn pardoned the Men of the East and South who had fought for Sauron.
- He sent the Dead on their way, released from the oath of Isildur at last.
- He married Arwen Undómiel (`../../rivendell/arwen.md`) at midsummer.

## Administrative note

Gondor has a king again, and Arnor is re-founded, and the two thrones are
one. The Stewards' line continues — Faramir serves as Prince of Ithilien,
and the records are finally filed under the correct header.
