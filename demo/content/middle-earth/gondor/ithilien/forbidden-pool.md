# The Forbidden Pool: incident report

**Site:** Henneth Annûn, the hidden pool below the cave
**Date:** 3019-03-07
**Severity:** minor (one guide nearly shot; one hobbit extremely wet)
**Filed by:** Anborn, Ranger of Ithilien, on duty at the pool ·
**Incident ID:** ITL-FP-3019-007

## What happened

- Gollum, told to stay put, went fishing in the Forbidden Pool. The pool is
  forbidden because touching it reveals the outpost.
- Faramir's men drew bows; Gollum froze on a rock, lit by moonlight, more
  like a thing than a person.
- Frodo climbed down and talked him in, rather than see him shot.

## The shot that was not taken (attribution)

> "I am not in a shooting mood" — but the bow was drawn the whole time.
> — Anborn, at the pool's edge, 3019-03-07

Faramir held the order to shoot in his hand and did not give it. The guide
was fished out wet, cross, and still under contract (`gollum/service-record.md`).

## Why Frodo saved him

- Pity. The same reason Bilbo did not kill him in the dark under the
  mountain, sixty years earlier. Frodo said of him, "he deserves death", and
  then spared him anyway.

## Long-term impact

That mercy is the entire reason the Ring was destroyed. The Ring was not
unmade by strength, but by the fact that Gollum was still alive to bite a
finger at the edge of the fire (`../../mordor/mount-doom/ring-destruction.md`).

## Lesson

Pity is not a bug in the plan. It was the plan, all along, and nobody knew
it.

## Addendum (filed 3019-03-08)

Anborn later reported that the same guide was brought to Henneth Annûn,
bound and blindfolded, "complaining in two voices." The ranger closes the
entry: *the pool has since been declared no less forbidden, and one hobbit
has been declared no less wet.*
