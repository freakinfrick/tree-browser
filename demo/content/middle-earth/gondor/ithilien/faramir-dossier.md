# Faramir, Captain of Gondor — service record

**Rank:** Captain of the Ithilien Rangers (then Steward, then Prince of Ithilien)
**File opened:** by the Steward's house, on his commissioning · **Maintained by:**
the Tower records, Minas Tirith · **Record ID:** GND-ITL-FAR-001 ·
**Status:** ACTIVE (promoted 3019, three times in one season; see below)

**Father:** Denethor II (a complicated man; see his pyre in
`../minas-tirith/siege-of-gondor.md`)
**Brother:** Boromir (see `../../anduin/amon-hen/boromir-last-stand.md`)

## Notable postings

- Commander of the Rangers of Ithilien — the guerilla force holding the land
  between the Anduin and Mordor.
- Base of operations: Henneth Annûn (`henneth-annun.md`).

## The Ring test

Faramir captured Frodo and Sam in Ithilien and deduced they carried
something of great power. Offered the thing he wanted most (his father's
approval, Gondor's strength), he said:

> "I would not take this thing, if it lay by the highway."
> — Faramir, Henneth Annûn, 3019-03-07

He is one of exactly two Men in the entire record who held the Ring within
reach and let it go. The other was his brother, and his brother died
failing at it.

## Addendum: the Boromir contrast (filed by the same clerk, later)

The two sons of Denethor were given the same test and returned different
answers. Boromir reached for the Ring at Amon Hen and was retired as a
casualty of that attempt (`../../anduin/amon-hen/boromir-last-stand.md`).
Faramir held the Ring, looked at it, and declined the invoice. The clerk
notes, in the margin: *one brother died proving what the Ring costs; the
other lived proving what it does not have to.* No further explanation is
offered, because the record is not a sermon.

## Later record

- Wounded at the Pelennor; healed by Aragorn with athelas
  (`../minas-tirith/houses-of-healing/athelas-inventory.csv`,
  `../minas-tirith/houses-of-healing/hands-of-the-king.md`).
- Fell in love with Éowyn in the Houses of Healing. The records note that
  the King of Rohan and the future King of Gondor both stood back and let
  this happen.
