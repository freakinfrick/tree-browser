# Henneth Annûn: the Window of the Sunset

**Type:** hidden outpost · **Location:** a cave behind a waterfall in Ithilien
**Crew:** the Rangers of Ithilien · **CO:** Captain Faramir ·
**Established:** long before the record · **Record ID:** ITL-HA-SITE ·
**Status:** ACTIVE (then handed over, 3019, with the rest of Ithilien)

## Site notes

- Entry is through the waterfall. If you do not know the path, the water
  does not part for you.
- The name means "Window of the Sunset": the cave mouth faces west, and at
  evening the whole cave fills with red and gold.
- Rules: no open fires after dark (the Eye watches), speak quietly, and
  anything you carry will be inspected.

## Visitors log (abridged)

- **3019-03-07** — two small travellers (Frodo and Sam), one guide (Gollum,
  under guard, in the Forbidden Pool — see `forbidden-pool.md`). Entry
  recorded by the watch as *"three small persons, one of them arguing with
  itself."*
- the travellers were fed, questioned, and released with supplies. This was
  not the plan, but it was the right call.

## Note on the interrogation

Faramir learned of the Ring here. He had it, briefly, in his custody. He let
it go. There is no document recording why, because the reason is simply
that he meant what he said.

## Addendum: what the captain actually said (attributed)

> "I would not take this thing, if it lay by the highway."
> — Faramir, to Frodo, Henneth Annûn, 3019-03-07

The clerk adds, dry: *Boromir would have taken it, and did try
(`../../anduin/amon-hen/boromir-last-stand.md`). Faramir only looked at it
and set the carrying of it back on the one who came with it.*

## Release terms (filed 3019-03-08)

The two travellers were released with food and walking-staves, and the
guide was released into their custody on parole. Faramir's last warning to
the guide is entered verbatim:

> "You must be held by your oath. If you prove false, I will have you
> hunted to the ends of the world." — Faramir, to Gollum, 3019-03-08
> (`gollum/service-record.md` notes the guide's response was not parseable.)
