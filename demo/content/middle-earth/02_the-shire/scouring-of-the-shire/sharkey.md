## Downgrade path

1. **Curumo / Saruman the White** — head of the order, voice of authority.
2. **Saruman of Many Colours** — rebrand; nobody liked it (`12_isengard/orthanc/config.yaml`).
3. **Sharkey** — fell to wrecking a country of farmers out of spite.

## Notable final actions

- had the mill torn down and the Party Tree felled — for no strategic reason
  at all, which is the point.
- attempted to have the hobbits killed at Bag End.
- was spared (twice, by Frodo) and, ungrateful to the last, tried to knife
  Frodo in the doorway. The knife broke on the mithril coat. Sam offered him
  a fair fight; he declined.

## Death

Cut down by his own diminished servant, Gríma Wormtongue, on the doorstep of
Bag End. The Ring destroyed his power; the Shire was only where the embers
landed.

## Attribution (final exchanges, Bag End doorstep, 3019-11-03)

- "You have grown, Halfling. Yes, you have grown very much. You are wise,
  and cruel." — Saruman (as Sharkey) to Frodo Baggins, Bag End, 3019-11-03.
- "If you wish it, you shall have no memory of me." — Saruman's last
  overture, rejected; Bag End, 3019-11-03.
- "Worm! If you come with me I will make an end of you!" — Saruman to Gríma
  Wormtongue, in the doorway, 3019-11-03 (the remark that decided it).
- "You told me to; you told me to do it." — Gríma Wormtongue, as recorded
  by the Shirriff who heard the words through the door; the knife, Bag End,
  3019-11-03 (disputed: no other ear was close enough to confirm).

The knife and the body were both removed the same evening (`report.md`,
follow-up). No claim was filed for either.

> better to do is just a man with a grudge.
