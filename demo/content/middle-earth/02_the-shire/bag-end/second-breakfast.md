## Field rations (for the road)

For provision on the road the pantry follows the leaf-and-wafer standard set
down in `08_lothlorien/lembas-recipe.yaml`; one wafer will do for a day of
travelling, which is more than can be said for seed cake, and considerably
more than can be said for the pie from Tuesday.

## Elevenses resolution

Elevenses on market days will be served at 10:45 precisely, at the bench by
the back door, because the 11:00 crowd arrives hungry and has opinions. On
all other days elevenses remains at 11:00 and is not to be discussed further.
