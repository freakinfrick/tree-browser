# Bilbo's farewell speech

**Filed by:** the Party Committee (self-appointed), the Party Field, Hobbiton, 2968-09-22
**Reference:** INC-2968-111 (one hundred and eleventh birthday; also Frodo's thirty-third)
**Severity:** none (the host scheduled his own disappearance and left instructions)
**Cross-refs:** `unwanted-visitors.log`, `pipe-weed-ledger.csv`, `the-ring/bearers.csv`

**Occasion:** the combined birthday party, the Party Field, 2968-09-22
**Attendance:** one hundred and forty-four, plus a wizard's fireworks
**Result:** the host vanished mid-speech. The Ring passed to Frodo.

## The speech (excerpts)

- "My dear Bagginses and Boffins, and my dear Tooks and Brandybucks, and
  Grubbs, Chubbs, Hornblowers, Bolgers, Bracegirdles, Goodbodies,
  Brockhouses and Proudfoots."
  — Bilbo Baggins, the Party Field, 2968-09-22
- "Proudfeet!"
  — an elderly Proudfoot (given name lost; he was one of the Brockhouse
  cousins, and very particular about the plural), the Party Field, 2968-09-22
- "I don't know half of you half as well as I should like, and I like less
  than half of you half as well as you deserve."
  — Bilbo Baggins, the Party Field, 2968-09-22
- Then he put on the Ring, vanished, and the event log records only
  `host vanished mid-speech (flash, bang)` (`unwanted-visitors.log`).

## Aftermath

- the Ring passed to Frodo, with Bilbo's help and against Bilbo's instincts.
- the relatives queued at Bag End for a week; the spoons went missing;
  Lobelia was a suspect before she was a victim.

## Addendum (filed after the dispersal of the relatives, 2968-09-30)

- Bilbo is the only Ring-bearer in the record to surrender the Ring of his
  own free will, and the only one whose hand took some persuading to let go
  (`the-ring/bearers.csv`, column "how parted"). It nearly broke him, and it
  is why he is remembered as more than a burglar.
- The fourteen relatives in the queue were outlasted by the spoons' absence:
  count fell to 11 of 12 and stayed there (`unwanted-visitors.log`, entry
  2968-09-23). Lobelia's claim that she was "only measuring the parlour for
  curtains" was noted and filed under pending.
- Bilbo left for Rivendell before the week was out and never lived in the
  Shire again. The new occupier of Bag End signs his name "Baggins" and
  means it.
