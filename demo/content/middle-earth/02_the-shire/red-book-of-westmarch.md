# The Red Book of Westmarch

**Origin:** Bilbo's *There and Back Again* (the draft is in
`../05_rivendell/library/red-book.txt`)
**Completed by:** Frodo, then Sam, then Sam's daughter Elanor
**Current location:** the Shire, in the keeping of the Fairbairns of the Towers

## Provenance (authorship, in layers)

| hand | period | portion |
|------|--------|---------|
| Bilbo Baggins | 2942 – 3001 | the Quest of Erebor; "There and Back Again"; the translations from the Elvish begun at Rivendell |
| Frodo Baggins | 3001 – 3019 | the War of the Ring; the years between; "And What Happened After" |
| Samwise Gamgee | 3019 – 3021 | the final chapters; completed in his own hand after Frodo sailed |
| Elanor Gardner (Fairbairn) | Fourth Age 62 onward | binding, titles, and the family copies for the Towers |

The handover dates are marked in the margins: Bilbo left the book to Frodo
in 3001; Frodo left it to Sam in 3021; Sam left it to Elanor in Fourth Age
62. "You may read it when I am gone, and not before, for it has your father
in it more than it has me." — Frodo Baggins to Samwise Gamgee, Bag End,
3021 (as recorded by Sam on the flyleaf).

## What it contains

- Bilbo's account of the Quest of Erebor.
- Frodo's account of the War of the Ring — the part of the story this repo
  is assembled from.
- Sam's continuation, finished in his own hand after Frodo sailed.

## The title page

> "My Diary. My Unexpected Journey. There and Back Again. And What
> Happened After. Adventures of Five Hobbits. The Tale of the Great Ring,
> compiled by Bilbo Baggins from his own observations and the accounts of
> his friends. What we did in the War of the Ring."

## Why it matters

Every document in this tree is, in the fiction, a leaf fallen from this one
book. The logs, the ledgers, the postmortems — all of it is the Red Book
wearing a different uniform. Read this repo as the machine's transcription
of a hobbit's memoir, and every file falls into place.

## Final note (Sam's)

> "Well, I'm back." — the last words of the book, and the only line that
> needed to be.

## Addendum (Elanor's note, Fourth Age 62)

The book gained two further hands after Sam's: Elanor's own, and the
Fairbairn copyists', who added nothing but errors. "The copyists improved
the spelling; the family kept the original, mistakes and all, because the
mistakes were his." — Elanor Gardner, Undertowers.
