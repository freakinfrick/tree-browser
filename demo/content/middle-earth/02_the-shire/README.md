**Kind:** the Hobbits' homeland: farmland, villages, one inn, one post office.
**Position:** between the Brandywine (Baranduin) and the Far Downs, west of
Bree; protected, secretly, by the Rangers of the North.
**Who lives here:** Hobbits, and almost no one else — by design.

## What it is

The Shire is a country of tilled fields, hedgerows, and round doors, divided
into the four Farthings, with Buckland across the river and the Westmarch
added in the Fourth Age. It keeps no army, no king, and no clock beyond the
meal schedule (`bag-end/second-breakfast.md`). It is, in the words of the
records, the *origin*: the Ring was created, found, and inherited here.

## History

- **TA 1601.** The brothers Marcho and Blanco (Fallohides) lead the first
  Hobbits across the Brandywine; the King at Fornost grants them the land.
- **TA 2941.** Bilbo Baggins returns from the Lonely Mountain with a ring he
  found under the mountains, and files it as a trinket (`the-ring/bearers.csv`).
- **The Long-expected Party.** Bilbo's 111th birthday; he vanishes mid-speech,
  leaving Bag End — and the Ring — to Frodo (`bag-end/unwanted-visitors.log`).
- **TA 3018.** Frodo, Sam, Merry, and Pippin leave, pursued by Black Riders
  (`black-riders.md`). The Shire itself is not the field of battle — yet.
- **TA 3019, the Scouring.** Saruman — "Sharkey" — arrives, industrialises
  the mill, and installs ruffians. The four returned hobbits rouse the
  Shire; the Battle of Bywater routs the ruffians; Saruman dies by Gríma's
  hand (`scouring-of-the-shire/report.md`, `scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`).
- **Fourth Age.** Samwise Gamgee serves seven terms as Mayor, plants the
  mallorn seed from Galadriel's box (the only mallorn west of the mountains),
  and the Shire becomes the place where the great histories are kept
  (`fourth-age.md`, `08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`).
