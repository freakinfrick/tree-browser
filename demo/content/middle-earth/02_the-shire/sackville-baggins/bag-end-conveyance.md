# Conveyance file: Bag End, Hobbiton (three owners in two years)

**Office:** the Mayor's clerk, Michel Delving · **File:** BAG-END-3018/3019
**Parties:** Frodo Baggins; Lotho and Lobelia Sackville-Baggins; Frodo
Baggins again · **Status:** returned to original owner, with apologies
**Cross-refs:** `../bag-end/unwanted-visitors.log`,
`../scouring-of-the-shire/report.md`, `../scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`

## Transfer 1: sale (3018, late summer)

- **Vendor:** Frodo Baggins, "moving to Crickhollow for a quiet life in
  Buckland." (He was not.)
- **Purchaser:** Lobelia Sackville-Baggins, on behalf of her son Lotho.
- **Price:** undisclosed; described by the vendor as "a good price" and by
  the purchaser as "robbery".
- **Keys handed over:** 3018-09-23, at the door, with the silver spoons
  counted in front of witnesses. They were all there. The purchaser counted
  them again.

## Transfer 2: occupation (3018-3019)

Lotho ("the Chief") moved in and began buying up mills, farms and pipe-weed.
The purchases were financed by someone in the South; the someone was
Sharkey. Bag End became the Chief's office, then Sharkey's, then a ruin with
a new shed in the garden where the old trees had been.

Lobelia was moved out of the house and into the Lockholes at Michel
Delving, after she went for a party of ruffians with her umbrella. She is
the only resident on file who resisted the occupation from the first day.
It is filed here with some embarrassment by the clerk who once refused
her a parking permit for her cart.

## Transfer 3: return (3019-11)

- Lotho: deceased, in Bag End, at the hand of Wormtongue.
- Lobelia: released from the Lockholes to cheering (she had not been cheered
  before), and so shaken by it that she gave Bag End back to Frodo and
  left for her own people in Hardbottle.
- **Price:** none. The house was not in a condition to charge for.

## Bequest (3020)

Lobelia died the next spring, past a hundred. She left what remained of her
money, and Lotho's, to Frodo, to help hobbits made homeless by the troubles.
The feud is closed. The spoons are still counted every quarter, from habit.
