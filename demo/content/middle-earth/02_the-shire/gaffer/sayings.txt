SAYINGS OF THE GAFFER
Hamfast Gamgee, No. 3 Bagshot Row, Hobbiton. Gardener (retired), potato
authority (not retired). Collected by his son Samwise over many years,
filed here so they stop being quoted wrong at the Ivy Bush.

ON POTATOES
  - The roots of a matter are the part to dig at. Top growth is talk.
  - Taters want a dry spell and a wet spell, and not both on the same day.
  - A poor crop is a gardener's fault; a good crop is the weather's.

ON THE GENTRY
  - Mr. Bilbo is a very nice gentlehobbit, and there's no call to go
    talking about where his money came from.
  - Elves and dragons: the less said. Cabbages and potatoes: say what you
    like, it'll do you no harm.
  - Don't go getting mixed up in the business of your betters, or you'll
    land in trouble too big for you.

ON TROUBLE
  - Where there's life there's hope. (Sam carried this one to Mordor and
    back. It is the only saying in this file to have been field-tested.)
  - It's an ill wind as blows nobody any good, and all's well as ends better.
  - Trouble won't come to a hole that keeps its door shut. (Disproved,
    3018-09-23; see ../bounders/border-watch.log. The Gaffer does not wish
    to discuss it.)

ON BAGSHOT ROW (added 3019-11)
  - They dug up the Row for a gravel pit. They put me in a house on the
    new side. A house is not a hole, whatever it says on the door.
  - Mr. Frodo got me back into a proper place, and my son got me a
    daughter-in-law. I've had worse Novembers.
  - "New Row" is what they're calling it. Sharkey's End is what I'm calling
    it, and I'm the one who lives there.

END OF FILE. The Gaffer has asked that this list be read "in a respectful
voice" and never set to music at the Green Dragon (see
