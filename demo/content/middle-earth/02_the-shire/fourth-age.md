# The Fourth Age: opening entries

**Compiled by:** the Mayor's Office, Hobbiton ·
**Span:** 3021 (sailing of the Ring-bearers) through Fourth Age 61 ·
**Cross-refs:** `16_grey-havens/last-boat-timetable.csv`,
`the-ring/provenance.md`, `red-book-of-westmarch.md`,
`08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md`.

The Ring is gone; the Shire begins its quiet, loudest century.

## Notables

- Samwise Gamgee: Mayor of the Shire (seven terms, because nobody would let
  him stop). Married Rosie Cotton; they had thirteen children and one
  famous box of earth.
- the mallorn: Sam's seed, from Galadriel's box, grew on the Party Field
  where the old tree stood. It is the only mallorn west of the mountains.
  "A nut in a grey box, and now there is a tree the whole West can see the
  sun through." — Samwise Gamgee, Party Field planting, 3019 (see
  `08_lothlorien/farewell-gifts.md` for the gift as logged by Galadriel's
  house).
- Merry and Pippin: grew tall (the Ent-draught), became Master of Buckland
  and the Thain; their accounts of the war were the best-attended readings
  in the Shire.
- Frodo: never entirely healed. The wound, the sting, and the Ring's weight
  did not leave him; the Fourth Age opened with him still in the Shire and
  already part-way West.

The records in `the-ring/provenance.md` close with the Ring un-made; the
Shire's own records open here, with the Ring-bearer's gardener on the
mayoral roll and the only mallorn west of the mountains on the Party Field.

## The last entries

- 3021: Frodo, Bilbo, and Gandalf ride to the Grey Havens
  (`16_grey-havens/last-boat-timetable.csv`).
- Fourth Age 61: Sam sails, last of the Ring-bearers.
- the Red Book stays behind (`red-book-of-westmarch.md`), and the Shire,
  which was never in the great histories, becomes the place the great
  histories are kept.
