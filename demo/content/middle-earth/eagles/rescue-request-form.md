# Great Eagles: rescue request form (ER-1)

**Issued by:** the Eyrie, above the Misty Mountains · **Form rev:** TA 3019 ·
**Cross-refs:** `membership.yaml`, `flight-log.csv`, `why-not-just-fly.md`

Please complete in full. Incomplete forms are carried off by the wind.

## Section A: requester

- [ ] Name, lineage, and who told you eagles do this
- [ ] Are you a friend of the Eagles? (*Being rescued once does not make you
      one. See: Thorin, 2941.*)
- [ ] Is a wizard present? If yes, has he spoken to a moth?

## Section B: situation

- [ ] You are on a burning tree / a pinnacle / an erupting mountain
- [ ] You have finished the thing you came to do
- [ ] The thing cannot be done by flying (*if you think it can, attach reasons;
      they will be returned unread*)

## Section C: eligibility (Eyrie use only)

| check | pass if |
|---|---|
| timing | the hard part is over, or would be over in a moment without help |
| airspace | no winged Nazgûl in pursuit, or the Nazgûl have just been unmade |
| payload | fits in talons; no Ring aboard (a Ring aboard is seen from the Tower at once) |
| authority | Manwë is content; this is never discussed with the requester |
| narrative | granting does not skip the story |

## Section D: outcome

- [ ] APPROVED: rescue arrives late, exactly once
- [ ] DENIED: walk. It is good for you. It is also the point.
