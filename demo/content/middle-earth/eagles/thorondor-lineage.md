# The Eagle line: from Thorondor to Gwaihir

**Filed by:** the Eyrie, oral tradition, written down once for the archive ·
**Record:** `EYRIE-LINE-0001` · **Status:** ACTIVE (same service, older
aircraft) · **Cross-refs:** `membership.yaml`, `flight-log.csv`

## First Age: Thorondor, King of Eagles

Wingspan: thirty fathoms. Eyrie: the Crissaegrim, south of Gondolin. Mandate:
from Manwë, to watch the Enemy in the north.

| service | payload | note |
|---|---|---|
| Thangorodrim, the cliff | Maedhros (hung by one wrist) | Fingon found him; Thorondor lifted them both down |
| Angband's gate, after the duel | the body of Fingolfin | the Enemy's face marred by the eagle's talons; the King carried to the mountains for burial |
| Angband | Beren and Lúthien | carried out and home; the Silmaril still inside the wolf, which is another ticket |
| Cirith Thoronath | the Gondolin refugees | Glorfindel fell fighting a Balrog on the pass; Thorondor recovered the body for burial |

## Third Age: Gwaihir the Windlord

Descended (the Eyrie says) from the line of Thorondor. Smaller birds, same
policy. The rescues are logged in `flight-log.csv`: the burning trees
(2941), the pinnacle of Orthanc (3019-02), Zirakzigil after the fight with
Durin's Bane (3019-02, the returned wizard carried to Lórien, not logged as a
flight because nobody filed it), and the Cracks of Doom (3019-03-25).

## Continuity notes

- Every rescue on record happens **after** the hard thing, never instead of
  it. The policy predates anybody asking why.
- No eagle has ever carried an object of power *toward* its destruction.
  The Eyrie considers this a feature.
- The line is old; the service is older; the questions are the same.
