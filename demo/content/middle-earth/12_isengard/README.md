**Kind:** a ring-wall fortress in a vale, built around a tower that was never
meant to be an industry.
**Position:** the southern end of the Misty Mountains, in Nan Curunír — the
Wizard's Vale.
**Who lives here:** no one now; the water runs through it.

## What it is

Isengard is the fortress of the **Orthanc**, a tower of unbreakable black
stone raised by the Dúnedain in the Second Age, its summit holding one of the
palantíri. For centuries it was a watch-post against the West; then the
Stewards of Gondor gave it to **Saruman the White** (TA 2759), and the watch
became a factory.

## The fall

- **TA 3018.** Saruman imprisons Gandalf atop Orthanc; Gandalf escapes
  (`orthanc/prisoner-log.md`, `05_rivendell/istari/dossier.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-03.** The Ents march on Isengard: they break the dam and flood
  the vale, drowning the forges and the pits where the **Uruk-hai** were bred
  (`orthanc/uruk-hai-production.csv`, `10_fangorn/breaking-of-isengard.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-06.** Pippin looks into the Orthanc-stone; Sauron is on the
  other end, and the stone is later thrown down (`orthanc/palantir-access.log`,
  `palantiri/registry.md`).
- **TA 3019.** Saruman, shut in his tower, talks and talks (`saruman-memo.pdf.src`)
  until Gandalf breaks his staff; released, he ends his days as "Sharkey" in
  the Shire (`02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`).

## The tower

Orthanc survives everything — the flood, the war, the wizard. In the Fourth
Age, King Elessar searches it and finds the Orthanc-stone and Saruman's
stolen treasures; the tower stands, empty, as a monument to what the machine
did to a garden.

## Status

Decommissioned. The furnaces are out, the water has come in, and the Ents
have planted trees in the ruins.
