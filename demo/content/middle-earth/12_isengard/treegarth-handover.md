# Handover: Isengard to the Onodrim

**Record:** `isengard-roll-0002` · **From:** the Crown of Gondor (King
Elessar) · **To:** Fangorn (Treebeard, eldest of the Ents) · **Effective:**
Fourth Age · **Cross-refs:** `README.md`, `10_fangorn/breaking-of-isengard.md`

## Timeline

- **TA 3019-03-03.** The Ents break the dam; Isengard flooded (`README.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-05.** Saruman confined to Orthanc under Ent guard.
- **TA 3019-08.** Treebeard releases Saruman and Wormtongue: "I do not like
  to see any living thing caged". The travellers, arriving 3019-08-22,
  find the tower empty of its wizard. Gandalf is not pleased; Treebeard is
  not hasty about being told so.
- **TA 3019-08.** Treebeard hands the keys of Orthanc to the King.

## Note on the rename

The new name was agreed by the Ents after due deliberation and is expected
to be in use for an Age at least. It is longer than the old one. The Ents
consider this a feature.
