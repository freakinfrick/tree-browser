# Incident: object thrown from an upper window of Orthanc

**Ticket:** ORT-3019-0305 · **Filed by:** the parley party (Gandalf, Théoden,
Aragorn and company) · **Date:** TA 3019-03-05 · **Site:** the stair of
Orthanc · **Severity:** SEV-2 (would have been SEV-0 if it had hit) ·
**Status:** closed; object recovered

## Summary

During the parley with Saruman, at the moment Gandalf broke the wizard's
staff and cast him out of the Order, a heavy dark globe was thrown from a
window above. It missed Gandalf, cracked the step, and rolled toward the pool.
Pippin picked it up. Gandalf took it off him.

## Sequence

1. Saruman's Voice: ~20 minutes of charm. Théoden's riders wavered; Théoden did not.
2. Gandalf: "Saruman, your staff is broken." It was.
3. Object falls from above. Missed the wizard, hit the stair.
4. Thrower: Gríma Wormtongue (most likely target: Gandalf or Saruman; he
   may not have known which).
5. Pippin retrieves the object; Gandalf reclaims it under his cloak.

## Object

One of the palantíri: the Orthanc-stone. See `palantir-access.log` for what
happened when Pippin borrowed it back that night, and `palantiri/registry.md`.

## Root cause

A window. The tower had many, and a disgruntled counsellor had nothing else
left to throw.
