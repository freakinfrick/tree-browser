## The find

Aragorn and Gandalf climbed to a hallow above the city, on the slopes of
**Mindolluin**, where the snow meets the stone. The sapling was growing at
the edge of the snow, less than three feet high, already in leaf. Gandalf
reckoned it had lain as seed for many lives of men, waiting.

## The planting

- The Dead Tree was removed with honour and laid to rest in Rath Dínen.
- The sapling was set in the court of the Fountain, in the old place.
- It flowered in June. Arwen arrived at Midsummer.

## Gardener's notes

- Do not prune. Do not hurry it. It has waited this long without advice.
- Watering: the fountain. The fountain has never needed a schedule.
- Guard detail: unchanged. The Guard of the Citadel still wears the tree,
  and now has one to stand beside.
