**Filed by:** the Citadel quartermaster · **Record:** `GC-3019-0309-PT` ·

**Cross-refs:** `../stewards-succession.md`, `../siege-of-gondor.md`,
`palantiri/registry.md`

## The oath

Sworn in the Hall of the Kings, before Denethor II, Steward of Gondor, on
the day of arrival. The recruit offered his sword unasked, in payment of a
debt he felt for Boromir, who died defending him and his cousin at Amon Hen.

The Steward accepted, held the sword across his knees, and administered the
oath of fealty: to speak and be silent, do and let be, come and go, until
his lord release him or death take him or the world end.

## Duties

- Esquire of the chamber to the Steward: wait, pour wine, sing on request.
  (He sang a walking song. The Steward listened. Neither remarked on it.)
- Stood watch at the Citadel gate, 3019-03-10 to 03-15.
- Ran for Gandalf when the Steward ordered the pyre (03-15). This was not
  in the duties. It saved Faramir.

## Discharge

Released from the oath by the King, not the Steward, TA 3019-05. Uniform
retained. The quartermaster did not ask for it back; it would fit no one else.
