# The hands of the king: athelas in use

**Site:** the Houses of Healing, Minas Tirith
**Date:** 3019-03-15, after the Pelennor
**Cases:** three · **Cure:** athelas (kingsfoil), held by the hands of the King.
**Filed by:** the Warden of the Houses, at the king's command ·
**Record:** HEALING-15 · **severity:** none — all three recovered.

## The three patients

1. **Faramir** — a dart-wound and the black breath; his father had given him
   up for dead. He woke and knew the King at once.
2. **Éowyn** — the Witch-king's touch; the black breath, and the shadow of
   the arm that held her. See `../../pelennor-fields/eowyn-vs-witchking.md`.
3. **Merry** — the same shadow, from the same blow.

## Who held the herb

It was not the wardens, nor the chief healer, nor the Steward. It was
Aragorn son of Arathorn, the returning King, who went among the beds and
crushed the leaves with his own hands. The wardens knew the rhyme and did
not believe it; the King did not know the rhyme and proved it. Ioreth, the
wise-woman of the Houses, said it first and loudest:

> "The hands of the king are the hands of a healer, and so shall the
> rightful king be known." — Ioreth, Houses of Healing, Minas Tirith,
> 3019-03-15.

## The cure

The herb was known to the wardens ("kingsfoil"), but they called it a weed
with no virtue. The old rhyme was right, and nobody had read it:

> *When the black breath blows, and death's shadow grows,
> and all lights pass, come athelas!*
> *Life to the dying — in the king's hand lying.*

Aragorn crushed the leaves in steaming water, and the sweetness of the
garden of Ithilien filled the room; and the patients breathed again, and
woke.

## What this proved

The King has returned, because only the King could have done this. It was
the first sign of it that did not need a banner or a crown. The city took
note; four days before the crowning, the rumour was already running that a
healer who came out of the north wore the kingsfoil of the old rhyme, and
that the hands were the hands of a king. See `../coronation.md`.

## Inventory note

See `athelas-inventory.csv` — stock was 0 until "the king asked". After the
king asked, six leaves appeared. Kingsfoil, it turns out, answers to the
same chain of command as everyone else.
