# The charge of the Rohirrim

**Date:** 3019-03-15, dawn · **Formation:** the entire éored of Rohan
**Result:** broke the siege line; rode into legend.
**Filed by:** the Marshal's clerk, Rohan · **Record ID:** ROH-EDO-CHG-3019 ·
**Source:** eyewitness transcription, assembled after the field was won
(see `../../11_rohan/edoras/` for the house this charge rode out of.)

## Théoden's speech (field transcription)

> "Arise, arise, Riders of Théoden! Fell deeds awake: fire and slaughter!
> Spear shall be shaken, shield be splintered, a sword-day, a red day, ere
> the sun rises! Ride now, ride now! Ride to Gondor!"
> — Théoden, King of Rohan, before the line, Pelennor, 3019-03-15

He blew the horn as he spoke, and the horn is said to have burst. The charge
that followed answered with one voice:

> "Death! Death! Death!" — the Rohirrim, in answer, Pelennor, 3019-03-15

## What the charge did

- Six thousand riders hit the lines of Mordor before the sun was up.
- They took the field, then met the Haradrim and the oliphaunts, and the
  charge stalled — but it had already done its work: it broke the siege and
  bought the city the hour it needed.
- Théoden fell in it (`theoden-requiem.txt`); Éowyn and Merry stood over
  him; the Witch-king fell there (`eowyn-vs-witchking.md`).

## Note on the horn

The horn of the Rohirrim is not a warning. It is the sound the morning makes
when the morning is about to be a battlefield.

## Addendum: the night before (attributed)

The charge did not come out of nowhere. The Riders rode the night through
the Stonewain Valley, past the blockade, and the morning found them on the
field before the city had given up. The clerk notes that the King rode the
whole way "on a horse the like of which Rohan has not seen, and will not
again" — and that when he was told the field was already black with the
enemy, he is recorded as answering, "Good. It will be a fair morning's
work." (disputed — the Marshal's clerk could not confirm who heard it.)
