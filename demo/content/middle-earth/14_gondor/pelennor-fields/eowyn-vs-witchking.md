# Incident: the Witch-king is destroyed

**Perpetrator of the prophecy:** the Witch-king of Angmar ·
**Destroyed by:** Éowyn of Rohan (with Merry, as it turned out) ·
**Date:** 3019-03-15 · **Severity:** resolved (for everyone else).
**Filed by:** the Marshal of the Mark's clerk, from witnesses on the field ·
**Incident ID:** PEL-WK-3019-03-15

## What the Witch-king believed

> *"No living man may hinder me."*
> — the Witch-king, to the rider over Théoden's body, Pelennor, 3019-03-15

This is technically an availability guarantee. It held right up until it did
not. The prophecy, it emerges, said **man**, and said nothing at all about
**women**, and said nothing about **hobbits**, and the Witch-king filed no
query about either.

## Sequence

1. Théoden falls; the Witch-king moves in to claim the field.
2. A rider stands over the king's body and will not move. The Witch-king
   repeats the prophecy, louder, as if that helps.
3. The rider removes her helm: **"No living man am I."**
   — Éowyn, daughter of Éomund, Pelennor, 3019-03-15
4. The Witch-king's threat model is now invalid. He is destroyed.

## Contributing factor

Merry's blade — a dagger of Westernesse, wound about with spells against the
Witch-king's own kind — had already found the back of his knee. It unbound
the sinew of the "no living man" claim. The blow Éowyn landed would not have
landed otherwise. (Provenance of the blade:
`../../03_old-forest/barrow-downs/barrow-wight-report.md`, under "Loot
recovered".)

## Addendum: the prophecy, properly sourced

The foretelling "not by the hand of man shall he fall" was spoken long ago,
by Glorfindel, at the fall of Angmar — when the Witch-king fled and Glorfindel
declared he would not fall by the hand of man. The clerk notes the prophecy
was filed without a test case, and the test case walked up with a helm and a
hobbit and deleted the record.

## Lessons

- A prophecy is a regex with no test cases. Write better regexes.
- Do not stand on a battlefield declaring yourself immune. The field has
  statistics.
