**Who lives here:** the Men of Gondor; the Stewards, then the King.

## What it is

Gondor is the twin of the northern kingdom of Arnor, founded by Elendil's
sons **Isildur** and **Anárion**. Its capital moved from the ruined river-city
of **Osgiliath** to the seven-tiered **Minas Tirith**, the White City under
Mount Mindolluin, with the White Tree in its Citadel. The **Stewards** ruled
after the line of kings failed, for nearly a thousand years — Denethor was
the last but one.
