ITHILIEN RANGERS: WILDLIFE SIGHTING FORM
form no. ITL-WS-3019-0307            ref: 14_gondor/ithilien/henneth-annun.md
reported by: Samwise Gamgee (civilian, detained, observing)
witnessed by: Mablung, Damrod (rangers)
date: TA 3019-03-07, mid-morning, the road north of the Crossroads

species:        oliphaunt (Harad war-beast; the Rangers say "mûmak")
count:          1 (confirmed). witness requested it be written "ONE, but a big one"
size:           "grey as a hill, tall as a house (a large house)"
features:       tusks with bands of gold; tower on its back, full of soldiers;
                ears like sails; legs like trees that have learned to walk
behaviour:      charged through the ambush, then off into the woods toward the
                river, trumpeting. rangers did not pursue. nobody pursued.
condition:      unknown. last seen crashing south-east, rider-less
threat level:   to rangers: high. to the Haradrim: also high, as it turned out

witness statement (verbatim, lightly edited for punctuation):
  "There was an Oliphaunt! Nobody at home is going to believe me. The Gaffer
   said they were a tale for fireside. Well, I've seen one, and I don't want
   to see another. Put it down that I saw it. Put down the date."

ranger's note (Damrod): witness would not stop talking about it for the rest
  of the day. confirmed he has heard a rhyme about them in his own country.
  it rhymes. we let him recite it once.

disposition: sighting logged. no further oliphaunts in Ithilien this month;
             more at the Pelennor (see pelennor-fields/battle-report.md).
