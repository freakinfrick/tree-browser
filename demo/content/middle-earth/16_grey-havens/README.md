**Kind:** the Elvish harbor at the end of the world's road.
**Position:** the Gulf of Lune, in the west of Eriador.
**Who lives here:** Círdan and the shipwrights; the Elves who linger.

## What it is

Mithlond is the port from which the Elves sail **West** — down the Straight
Road, the path removed from the circles of the world when it was made round
at the Downfall of Númenor. It was founded by the survivors of Beleriand in
the Second Age, and its lord is **Círdan the Shipwright**, the oldest Elf
remaining in Middle-earth — the one who gave Narya, the Ring of Fire, to
Gandalf at the very beginning (`05_rivendell/istari/dossier.md`).

## The last ship

On **TA 3021-09-29**, the Ring-bearers sail:

- **Frodo**, never fully healed, and **Bilbo**, the oldest Hobbit ever.
- **Gandalf**, carrying Narya, the ring of fire he has held all along.
- **Elrond** and **Galadriel**, the keepers of the other two, their power
  spent with the Ring.

The timetable is one-way (`last-boat-timetable.csv`); the packing list is
minimal (`packing-list.md`). Sam, who carried the Ring for the last miles,
stays — the Red Book remains in the Shire, and he sails only much later, last
of the Ring-bearers.

## Status

Departing. Círdan stays until the last of the Elves, and is said to have
sailed on the very last ship. The Havens are the exit of the record.
