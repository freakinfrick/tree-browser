## Arrival

Sam rode up the Hill as evening came on. There was yellow light, and fire
within; the evening meal was ready, and he was expected. Rose drew him in,
set him in his chair, and put little Elanor on his lap.

He drew a deep breath and said three words: "Well, I'm back."

## Postscript
