# The Watcher in the Water: incident report

**Ticket:** MOR-WG-3019-0115 · **Filed by:** Samwise Gamgee (acting; the
scribe was occupied being dragged) · **Date:** 3019-01-15
**Site:** the West Gate of Moria, the dark pool before the Doors of Durin
**Severity:** SEV-1 (one companion seized; the door sealed behind them)
**Cross-ref:** `07_moria/west-gate/password-hint.txt` · `07_moria/mazarbul-chamber/book-of-records.txt`

## What happened

- The Fellowship solved the door (see `password-hint.txt`; the word was
  "mellon"). While they waited, a tentacled thing from the black pool woke.
- Frodo was seized by the ankle and dragged toward the water. Sam cut at the
  arm; the company hauled Frodo back.
- The Watcher, frustrated, tore the two great trees by the gate down across
  the doors — sealing the company *inside* Moria.

## The asset

An unclassed predator of the black pool — a "many-armed" thing, pale and
sinuous, that coils just under the surface and waits at the door. Its arms
are not quick; its patience is. It did not pursue into the mine. The pool is
its only territory, and it will hold that one crossing forever.

## What the Watcher is

Unknown. Older than the orcs, older than the dwarves' mining. The Book of
Mazarbul (`07_moria/mazarbul-chamber/book-of-records.txt`) records that it took
Óin. It does not speak. It does not need to.

### Addendum

The Watcher struck only after the doors opened and the company lingered at
the water's edge — as though it had been listening for the door, not the
company. No one has returned to test this. No one is expected to.

## Lesson

The riddle on the door is not the hard part of the door. The hard part of
the door is the thing that lives in the water in front of it.
