# Ithildin tracing: maintenance record

**Asset:** the West-gate inscription, Doors of Durin · **Material:**
*ithildin* ("starmoon"), visible only by starlight and moonlight · **Owner:**
Narvi (doors), Celebrimbor of Hollin (lettering) · **Warranty:** expired with
Eregion, SA 1697

## The design

The doors are invisible when shut. The tracing shows the arch, the anvil and
hammer of Durin, the seven stars, the crown, and the star of the house of
Fëanor, with the inscription:

> Ennyn Durin Aran Moria: pedo mellon a minno.

Translation on file: *The Doors of Durin, Lord of Moria. Speak, friend, and
enter.* The sub-line is the maker's mark: *Im Narvi hain echant: Celebrimbor
o Eregion teithant i thiw hin.*

## Service log

- **SA 750.** Installed. Doors open for any friend who speaks the word.
  Everyone is a friend; this is the Second Age.
- **TA 1981.** Dwarves leave. Nobody changes the password.
- **TA 2994.** Balin's colony trapped between the drums and the lake; the
  gate "was shut"; the Watcher takes Óin (`../mazarbul-chamber/book-of-records.txt`).
- **TA 3019-01-13.** Tracing activated by moonlight; read correctly; parsed
  wrongly for most of an evening (`sign-in.log`).
- **TA 3019-01-13.** Doors closed from the lake side. Tracing no longer
  observed. The asset is now a wall.
