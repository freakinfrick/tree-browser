**Kind:** the greatest of the Dwarf-mansions, dug under the Misty Mountains.
**Position:** beneath the mountains at their middle; the West-gate faces the
Hollin Gate; the East-gate opens on the Dimrill Dale.
**Who lives here:** no one now. Something woke up.

## What it is

Khazad-dûm, the Dwarrowdelf, was the seat of Durin's Folk: halls of
unimaginable craft, lit once by lamps and later by the veins of **mithril**
the Dwarves dug out of the deep. It was the richest, then the darkest, of
the Dwarf-holds — the one they delved too deep.

## History

- **First Age.** Durin I (the Deathless) wakes under Mount Gundabad and
  founds Khazad-dûm; his line rules it for ages.
- **TA 1980.** The Dwarves, mining mithril, wake a **Balrog** — Durin's Bane.
- **TA 1981.** The Balrog slays Durin VI; the Dwarves abandon Khazad-dûm, and
  it is renamed **Moria**, the Black Pit.
- **TA 2989–2994.** Balin leads an attempt to reclaim it; the colony is
  destroyed. The Book of Mazarbul records the last days; Gandalf finds it,
  torn, in 3019 (`mazarbul-chamber/book-of-records.txt`).
- **TA 3019-01-15.** The Fellowship passes through: the West-gate opens for
  the word *friend* (`west-gate/lock.rs`, `west-gate/password-hint.txt`), the
  Watcher strikes (`west-gate/watcher-incident.md`), and at the Bridge of
  Khazad-dûm Gandalf holds the Balrog and falls
  (`balrog-incident-report.md`, `durins-bridge-inspection.md`).

## The Balrog

The incident report (`balrog-incident-report.md`) is the definitive filing: a
fire-demon of the First Age, woken by the mining, and removed — at the cost
of the bridge and of Gandalf the Grey, who came back with more privileges
(`10_fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log`).

## Status

Abandoned. The gate still answers to "friend", the drums have stopped, and
the Balrog is gone — but nobody has gone back to verify the third part.
