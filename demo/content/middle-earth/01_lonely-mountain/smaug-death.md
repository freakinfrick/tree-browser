# Smaug: retirement of a dragon

**Subject:** Smaug, chiefest and greatest calamity · **Age:** older than the
mountain he slept on · **Status:** TERMINATED, Third Age 2941, at Laketown.

## Career

- Took the Lonely Mountain from the dwarves, with fire.
- Slept on the treasure for 171 years. His bedding was the entire hoard.
- On waking, burned Laketown to its timbers.

## The kill

- A thrush told Bard the Bowman the one place the dragon's armour was
  missing: a bare patch over the heart, left breast.
- Bard's last arrow (the Black Arrow, secondhand but true) found it.
- Smaug fell from the sky onto the town, which was already on fire, which he
  had already set. It was, all told, a very complete exit.

## Legacy

- the hoard: unguarded (see `treasure-audit.csv` for the resulting mess).
- the mountain: back in dwarvish hands, briefly, then contested by everyone.
- the dragon's place in history: the last great fire-drake. The species does
  not recover.
