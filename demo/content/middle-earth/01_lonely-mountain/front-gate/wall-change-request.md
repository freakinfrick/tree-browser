# Change request CR-2941-07: wall up the Front Gate

**Requested by:** Thorin Oakenshield, King under the Mountain (self-declared,
three days in office) · **Date:** TA 2941, the week after the dragon fell
**Approvers:** the King (yes), the Company (yes, reluctantly), the burglar
(abstained, then did something about it)
**Risk:** HIGH · **Rollback plan:** none filed
**Cross-refs:** `../ravens/roac-dispatches.log`, `../battle-of-five-armies.md`,
`../treasure-audit.csv`

## Change

Close the Front Gate, the one Smaug broke, with dressed stone, leaving
only a narrow opening under the arch, and dam the river so the gate's
approach becomes a pool. Hold the Mountain until the Iron Hills arrive.

## Justification (as filed)

- The hoard is ours by right of Thrór.
- An army of Men and Elves is marching on the hoard, not on the dragon,
  which is dead and therefore not their concern.
- "No one will be admitted who comes in arms."

## Justification (as reviewed by the burglar, unsolicited)

- The Men of the Lake lost their town to the dragon we woke up.
- The Elvenking lost prisoners (us) and would like an apology in any
  currency.
- The Arkenstone is not in the hoard. It is in the burglar's pocket.

## Post-implementation review

The wall worked perfectly and prevented nothing. The burglar left over it
by rope and gave the Arkenstone to Bard, so the parley moved from swords to
a trade, and then the goblins arrived and the question became academic.
The wall was taken down the next spring by Dáin, who wanted his front door
back. Status: **closed, won't fix**.
