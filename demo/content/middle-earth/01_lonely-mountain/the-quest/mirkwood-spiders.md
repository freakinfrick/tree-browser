# Incident: the spiders of Mirkwood

**Site:** Mirkwood, north of the Elf-path
**Date:** Third Age 2941
**Severity:** SEV-1 (dwarves wrapped; the burglar did the rescue)

## What happened

- The company left the path (against every instruction) and was captured by
  the great spiders of the wood.
- Bilbo, using the ring and the sword Sting, cut the webs and lured the
  spiders away with taunts — chiefly "Attercop!" and "Lazy Lob!", which are
  spider-insults and which the spiders did not appreciate.

## Aftermath

- the dwarves: freed, then immediately recaptured by the Wood-elves. This is
  the kind of day it was.
- Bilbo: first real fight. Wrote "Flies and Spiders" into the book, and kept
  the chapter title.

## Lesson

The Ring made Bilbo invisible; it did not make him brave. That part was
new, and it was his own.
