# The barrel escape (logistics)

**Route:** the cellars of the Elvenking's halls → the Forest River → Laketown
**Cargo:** thirteen dwarves, sealed in barrels · **Method:** approved by no one

## Plan

Bilbo, invisible, got the sleeping dwarves into empty barrels and closed
them in, then rode the barrels out on top. The barrels were bound for
Laketown down the river.

## Notes from the manifest

- dwarves packed in barrels: 13 (against cargo regulations)
- one burglar on top: 1, very wet, very proud
- the barrels leak. This is noted nowhere in the plan.

## Arrival

Laketown received a shipment of very tired, very cold, very dwarven barrels.
Bilbo, unbarrelled, announced that they were, in fact, the party that would
reclaim the Lonely Mountain. The Master of Laketown was not entirely
convinced, but the story was too good not to provision.

## Lesson

If you must move thirteen dwarves, move them like goods. It is the only
part of the quest that ran on schedule.
