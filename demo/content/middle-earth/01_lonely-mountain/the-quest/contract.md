# Engagement letter: one burglar, fixed term, share of profits

**Parties:** Thorin Oakenshield & Company (thirteen Dwarves, one wizard as
introducer) and Bilbo Baggins, Esq., of Bag End, Under-Hill, Hobbiton
**Executed:** TA 2941, spring, the morning after the Unexpected Party (the
exact day is not on file; the hobbit overslept it)
**Delivery:** under the clock on the mantelpiece, Bag End; found by Gandalf,
not by the counterparty
**Reference:** EREBOR-2941-BURGLAR-01
**Cross-refs:** `../treasure-audit.csv`, `troll-incident.md`,
`02_the-shire/bag-end/unwanted-visitors.log`

## Summary of terms

| clause | term | as later performed |
|---|---|---|
| role | "burglar" (professional; references from one wizard) | burgled: one cup, one Arkenstone, one dragon's patience |
| remuneration | cash on delivery, up to one fourteenth of total profits (if any) | two small chests, by the burglar's own choice |
| expenses | travelling expenses guaranteed in any event | paid, eventually, in trolls' gold |
| funeral | expenses defrayed by the Company "if occasion arises" | not invoked (the burglar); invoked (three of the Company) |
| start | Green Dragon, Bywater, eleven o'clock sharp | arrived 10:59, no pocket-handkerchief |

## Notes from counsel (Gandalf, unpaid)

- The phrase "if any" in the remuneration clause was drafted by Dwarves and
  carries more weight than it looks.
- "Travelling expenses" was never defined. The burglar did not ask. The
  burglar should have asked.
- The contract names no dragon. Counsel considers this an omission of
  scale, not of fact: everyone at the table knew about the dragon.
- The burglar signed nothing. Acceptance was by conduct: he ran down the
  hill without a hat.

## Performance review (TA 2941, at the Mountain)

The burglar delivered every task in scope and one out of it: he handed the
Company's most valuable asset to the other side of a siege to stop a war.
The Company's chief executive called this theft. On his deathbed he called
it something else and apologised, which no clause required of him.

## Closing entry

Contract closed TA 2941. Burglar returned to Bag End to find himself
declared "presumed dead" and his spoons at auction (see
`02_the-shire/bag-end/unwanted-visitors.log`). The Company took no
responsibility for the auction. The contract did not cover auctions.
