# Queer Lodgings: the bear-man

**Site:** a hall of oak in the wild, east of the Misty Mountains
**Host:** Beorn, a skin-changer (a man who is also a great bear)
**Date:** Third Age 2941

## What the company found

- bees the size of your thumb, dogs that walk on two legs and set the table,
  and a host who could lift a goblin with one hand and not care which.
- Beorn hates goblins and wargs more than he loves any creature, which made
  the dwarves (and their goblin-chase) an easy sell — once Gandalf told the
  story with proper pauses.

## The deal

- food, rest, and ponies to the edge of Mirkwood.
- one favour, unstated, that Beorn would collect at need. He collected it
  later, arriving at the Battle of Five Armies in the shape of a bear, and
  the goblins' line simply ceased to be.

## Note

Beorn's line continued; his son Grimbeorn rules the same people in the
Fourth Age. The bears of the North are not entirely tame. Neither is the
story.
