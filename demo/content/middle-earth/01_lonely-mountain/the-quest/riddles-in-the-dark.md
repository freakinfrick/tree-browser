# Riddles in the Dark

**Site:** a tunnel under the Misty Mountains, after the goblins
**Date:** Third Age 2941
**Outcome:** a hobbit, a ring, and the most expensive riddle in history.

## The game

Bilbo, lost in the dark, met Gollum in his lake. They played a riddle game:
if Gollum won, he ate Bilbo; if Bilbo won, Gollum showed him the way out.

## The final question

Bilbo, groping in his pocket, found a small ring and muttered "what have I
got in my pocket?" — not a riddle, but Gollum treated it as one, guessed
wrong three times, and lost.

## What Bilbo had in his pocket

The One Ring, which Gollum called his birthday present and his Precious.
Bilbo did not know it then. The Ring, as always, knew exactly what it was
doing.

## Aftermath

- Gollum, cheated (in his view), became the story's most persistent
  subcontractor (`../../gollum/service-record.md`).
- Bilbo kept the ring for sixty years, and the finding of it cost him
  nothing and cost everyone else an Age.
