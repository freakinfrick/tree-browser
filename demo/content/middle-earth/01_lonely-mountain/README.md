**Kind:** a Dwarf-kingdom dug inside a single great mountain.
**Position:** east of Mirkwood, at the head of the River Running; the Men of
Dale at its feet.
**Who lives here:** Durin's Folk (the Longbeards), the Men of Dale.

## What it is

Erebor is the Lonely Mountain: not a range, but one peak, hollowed over the
Second and Third Ages into a city of galleries, halls, and treasure-vaults.
It is the seat of the King under the Mountain, and for two centuries it was
the richest of the Dwarf-holds — until the dragon moved in.

## History

- **TA 1999.** Thráin I, fleeing the fall of Khazad-dûm, founds the kingdom
  beneath Erebor and digs up the **Arkenstone**, the Heart of the Mountain.
- **TA 2210** (approx.). The line moves to the Grey Mountains; Erebor is
  briefly empty, then re-settled by Thrór.
- **TA 2770.** **Smaug the Golden** descends from the north, sacks Erebor and
  Dale in a single night, and makes the hoard his bed. Thrór and his heirs
  escape by the secret door. Smaug sleeps on the gold for roughly a century
  and three quarters.
- **TA 2941.** Thorin Oakenshield and twelve companions, with Bilbo Baggins
  as "burglar" and Gandalf as the one who insisted, drive out the dragon:
  Smaug is retired by Bard the Bowman with the last of the black arrows
  (`smaug-death.md`, `the-black-arrow.md`).
- **TA 2941, the Battle of Five Armies.** Men, Elves, and Dwarves against
  the goblins and wargs of the north. Thorin, Fíli, and Kíli fall; Dáin II
  Ironfoot takes the crown. Thorin is buried with the Arkenstone on his
  breast (`battle-of-five-armies.md`).
- **TA 3019-03.** While the south fights at Minas Tirith, Easterlings attack
  Erebor. Dáin II falls defending the body of King Brand of Dale; the Dwarves
  and Men hold the mountain until the Ring's destruction, then break out and
  drive the enemy east.
