**Origin:** Rivendell and the North · **CO:** Halbarad (a kinsman of Aragorn)
**Strength:** thirty Rangers of the Dúnedain, plus two elves
**Elven contingent:** Elladan and Elrohir, the sons of Elrond

## Mission

To find Aragorn and ride with him to war. They rode out of the North,
gathered at the Fords of Isen, and met Aragorn in Rohan after Helm's Deep —
the only army in the record that arrived by being *looked for* rather than
summoned.

## Cargo

- thirty Rangers: no pageantry, all of it lethal.
- the reforged standard of the house of Elendil, woven by Arwen
  (`arwen.md`): the White Tree, seven stars, and the crown, on black.
- one message from Elrond, delivered to Aragorn: take the Paths of the Dead,
  and claim what was promised.

## What they did

- followed Aragorn through the Paths of the Dead. The Rangers, being mortal,
  were the only company that did not lose its nerve; the Dead were a
  specialist unit, but the Rangers held the line between them and the
  living.
- came up the river in the black ships and turned the Pelennor
  (`../14_gondor/pelennor-fields/battle-report.md`).
- Halbarad fell there. The standard he carried was the thing unfurled on the
  field, and the thing everyone remembers.

## The meeting (3019-03)

They found Aragorn in Rohan after Helm's Deep — the only army in the record
that arrived by being *looked for* rather than summoned. Halbarad's first
report, as he records it: "I have thirty with me. That is all of our kindred
that could be gathered in haste." — Halbarad, Rohan (3019-03-06). The two
sons of Elrond spoke with Aragorn alone, and gave him their father's message
and the standard of Arwen. No horns, no ceremony.

## Note

Thirty men. No horses to spare, no horns, no ceremony. The whole point of
the Grey Company is that the Heir of Isildur does not come to claim his
throne alone.

## Addendum (CO's closing entry)

Halbarad did not return to write the last page. The scribes of Gondor closed
the record in his stead: of the thirty-one who rode, the standard of the
house of Elendil was unfurled at the Pelennor, and it is the thing everyone
remembers; the man who carried it fell holding it up
(`../14_gondor/pelennor-fields/battle-report.md`). The charge they joined was
in the same key as the cry that opened it.
