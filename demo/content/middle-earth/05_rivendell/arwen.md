> **Severity:** none. This is not an incident; it is the ending of an age.

**House:** daughter of Elrond, of the line of Lúthien
**Choice:** mortality, for love of Aragorn
**Result:** Queen of Gondor and Arnor; the Evenstar of her people.

## The choice

Elves may sail West or remain; Arwen chose to remain, and to die. She gave
up the immortality of her kind for one mortal life with Aragorn — the same
choice Lúthien made, ages before, and the same sorrow at the end.

## The standard

While the war went on, she wove a banner for Aragorn: the White Tree of
Gondor, seven stars, and the crown of Elendil, on a black field. It was
delivered by her brothers with the Grey Company (`grey-company.md`). It was
unfurled from the black ships at the Pelennor, and it is why the defenders
knew the King had returned.

## The ending

- Married Aragorn after the war. Lived with him for over a hundred years.
- After his death, she went to Cerin Amroth in Lothlórien and laid herself
  down upon the hill, and died there, the last of the Eldar in Middle-earth.
- She does not appear on the manifest at `16_grey-havens/` (TA 3021-09-29).
  Of the House of Elrond, only the master of the house sailed; the Evenstar
  kept the mortal road to its end.

> "I would rather share one lifetime with you than face all the ages of this
> world alone." — Arwen Undómiel, Cerin Amroth, TA 121 (the year of the
> King's death; the words are remembered by the people of the wood, and
> they held).

## Addendum (loremaster's aside)

The Evenstar's choice is the second time the House of Elrond has seen a
daughter of Lúthien's line walk the mortal road: Elrond himself chose the
Elves three thousand years before, and spent those ages only to lose Arwen
to the other half of the same choice (`05_rivendell/elrond-half-elven.md`). The
loremasters note, without comment, that no one has yet filed the symmetry as
a bug.
