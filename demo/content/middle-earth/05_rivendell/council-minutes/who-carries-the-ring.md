## Closing

"You shall be the Fellowship of the Ring." — Elrond, Council (3018-10-25),
after the nine were named. The scribe notes that this was the first time the
escort had a name, and that nobody asked what the Fellowship was a fellowship
*for* beyond the obvious.

## Addendum (scribe's aside)

"The Company of the Ring shall be Nine; and the Nine Walkers shall be set
against the Nine Riders that are evil." — Elrond, Council (3018-10-25). The
arithmetic was not challenged, though the Ring itself was not given a seat.
