## Observations (as filed)

- **Birds.** Thrushes: normal. Crows of the south: "crebain", gathering in
  numbers over Dunland and the Greyflood, flying in flocks that do not act
  like birds. Recommend: watch them.
- **Beasts.** Wolves astir in the Vales of Anduin, earlier than the season
  warrants. The eagles of the Misty Mountains report it too.
- **The Nine.** Crossed the Anduin, riding west in the guise of riders in
  black, asking for the Shire. Seen from above by birds who do not wish to
  be seen by them.

## Messages carried (as filed)

- **From Saruman, to Gandalf:** "Come to Isengard; Saruman will help
  against the Nine." Delivered, in person, on the road near the borders of the
  Shire, at midsummer 3018.
- **To the eagles, from Radagast, on Gandalf's request:** bring any news of
  wolves or the Nine to Orthanc, where Gandalf will be.

## Outcome (added by the Council, not by the author)

The message from Saruman was a trap. The request to the eagles was the way
out of it: Gwaihir the Windlord came to Orthanc with news and found Gandalf
a prisoner on the pinnacle. He carried him off the tower instead
(`../../12_isengard/orthanc/`). Saruman had used Radagast as an honest
courier because he believed him simple. The one thing Saruman did not
account for was that honest couriers carry every message, not just yours.

## Reviewer's note (Elrond)

The report arrived on bark, with moss on it, and a beetle. The content was
correct in every particular. The Council has resolved to stop asking
Radagast to use parchment. Radagast did not attend the Council of Elrond;
he was, by his own account, "busy with a sick badger". This is accepted.
