THE LAY OF EÄRENDIL (working draft, Bilbo's hand; margins by others)
Hall of Fire, Rivendell. Performed 3018-10-24, the night of the feast.
Status: performed once; revised forever.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------
  [draft verse, stanza 1, Bilbo]
    A mariner there was who built
    a ship of silver-birch and gilt,
    and sailed it where no charts were kept,
    while all the lamps of Westward slept.

  [margin, Bilbo]  "birch" is fine. do not let anyone say "birch" is not
                   fine.
  [margin, Aragorn] Birch is right, for once: the timber was felled in
                   Nimbrethil. "Gilt" is decoration. Leave it.

  [draft verse, stanza 2, Bilbo]
    Upon his brow a jewel burned ...

  [margin, Aragorn] Add a green stone. Put it in.
  [margin, Bilbo]  there is already a jewel. it is the Silmaril. it is
                   the whole point of the song.
  [margin, Aragorn] A green stone as well. It matters.
  [margin, Bilbo]  to whom?
  [margin, Aragorn] Put it in.

  [draft verse, stanza 2, revised]
    Upon his brow a jewel burned,
    and at his breast, as I have learned
    from one who would not tell me why,
    a stone of green, like leaves, like sky.

 ------------------------------------------------------------------------

EDITOR'S NOTES (Lindir, of the household of Elrond)
  - The Hall could not tell which parts were written by a Man and which by
    a Hobbit. This was meant as a compliment. Mr. Baggins did not take it
    as one, and neither, oddly, did the Dúnadan.
  - Mortals, to Elvish ears, all sound much alike; this is also a
    compliment, and was also received poorly.
  - Stanzas 3 through 8 are omitted from this file for length. They are
    very long. The Hall was asked to sing them twice. Bilbo counted this
    as an encore.
