## 3018-10, the Ring and the sword

- **3018-10-25.** The Ring came to Council. Gandalf spoke the Black Speech;
  the Hall has not entirely warmed again. It must go to the Fire; there is no
  other answer, and I do not like the answer there is.
- **3018-10-25, late.** The Ring-bearer volunteered before anyone asked him.
  I was not ready to hear it, and he was not ready to say it, and yet it is
  done. The smallest of them is the one who will carry it. (See
  `05_rivendell/council-minutes/who-carries-the-ring.md`.)
- **3018-10-26.** The broken sword again. Aragorn keeps looking at the shards
  as though they owe him a kingdom — which, in fairness, they do. The smiths
  say *two weeks*; the pieces are being remade now. It will be Andúril, and
  it will go by the Paths of the Dead (`11_rohan/paths-of-the-dead/anduril.md`).
  Narsil was broken, not finished.

- Arwen has made her choice. Stop asking her to unmake it. (See arwen.md.)

- If Saruman sends a messenger, do not let him in. If he sends a moth, ask it for the weather.
