**House:** son of Eärendil and Elwing, of both the Eldar and the Edain
**Choice:** to be counted among the Elves
**Result:** lord of Rivendell; keeper of Vilya; the memory of the world.

## The choice of the Half-elven

- The line of Eärendil was given a choice: the fate of the Elves (immortal,
  bound to the world) or the fate of Men (mortal, leaving it). Each chose
  once, for all time.
- Elros, Elrond's brother, chose Men and became the first King of Númenor —
  which is why Aragorn, after sixty-odd generations, is Elrond's kinsman.
- Elrond chose the Elves. This is the root of the great irony of Arwen:
  Elrond waited three thousand years only to lose his daughter to the same
  choice he once made the other way.

## Role in the story

- hosts the Council (`council-minutes/`), heals Frodo of the Morgul-wound,
  reforges Narsil (`11_rohan/paths-of-the-dead/anduril.md`), and sends the Grey
  Company (`grey-company.md`).
- keeper of Vilya, the Ring of Air (`the-ring/rings-of-power.txt`); one of
  the Three that passed to the Elves, and never to the Dark Lord's hand.
- Chair of the White Council, in which capacity he holds a watching brief on
  the Istari (`05_rivendell/istari/dossier.md`), whose coming from the West he
  recorded as "five sent, three remain accounted for."

## The ending

After the war he stayed only a while, then sailed West — but not before
giving Aragorn the crown, the sceptre, and his daughter, which is to say:
everything.

## Addendum (loremaster's aside)

The choice of the Half-elven was offered once, to Eärendil and Elwing and
their sons, and is not a thing a loremaster may revisit lightly: Elros took
the fate of Men and founded Númenor; Elrond took the fate of the Elves and
outlived the kingdom his brother began. The loremasters note, without
comment, that both halves of the choice are now, after TA 3019, closed —
Elros's line is crowned again, and Elrond's house has sailed. The symmetry
holds to the end, which is either the point or the proof.
