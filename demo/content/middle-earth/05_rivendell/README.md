**Kind:** a hidden valley and refuge, home of the Half-elven.
**Position:** in the foothills of the Misty Mountains, at the foot of the
Ford of Bruinen; the Bruinen rings it on the way west.
**Who lives here:** Elrond, his household, the Eldar, and the Wise who pass
through.

## What it is

Imladris — "the deep valley of the cleft" — is the Last Homely House East of
the Sea: a refuge, a library, and the only place in Middle-earth where a
Council could sit down with the whole problem of the Ring and come up with a
plan nobody liked (`council-minutes/who-carries-the-ring.md`). Elrond rules
here and keeps **Vilya**, the Ring of Air, which preserves the valley.

## History

- **SA 1697.** Elrond founds Imladris as a refuge during the war with Sauron,
  after Eregion falls.
- **TA 2941.** Elrond reads the moon-letters on Thrór's map and names the
  swords of the Troll-hoard (`library/translations/moon-letters.py`).
- **TA 3018-10-20.** At the Ford of Bruinen, Elrond and Gandalf raise the
  flood; the Ring-wraiths are swept away, and Frodo is carried to safety
  (`ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`).
- **TA 3018-10-25.** The **Council of Elrond**: the Ring is carried to the
  Fire, and the Fellowship of the Ring is formed — nine walkers, one pony
  (`council-minutes/session-01.md`, `session-02.md`).
- **TA 3019.** The Grey Company musters here; Halbarad brings the banner of
  Aragorn and the reforged sword Andúril, remade from the shards of Narsil
  (`grey-company.md`, `11_rohan/paths-of-the-dead/anduril.md`).
- **3021.** Elrond sails West; the valley fades after him
  (`16_grey-havens/last-boat-timetable.csv`).

`two-towers.md`, the `red-book.txt`, and the `maps/` the Council argued over.
