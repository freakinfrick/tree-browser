**Kind:** a village of Men and Hobbits, and the ruined watchtower that guards
its eastern road.
**Position:** at the crossroads of the Greenway and the East Road, west of
Weathertop.
**Who lives here:** the Bree-folk (Men and Hobbits, together — the only such
settlement), and, unofficially, the Rangers.

## What it is

Bree is the most westerly village of Men and the one place where Hobbits and
Big Folk live side by side, behind a hedge and a gate. Its heart is **the
Prancing Pony**, the inn of Barliman Butterbur — register, bar tab, and a
lost-and-found that has held stranger things than cloaks
(`prancing-pony/lost-and-found.md`). The hobbits meet **Strider** here, a
Ranger the innkeeper forgot to mention.

## Weathertop

**Amon Sûl**, the hill of the ancient watchtower of Arnor, now a ruin. It is
here, on **TA 3018-10-18**, that the Nazgûl attack the hobbits and the
Witch-king wounds Frodo with a Morgul-blade (`weathertop/incident-report.md`).
The wound is a countdown; the only mitigation is Elrond, fast
(`05_rivendell/ford-of-bruinen/flood.log`).

## In the war

- **TA 3018-09-29/30.** Four hobbits reach the Pony, expecting Gandalf; they
  get Strider, a riddle in a letter, and a room swap that saves Frodo's life.
- **TA 3018-10-18.** Weathertop; the Ring-wraiths; a broken sword called
  "kindling" by the innkeeper who did not know it was Narsil
  (`05_rivendell/elrond-half-elven.md`).
- **Later.** Bree sees the Rangers ride out and the war pass it by; the
  Pony's guest book keeps the whole tale in bar tabs and alibis
  (`prancing-pony/guest-register.csv`).
