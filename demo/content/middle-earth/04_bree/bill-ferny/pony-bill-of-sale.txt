BILL OF SALE
======================================================================

Vendor ........ Bill Ferny, of the house by the West-gate, Bree
Purchaser ..... Barliman Butterbur (for the party of "Mr. Underhill")
Date .......... 3018-09-30, morning, in the Pony's yard
Witnesses ..... Strider (said nothing); Sam Gamgee (said a great deal)

ITEM
  One pony. Old, half-starved, "the only beast in Bree for sale this
  morning", which was true because the other ponies of Bree had all been
  taken or run off in the night, which was nobody's fault, said the vendor.

PRICE
  Twelve silver pennies.
  Market value at time of sale ...... about a third of that
  Vendor's comment .................. "take it or leave it"
  Purchaser's comment ............... not printable

COMPENSATION (separate transaction)
  Barliman Butterbur to Meriadoc Brandybuck, for the five ponies lost
  from the Pony's stable: eighteen silver pennies. Paid in full. The
  innkeeper considers this a matter of honour. He also considers it
  expensive.

AFTERCARE (filed by the purchaser's party, later)
  3018-09 to 3019-01: the pony walks to Rivendell; fattens; given a name
    ("Bill", by Sam; a better Bill than the other one).
  3019-01-13: turned loose at the West-gate of Moria, the pony not able
    to climb stairs in the dark. Sam's farewell was longer than the one
    he made to the Shire.
  3019-10-28: found back in Bree at the Pony, having found its own way,
    and returned to Sam. Four of the five lost ponies had also come back. Neither
    Butterbur nor Merry has raised the eighteen pennies since.

VENDOR STATUS
  Bill Ferny left Bree with the southerners. Last seen at the Brandywine
  Bridge gate in the Chief's service, 3019-11, and sent packing by Merry.

Receipt made out by Nob. Nob does not know his letters. Nob has signed
with a pony.
