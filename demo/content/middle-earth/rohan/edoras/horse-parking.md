# Meduseld: horse parking

- **Visitors** tie horses at the foot of the stair. *Weapons stay at the door.*
- **Wizards**: staff counts as a weapon *unless it's a walking stick* (door-ward's call).
- **Shadowfax** parks wherever he likes. Do not argue with Shadowfax.
