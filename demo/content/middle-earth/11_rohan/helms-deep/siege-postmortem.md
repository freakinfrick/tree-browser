# Postmortem: Siege of the Hornburg

**Filed by:** the Marshal of the Westfold · **Date:** 3019-03-04 (after dawn) ·
**Ticket:** HORN-0001 · **Scope:** siege of the Hornburg, 3019-03-03 through 3019-03-04 ·
**Cross-refs:** `culvert-bug.md`, `10_fangorn/huorn-migration.csv`.

## Timeline

- **3019-03-03, night** — host of Isengard arrives; rain
- **3019-03-03, midnight** — culvert breached (see `culvert-bug.md`)
- **3019-03-04, before dawn** — retreat into the keep; Gimli and Legolas start *counting*
- **3019-03-04, dawn** — charge out; a wizard arrives from the east; a forest has appeared

## The horn

The horn of Helm Hammerhand was sounded from the keep as the retreat
finished. Who sounded it is not recorded with certainty — the keeper of the
horn was among the last up the stair, and several men claimed the blast
afterward (disputed). What is not disputed is the effect: the host of
Isengard checked, and the echo of the blast in the Deep was described as
"a thing no man could meet and keep his courage" — an unnamed man of the
Westfold, at the wall, 3019-03-04. The horn is not in the battle report's
inventory; it is assumed carried out by the man who blew it, or lost in the
retreat.

## The dawn cavalry

At dawn the charge came from two sides, and neither was promised:

- **from the keep** — the King, Théoden, led the sortie with the men who had
  held the wall.
- **from the east** — Erkenbrand, Marshal of the Westfold, arrived with
  the riders of the Westfold, and Gandalf the White rode beside him on
  Shadowfax. Gandalf had said he would return "ere the sun rises", and did —
  Gandalf, at the Deeping Wall, 3019-03-04.

## The forest

A wood stood in the Deeping Coomb at dawn where no wood had been the night
before. It was the Huorns, come down from Fangorn; the host of Isengard that
fled into the coomb never came out. See `10_fangorn/huorn-migration.csv` for
the count the keep later attempted.

## What went well
- the charge
- the wizard (on time, exactly as promised)
- the forest (unplanned; very effective)

## What went badly
- the wall
- the tally: Gimli 42, Legolas 41 (disputed)
