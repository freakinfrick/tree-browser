# Narsil, reforged as Andúril

**Asset record** · **Filed by:** the smiths of Rivendell · **Date:** 3018 ·
**Ticket:** RIV-0002 · **Cross-ref:** `05_rivendell/elrond-notes.md`.

**Original asset:** Narsil, the sword that cut the Ring from Sauron's hand
(Second Age, end). Broken on the field of the Last Alliance.

**Status:** reforged, 3018, by the smiths of Rivendell ·
**Name:** Andúril, *Flame of the West* ·
**Carried by:** Aragorn, son of Arathorn, heir of Isildur.

## Change log

- **Second Age (end)** — Narsil breaks. Elendil falls. Isildur keeps the shards.
- **Third Age 3018** — the shards reforged in Rivendell (the smiths said
  "two weeks"; Elrond's notes say they always say that).
- **Third Age 3018, Council** — shown to Boromir, who is told the sword
  answers to one man.
- **Third Age 3019, Paths of the Dead** — raised at the stone of Erech as
  proof that the heir has come to collect.
- **Third Age 3019, Pelennor** — "Andúril! Andúril for the Dúnedain!"
  The line still rings in the ear of everyone who heard it.

## Sheath note

The blade has no warranty against the Dead; it is not the blade that holds
the Dead. It is the *name* it was carried under.

## The name, and the runes

"Flame of the West" is the rendering of the name; the smiths stamped the
blade with seven stars and a rayed crown, and cut runes down the length of
it. The runes read, in the old tongue: "Nányë Andúril i né Narsil i macil
Elendilo. Lercuvanten i máli Mordórëo." — *I am Andúril, that was Narsil,
the sword of Elendil; the thralls of Mordor shall flee from me.* The smiths
of Rivendell cut the runes but did not promise the flight; that part was
left to the hand that held it. (`05_rivendell/elrond-notes.md` records the
smiths' estimate of "two weeks", and Elrond's dry note that they always say
that.)
