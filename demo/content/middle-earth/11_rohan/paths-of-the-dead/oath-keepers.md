# The Paths of the Dead: oath-keepers

**Filed by:** the heir's herald, at the stone of Erech · **Date:** 3019-03-10 ·
**Ticket:** DUN-0001 · **Cross-ref:** `14_gondor/pelennor-fields/battle-report.md`.

The people of the Mountains betrayed Isildur long ago and were cursed to
walk without rest until their oath was kept. Aragorn, as Isildur's heir,
called the debt due.

## The broken oath

In the Dark Years the Men of the Mountains worshipped Sauron, and when the
Last Alliance was mustered they swore to Isildur at the stone of Erech that
they would fight beside him. When the war came they would not come down from
the hills. Isildur laid the curse upon them then, in the hearing of all who
stood there:

> "Thou shalt be the last king. And if the West prove mightier than thy
> Black Master, this curse I lay upon thee and thy folk: to rest never until
> your oath is fulfilled." — Isildur, at the stone of Erech, Second Age (end).

They hid until they were dead, and dead they could not hide. The oath is the
one contract in the realm that does not lapse at death; the King's heir is
the only signatory who can sign it closed.

## The stone of Erech

- where: at the black stone, at the entrance to the Paths.
- who must answer: the oath-breakers, and only them.
- terms: one battle, then release.

## Mustering

| cohort | count | condition | discharged |
|---|---|---|---|
| the Dead of the Dwimorberg | a host, uncounted | cannot be killed; cannot kill with arms — fear does the work | after the Pelennor |
| the Corsair fleet | many ships | captured at Pelargir, crewed by ghosts | after the Pelennor |
| the King of the Dead | 1 | answered, grudgingly | released with the rest |

## What the Dead did

- swept the Corsairs from the southern river.
- arrived at the Pelennor in black ships flying the enemy's flag (a
  surprise that ended the battle).
- asked, once, to be released. The King said: keep your oath, then go.

> They were not an army. They were a *liability* called in — a debt, collected
> at the exact moment the account was empty.

## The release

When the King of the Dead asked whether they were now released, the answer
was recorded as the last word spoken to them: "Keep your oath, and go." —
Aragorn son of Arathorn, on the Pelennor, 3019-03-15. The Dead dispersed
then, and are not recorded again. The host that fought at the Pelennor is
accounted in `14_gondor/pelennor-fields/battle-report.md`, where the black
ships are listed among the arrivals that ended the day.
