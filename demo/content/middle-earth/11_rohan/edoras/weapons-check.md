# Policy: weapons at the doors of Meduseld

**Policy:** MED-SEC-001 · **Owner:** Háma, Doorward of Théoden · **Effective:**
by order of the king (drafted, in practice, by his counsellor) · **Last
exercised:** TA 3019-03-02

## Rule

No guest enters the Golden Hall bearing arms. Weapons are left at the door
with the Doorward and collected on the way out.

## Exceptions log, 3019-03-02

| guest | weapon | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Aragorn | Andúril | surrendered after a speech about whose sword it is; "whoever touches it save me, death" |
| Legolas | bow, knives | surrendered without comment |
| Gimli | axe | surrendered, to be collected "the moment it is needed" |
| Gandalf | staff | **retained.** Háma: "the staff of an old man" is not a weapon. Doorward's judgement. |

## Post-incident review

The exception for the staff was the most consequential access decision of the
year. Within the hour the staff had been used to break Saruman's hold on the
king and to put Gríma Wormtongue on the floor (`grima-wormtongue.md`).

Finding: the policy classified weapons by shape. It should classify them by
who is holding them. No action taken; the outcome was good.
