# Meduseld: horse parking

- **Visitors** tie horses at the foot of the stair. *Weapons stay at the door.*
- **Wizards**: staff counts as a weapon *unless it's a walking stick* (door-ward's call).
- **Shadowfax** parks wherever he likes. Do not argue with Shadowfax.

| bay | assigned to |
|---|---|
| 1 | Théoden (Snowmane) |
| 2 | Éomer (Firefoot) |
| 3 | Éowyn (Windfola) |
| 4 | *Gríma: revoked* |

## Shadowfax, the Lord of all horses

Not a bay. Bay assignment was never offered and would have been declined.
Shadowfax is a Mearas, of the line the Elves once rode, and no man of Rohan
has ever put a bridle on him and kept it. Théoden gave him to Gandalf
outright at the restoration, calling him "a gift such as only a king could
make, the lord of all horses" — Théoden King, Meduseld, 3019-03-02. He comes
when the wizard whistles, and goes where he will.

## Note on bay 4

Gríma's bay was revoked at the same stroke that turned him out
(`grima-wormtongue.md`). His horse was never the
problem. The man was. The stall now stands empty, in case of a guest who
rides better company.
