# Gríma Wormtongue: personnel file

**Role:** chief counsellor to Théoden (self-appointed)
**Actual employer:** Saruman of Isengard
**Status:** terminated 3019, by his own hand, on the doorstep of Bag End.

**Filed by:** the King's own hand, Meduseld · **Date:** 3019-03-02 ·
**Ticket:** MED-0007 (counsellor, defect) · **Cross-refs:** `horse-parking.md`,
`eomer-attendance.csv`, `02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`.

## On the name

"Wormtongue" was not self-chosen. It was the King's own word, spoken in
bitterness at Meduseld when the spell broke: "Down, snake! ... Wormtongue,
get you hence" — Théoden King, Meduseld, 3019-03-02. Gandalf had used it
openly a moment before: "the wise speak only of what they know, Gríma son of
Gálmód. You have become a witless worm" — Gandalf the White, Meduseld,
3019-03-02. Gríma answered neither, only crawled. Both attributions are
recorded in the hall's own book, and are not in dispute.

## Duties performed

- whispered in the King's ear until the King believed himself old and done.
  A sample, preserved in the hall book: "my lord, you are not yourself. Let
  me bear your burdens a little longer, and spare you the care of them" —
  Gríma, Meduseld, undated (before 3019-03-02).
- fed Saruman everything: the muster, the walls, the culvert
  (`11_rohan/helms-deep/culvert-bug.md` notes he read the architecture docs).
- had Éomer arrested on false advice (`eomer-attendance.csv`, the cell row).
- courted Éowyn. Was refused. This did not improve his judgement.

## Later, in the Shire

After the King cast him out, Gríma followed Saruman — now calling himself
"Sharkey" — into the Shire (`02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`).
There Saruman shortened him further, and called him only "Worm" — Saruman,
Bag End, 3019-11. The epithet the King once spat had, by then, become the
kindest name he was ever called.

## Removal

- Gandalf the White exposed him; Théoden, restored, cast him out.
- His parking bay at Meduseld: `revoked` (`horse-parking.md`, bay 4).

## End

- Followed Saruman into the Shire (`02_the-shire/scouring-of-the-shire/sharkey.md`).
- When Saruman fell to spite, Gríma cut his throat on the doorstep of Bag
  End, and was shot dead by the hobbits' archers a moment later.

## Lesson

A man who serves two masters serves neither, and in the end is paid by both
— badly.
