**Kind:** the kingdom of the Horse-lords, allies of Gondor by oath.
**Position:** the plains north of the White Mountains, between the Isen and
the Anduin.
**Who lives here:** the Rohirrim; the Mearas; and, under the Dwimorberg, the
Dead.

## What it is

Rohan is the Riddermark, the green land granted to **Eorl the Young** by the
Steward Cirion of Gondor in **TA 2510**, in return for riding to Gondor's
aid at the Field of Celebrant — the **Oath of Eorl**, which the Rohirrim keep to
this day (`14_gondor/`). Its capital is **Edoras**, with the Golden Hall of
Meduseld on the hill; its horses are the **Mearas**, descended from Eorl's own
Felaróf, and the lord of them all is Shadowfax (`edoras/horse-parking.md`).

## In the war

- **TA 3019-03-02.** Théoden is healed of Saruman's whisperer: Gríma is
  expelled, and the king rides again (`edoras/grima-wormtongue.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-03/04.** The **Battle of the Hornburg** at Helm's Deep: the
  Deeping Wall is breached at the culvert (a hole shipped by design,
  `helms-deep/culvert-bug.md`), the keep holds, and at dawn the Huorns and
  the charge of Théoden break the siege (`helms-deep/siege-postmortem.md`,
  `helms-deep/wall-repairs.rs`).
- **TA 3019-03-10.** Aragorn takes the **Paths of the Dead** under the
  mountain and summons the Oathbreakers — the Dead Men of Dunharrow, cursed
  by Isildur — to fulfil their oath (`paths-of-the-dead/oath-keepers.md`,
  `paths-of-the-dead/anduril.md`).
- **TA 3019-03-15.** Théoden falls on the Pelennor, and Éowyn slays the
  Witch-king (`14_gondor/pelennor-fields/eowyn-vs-witchking.md`).
