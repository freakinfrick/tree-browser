# The Downfall of Númenor

**Subject:** the island kingdom of the Dúnedain, and its end
**Shelf:** history, Second Age · **Warning:** do not read to the children.
**Filed by:** the Rivendell library, from the account of Elendil's house ·
**Date of the Downfall:** SA 3319 · **Date of the sailing:** SA 3318

## What Númenor was

- the great island given to the Edain (the Men who fought the first Enemy),
  west of Middle-earth, in the sea.
- its kings grew proud, its mariners grew great, and in time it reached for
  the one thing it was forbidden: the Undying Lands.

## The fall

- Ar-Pharazôn, the last king, took Sauron prisoner in the war of SA 3262 —
  and brought him home as a trophy. Sauron, in chains, talked his way from
  prisoner to counsellor to priest of the dark.
- The king built the greatest fleet ever assembled and sailed West in SA 3318
  to take immortality by force.
- The world was changed: the fleet was swallowed, the island was drowned in
  SA 3319, and the world was made round. Aman was taken away.

## The survivors

- the Faithful, Elendil and his sons Isildur and Anárion, sailed east before
  the Downfall and founded Arnor and Gondor. Elendil landed in the north and
  became High King; Isildur and Anárion ruled Gondor in the south, and their
  line runs, sixty-odd generations later, to Aragorn (`rohan/paths-of-the-dead/anduril.md`,
  `gondor/minas-tirith/coronation.md`).

## The cargo (addendum: what came ashore with them)

The Faithful did not flee empty-handed. The Elves had given Amandil's house
seven palantíri — the seeing-stones — and Elendil brought them east as the
one inheritance the sea could not sink. For their present condition, see
`palantiri/registry.md`. The master stone, the eighth, stayed behind in
Avallónë and now shows nothing but the West, which is the correct thing for
it to show.

## Lesson

Sauron was never defeated by force. He was defeated by being *invited in* —
and the one time someone invited him in with chains, he owned the house
within a generation.
