Five were sent from the West to contest the power of Sauron. They were not
to match force with force, but to teach, to encourage, and to resist.

## Names

The five were sent without names of their own; the peoples of Middle-earth
named them. Curumo the White is Curunír to the Elves, Saruman to Men; Olórin
is Mithrandir to the Elves and Men of the South, Gandalf in the North,
Tharkûn to the Dwarves; Aiwendil is Radagast. The two who went East (Alatar
and Pallando) kept their Quenya names, because no one who met them wrote
anything down. (disputed — see the Blue wizards note.)

## Notes

- Only one of the five (Gandalf) stayed true and saw it through. The order's
  success rate is therefore 20%, which the White Council does not put on
  slides.
- The two Blue wizards went East and vanish from the record. Historians
  disagree about whether they failed, succeeded strangely, or founded
  something nobody got the memo about. The record of their assignment —
  "to the East, beyond" — is the last entry, and it is unsigned.
- Radagast did not fail; he simply preferred animals to councils — which,
  given the councils, is a defensible position.
- Saruman (Curunír) was the head of the order and the first to fall. The
  White Council opened his file, marked it *defect*, and then spent an
  unreasonably long time debating whether "defect" or "turn" was the kinder
  word (`../../isengard/orthanc/`). The word settled on was "defected".
- Gandalf (Olórin) is the one entry with two statuses. The first line read
  *Grey, deceased — Moria, TA 3019-01-15*
  (`../../moria/balrog-incident-report.md`); it was reopened and corrected to
  *White, rebooted — TA 3019-03-01* (see `../../fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log`).
  The White Council asks that the word "deceased" not be used of a wizard who
  is sitting in the room.

## Addendum (Clerk's aside)

The order was five against the Nine and the One, sent in hope rather than
force. Its one success looked, from outside, like a grey man on a grey horse
arriving precisely when he meant to (`../../fangorn/gandalf-reboot.log`).
The Clerk was asked to remove this note and did not.
