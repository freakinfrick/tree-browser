# Incident: the Bonfire Glade (retrospective)

**Filed by:** Meriadoc Brandybuck, of Brandy Hall · **Date filed:**
3018-09-26, in the Glade itself, "while we still knew where the Glade was"
**Incident date:** "a long time ago" (Brandy Hall's books give no year)
**Incident ID:** BONFIRE-OLD · **Severity:** never closed, from the trees' side
**Refs:** `high-hay-maintenance.log`, `../old-man-willow.md`, `../README.md`

## What happened

Long ago the trees of the Old Forest came up against the Hedge, planted
themselves close, and leaned over it. The Bucklanders went into the Forest,
cut down hundreds of trees, and made a great bonfire. They burned a wide
ring of ground inside the Forest, and the trees drew back from the Hedge.

## What remains

A glade, open to the sky, with thin grass and a few weeds. Nothing tall
grows on it. It is the one bright place near the Forest's edge, and the
path to it is the only path the Forest does not shift.

The trees remember. Merry's account: "They're not friendly to Bucklanders,
and they have long memories." The four hobbits reached the Glade on the
morning of 3018-09-26 and lost the path on the far side of it within the
hour. Every path after that bent south and east, down to the Withywindle.

## Root cause analysis

| factor | Bucklanders' view | the Forest's view |
|---|---|---|
| trees leaned on the Hedge | encroachment | reaching for the light |
| a clearance was cut | fire safety | a massacre |
| the ring stayed bare | good ashes, poor soil | nothing agrees to grow there |
| paths shift | trick of the light | directing visitors, for their own good |

## Lessons

- A clearance is a lasting change to a very old system. Old systems log
  everything, forget nothing, and do not accept postmortems they did not
  write.
- The Glade is the one place the Forest will let you stand. It is not an
  apology. It is a scar the trees leave open so everyone can see it.
