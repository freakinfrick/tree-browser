## What it is

The Old Forest is the last unmanaged fragment of the great woods that once
covered all Eriador. Its trees are awake and resentful of the axe; the paths
shift; strangers who enter are shepherded — or swallowed. At its center is
the house of **Tom Bombadil**, who is older than the trees and answers to no
one (`tom-bombadil.md`).

## History

- **Since the First Age.** The trees have been here, and Tom remembers the
  first raindrop and the first acorn; the Forest is a relic, not a realm.
- **TA 3018-09-26.** Frodo, Sam, Merry, and Pippin enter to avoid the Black
  Riders and are almost lost: **Old Man Willow** swallows Merry, and a
  barrow-wight takes all four in the Barrow-downs. Tom rescues them twice,
  both times by song (`old-man-willow.md`, `barrow-downs/barrow-wight-report.md`).
- **The barrow-blades.** From the wight's hoard Tom hands each hobbit a
  Dúnedain blade of Westernesse, "wound about with spells for the bane of
  Mordor." One of these, wielded by Merry, will later undo the Witch-king
  (`14_gondor/pelennor-fields/eowyn-vs-witchking.md`).

## The Barrow-downs

`barrow-downs/` holds the Tyrn Gorthad, the burial mounds of the Men of the
North Kingdom. The wights that haunt them were the first dead the hobbits
met — and the first they had to be sung out of.

## The Ring test

At the Council, the question was raised: leave the Ring with Tom. The answer,
recorded in `tom-bombadil.md`: he would *forget* it, the way you forget a
button, and it would fall back into the world. The safest place in
Middle-earth is also the one that cannot be trusted with anything that
matters.
