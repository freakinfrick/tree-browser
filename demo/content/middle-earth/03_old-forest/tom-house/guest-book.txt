HOUSE RULES (Goldberry's hand)
  1. Wipe your feet; the floors are washed with rain.
  2. Nobody leaves hungry. Nobody leaves before Tom finishes the song.
  3. The dreams you have in this house are not our business. Please do not
     tell us about them at breakfast unless they are about ponies.
  4. Nothing is kept here that is not freely given. That includes the Ring.
